Brand Video Production. Three Modes.

Studio. Location. AIGC. One creative direction.

Most studios sell you one model dressed up as a methodology. We run three. The brief picks which one fits.

A beauty hero needs a controlled set. A travel campaign needs scouts and fixers. A fifty-market rollout needs an AIGC pipeline. Brands almost never need just one. We deliver all three without dropping a frame.

The work, defined

Three lanes, one creative direction.

Coverage

Studio shooting.

The studio, the crew, and the room that knows your product.

A controlled studio is still the right answer for a big share of brand work. Beauty textures, fashion editorial, packshots, food and drink, dialogue spots, product hero films all do better on a fixed set with calibrated light and a crew that knows the room.

In-house, end to end: director, DOP, gaffer and lighting, key grip, art director, stylist, prop master, hair and makeup, on-set DIT, editor and retoucher. Current-generation cinema cameras, full grip and lighting package, motion-control rig for product turntables. Same team storyboards, shoots and edits. No handoff, no quality drift.

In scope

  • Product hero films
  • Packshots & 360 turntables
  • Beauty & skincare textures
  • Macro & slow-motion
  • Fashion on talent
  • Food & beverage
  • Short-form social ads
  • eCommerce demos
  • Spokesperson content
  • Voice-over & ADR

Coverage

Location shooting.

Real places, real talent, real stories. We scout, we permit, we crew, we ship the masters.

When context is the message, no studio set replaces a real location. Travel and hospitality, automotive, large-format consumer goods, documentary, lifestyle, culture stories. The team handles the full chain: scouting, permits, casting, talent contracts, fixers and local crew, transport and logistics, insurance, weather backup plans, customs paperwork.

We've shot across mainland China, Hong Kong, Southeast Asia, Europe and the Middle East. Our fixer network means we land in a market with people who already know the city, the rules and where the best light falls at five in the afternoon. Director and DOP fly with the production. Treatment and shot list locked before wheels up.

In scope

  • Brand films & TVC
  • Travel & hospitality
  • Automotive
  • Large-format product
  • Documentary & testimonial
  • Lifestyle & culture
  • Fashion editorial
  • UGC with real talent
  • Multi-market shoots
  • Fixer network

Coverage

AIGC video production.

When the shot is impossible, the volume is too high, or the deadline is yesterday.

AIGC video has flipped the economics of brand production over the last eighteen months. Impossible environments. Brand ambassadors placed in cities they've never visited. Fifty-market localization with lip-sync in every language. Fifteen creative concepts A/B tested in a single week. Seasonal reshoots in days.

Model-agnostic by design. Veo 3.1 for native 48kHz audio and cinematic brand work, Kling 3.0 for high-motion sequences and the longest extendable output window, Seedance 2.0 for phoneme-level lip-sync across eight-plus languages, Runway Gen-4.5 for granular camera control, Wan 2.6 and Hunyuan for open-source fine-tuning on private infrastructure, Hailuo and Pika for expressive social motion at speed. ComfyUI for LoRA training and structural guidance. The model choice is a creative decision per shot, not a vendor lock-in.

Hybrid production

SIRL plus AIGC.

The strongest campaigns we ship in 2026 are rarely pure single-mode. They live in the hybrid zone, where live shooting captures what only a camera can capture and AIGC multiplies it across markets, formats and seasons.

Shoot the product hero in studio, then drop it into AIGC environments across a dozen markets. Film the brand ambassador once on location, then generate every regional variation, every seasonal reshoot, every platform cutdown without booking the talent again. The shoot day stays small. The output multiplies. The budget mostly does not have to.

  • Private GPU infra
  • NDA & DPA
  • EU AI Act
  • Data sovereignty

Everything runs on private infrastructure with hosting in mainland China, Hong Kong, the EU or the US depending on your data sovereignty position. Custom-trained LoRA models stay your property and can be exported if you ever leave.

If you only need one mode, that's also fine. We pick up where you need us and step back where you don't.

How a project runs

Same discipline,
every mode.

Studio, location, AIGC and hybrid all follow the same process. The mode changes; the discipline stays the same. The work is won in the treatment, not on the shoot day.

First two steps are where it is won or lost. The rest is execution.

Where it is won

Discovery and brief.

One working session to lock the goal, audience, channels, brand truth and no-go zones. Output is a one-page strategic brief.

Where it is won

Treatment and shooting board. The step that matters most.

Visual references locked, tone defined, shot list drafted, AIGC model selection made, talent and location options proposed. This is where a campaign becomes ownable.

The rest is execution

Pre-production.

Casting, scouting, custom LoRA training if AIGC is in scope, prop and set planning, scripting, legal clearances, permits, technical pre-light.

The rest is execution

Production. Shoot day or AIGC sprint.

Director on set or directing the pipeline. Live colour reference, live client review, same-day rushes.

The rest is execution

Post-production and delivery.

Edit, colour grade, sound design, music, voice-over, motion graphics, subtitles, multi-language adaptation, platform reformatting. Every format the campaign needs.
