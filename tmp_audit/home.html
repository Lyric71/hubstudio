Who we are

The studios shipping hundreds of assets a month for global brands now were not built for that volume. We were.

hubStudio is a full production house, headquartered in Hong Kong with studios in Shanghai, Changsha, and the Philippines. We shoot, we direct, we retouch, we edit, the same way a traditional studio does. What sits on top is a generation layer most studios have not built yet: dedicated GPU infrastructure, brand-trained models, and pipelines our team runs every day.

That mix is where the economics change. A blended team in China, on hybrid pipelines that decide per asset whether to shoot it or generate it, brings the cost of a finalized asset down by half or more without trading off what lands on the page. The savings are real, and we pass them through.
