# Pinterest pin size for 2026: every format, from Pinterest's own pages

A Pinterest pin is 2:3, or 1000 by 1500 pixels, by Pinterest's own
recommendation. Two staples of the size guides, the "long pin" and the Idea
Pin, are not current Pinterest formats: one was never on a Pinterest page,
the other was retired in 2023.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

## Pinterest pin size in 2026: what size should a pin be?

Pinterest pin size in 2026 is 1000 by 1500 pixels at a 2:3 ratio, as a PNG
or JPEG of up to 20 MB from a computer or 32 MB in the app. Pinterest warns
that a pin taller than 2:3 might get cut off in the feed. hubStudio can make
the file; you post it to Pinterest yourself.

Reviewed October 15, 2026. Every number below was read that day on
Pinterest's own sites. The dates of the format changes come from Pinterest's
announcements. The
help pages carry no date, so each row is dated by the read. In the tables,
"Help" means Pinterest's Help Center and "Business" its business site. Where
two Pinterest pages disagree, both values are printed and each page is named.

## What are Pinterest's standard pin specs?

| What | Pinterest's spec | Source, read October 15, 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | 2:3 recommended | Help: Review ad specs; Business: Creative best practices |
| Pixels | 1000 x 1500 | Help: Review ad specs; Business: Creative best practices |
| Files | PNG or JPEG | Help: Review ad specs |
| Weight | 20 MB from desktop tools, 32 MB in the app | Help: Review ad specs; Business: Creative best practices |
| Color | Converted to 8-bit RGB JPEG; upload PNG in sRGB; no custom color profiles | Help: Review ad specs |
| Title | Up to 100 characters; the first 40 may show in feeds (30 in double-byte languages) | Help: Review ad specs |
| Description | Up to 800 characters (Help) or 500 (Business) | Help: Review ad specs; Business: Creative best practices |
| Per upload, desktop | Up to 10 images, each one its own pin | Help: Create a Pin from an image or video |

> Pinterest recommends a 2:3 aspect ratio, or 1000 by 1500 pixels, for a
> standard image pin, as a PNG or JPEG of up to 20 MB from a desktop or 32 MB
> in the app, and warns that "Pins with an aspect ratio greater than 2:3
> might get cut off in people's feeds." Read directly from the specs page,
> which carries no date.
> Source: Pinterest Help Center, "Review ad specs," read October 15, 2026.
> https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-product-specs

The specs page sits in Pinterest's business help, but it isn't only for
ads. Each format on it says whether it can run in paid campaigns, organic
impressions or both, and the help article on creating a pin sends readers
to it for "technical details."

Two lines on the specs page trip people up. The first is color: Pinterest
turns every upload into an 8-bit RGB JPEG and doesn't support custom color
profiles, so colors can shift. It asks for PNG in sRGB to avoid losing
quality.

The second is a tint. On a white or black background, Pinterest lays a
faint layer over image pins in the home feed: black at 4 percent over a
near-white pin in light mode, white at 10 percent over a near-black pin in
dark mode. It does this to every image pin, organic or paid, so in light mode a
white-background packshot never shows pure white in the feed. Video is
exempt.

On the description, Pinterest's pages split. The Help Center says 800
characters; the Business best-practices page says 500. Write to 500 and both
are satisfied. Neither shows the description in the home or search feed
anyway, though the Help Center says Pinterest reads it to decide who sees
the pin.

## What happens to a Pinterest pin taller than 2:3?

It might be cut off. That's Pinterest's whole answer: "Pins with an aspect
ratio greater than 2:3 might get cut off in people's feeds," on the specs
page, and "Larger aspect ratios may get cut off in people's feeds" on the
Business page.

Neither page gives a height where the cut starts, a pixel size for a tall
pin, or a minimum size. The 1000 by 2100 "long pin," the 1000 by 3000
"infographic pin" and the 600 by 900 minimum that size guides repeat aren't
on any Pinterest page read for this guide, and the guides that print them
cite none.

What works in production instead (our practice, not Pinterest's rule): build
the pin at 2:3, keep the headline and the product in the top two thirds, and
if a long graphic really needs the room, split it into a carousel.

## What are the Pinterest video pin specs?

| What | Standard width video | Max. width video (ads only) |
|---|---|---|
| Files | MP4, MOV or M4V; H.264 or H.265 | MP4, MOV or M4V; H.264 or H.265 |
| Weight | Up to 2GB | Up to 2GB |
| Length | 4 seconds to 15 minutes | 4 seconds to 15 minutes |
| Shape | Between 1.91:1 and 1:2; 1:1, 2:3, 4:5 or 9:16 recommended | No taller than 1:1 |
| Use | Organic or paid | Paid campaigns only |

> Pinterest's video specs take MP4, MOV or M4V files encoded in H.264 or
> H.265, up to 2GB, from 4 seconds to 15 minutes long, in any shape between
> 1:2 and 1.91:1, and recommend square or vertical (2:3, 4:5 or 9:16).
> Source: Pinterest Help Center, "Review ad specs," read October 15, 2026.
> https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-product-specs

Pinterest's video specs give shapes, not pixels. The pixel size is advice: Pinterest's creator site recommends 1080 by 1920 at 9:16 for video and
asks you to leave room at the top and bottom, where the player's control bar
and settings icon sit.

How long should a video pin run? Pinterest's own pages give three answers.

> Three Pinterest pages give three ideal video lengths: 6 to 15 seconds for a
> video ad (the Help Center's specs), 15 to 60 seconds (the Help Center's
> article on pin performance) and 45 to 90 seconds (Pinterest's creator
> site). Each is Pinterest's advice, with no method published.
> Source: Pinterest Help Center and Pinterest Create, read October 15, 2026.
> https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pin-performance-and-distribution

Our own rule of thumb, not Pinterest's: cut an ad short and let an organic
how-to run longer. Captions are the
other thing to plan for. Pinterest generates them automatically and the
creator can't edit or style them, its help center says, so words that must
read right belong in the picture.

## How many images can a Pinterest carousel have?

Two to five. Collections take more, and that's where the "up to 24" in some
size guides comes from.

| Format | Files and weight | Count | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carousel | PNG or JPEG, 20 MB per image | 2 to 5 images (2 to 10 from a catalog, sales objective only) | 1:1 or 2:3 |
| Collections | PNG or JPEG, 10MB; video hero up to 2GB, 4 s to 15 min | 1 hero, 3 or more secondary recommended, 24 at most | Hero 1:1 or 2:3; secondaries all 1:1 or all 2:3 |
| Max width collections | PNG or JPEG, 10MB; video hero up to 2GB | 1 hero, 4 or more secondary recommended | Hero 1:1 or 2:3 (video 1:1 or 16:9) |

A collection shows one main picture above three smaller ones, on mobile.
Pinterest recommends square secondaries, because anything else is cropped at
the center. All three formats can run organically or as ads, the specs page
says.

## Which Pinterest formats are retired or renamed?

| Name you'll still see | Status | Pinterest page that says so |
|---|---|---|
| Story Pins | Renamed Idea Pins, May 18, 2021 | Newsroom archive, May 18, 2021 |
| Idea Pins | Folded into one Pin format, August 25, 2023 | Pinterest Create blog, August 25, 2023 |
| Separate image, video and Idea Pin options | One Create Pin option for images, video or both | Pinterest Create blog, August 25, 2023 |
| Long pin, infographic pin | Never a Pinterest format: no Pinterest page publishes them | None of the Pinterest pages read |
| Idea ads | Still current, as a paid format | Help: Review ad specs |

> Pinterest announced on August 25, 2023 that it was bringing standard Pins
> and Idea Pins "together in a single powerhouse Pin format," with one Pin
> option for images, videos or a mix of both, and said existing pins would
> not be affected.
> Source: Pinterest Create blog, "Everything you need to know about the new
> Pin update," August 25, 2023, read October 15, 2026.
> https://create.pinterest.com/blog/new-pin-format-update/

The old Story Pins address on the Help Center now redirects to an archived
Idea Pins article that no longer loads. Size guides that list Idea Pins with
a page count and a per-page length describe a format Pinterest folded into
the standard pin in August 2023.

Idea ads catch people the other way around. The name survived, as a paid
format with its own specs, covered in the next table.

## What should you check before you post a pin?

Check the file before you hit Publish. Once a pin is live, its image, video
or audio can't be edited, Pinterest's help says; the fix is a new pin.

The Create Pin form also asks about AI. Its "Mark as AI-Modified" toggles
cover content made completely or partly with AI, and content showing an
AI-generated person.

Scheduling depends on which page you read. The help on creating and
scheduling pins says a business account can publish up to 30 days ahead,
with up to 10 pins scheduled at once. The pin performance article says two
weeks.

- **Full-screen video can be downloaded.** For 9:16 video pins, you choose
  whether people can download them; a download carries your username as a
  watermark.
- **Up to 10 at a time.** On a computer, one upload takes up to 10 images or
  10 videos, and each becomes its own pin. Mixing pictures and video in one
  pin takes the Pinterest app.

## How do you make Pinterest files in hubStudio?

hubStudio doesn't publish or schedule to Pinterest, and its editors have no
Pinterest preset. You make the file in hubStudio, download it, and pin it
yourself in Pinterest's Create Pin.

For a picture, start in the Image studio with a portrait or vertical Shape
(the choices depend on the engine). The ChatGPT Image engines also offer Tall
(1:3), which is past 2:3 and risks the cut.

Then open it in the Image editor. In Crop, pick Free and drag the box to two
wide by three tall, reading the pixel size the editor shows under the
formats. Apply the crop. In Save, choose PNG or JPG (the two files
Pinterest's pin specs list) and type 1000 in Width. The height follows,
because the proportions stay locked, and lands at 1500 on a true 2:3 crop.
Download it, or save a copy in the Assets Library. The Image editor is free
and works in your browser.

For video, the Video editor's Format panel has a Vertical frame at 9:16 and
1080 by 1920, plus Portrait (4:5) and Square (1:1). All three sit inside the
shapes Pinterest recommends. It writes an MP4, at 1080p or 720p.

When you upload a file you made with an AI engine, the Mark as AI-Modified
toggles on Pinterest's Create Pin form are the place to say so.

The Instagram post sizes guide in the platform specs library covers the
other feed most pins start from. Teams that want the pins made for them can
brief the social media design service; product pins for a store sit with the
ecommerce design service.

## FAQ

### What size should a Pinterest pin be?

Pinterest recommends 1000 by 1500 pixels, a 2:3 ratio, for a standard pin.
Upload a PNG or JPEG of up to 20 MB from a computer or 32 MB in the app.
Pinterest converts every upload to an 8-bit RGB JPEG, so it suggests PNG in
sRGB to avoid losing quality. It publishes no minimum size.

### What aspect ratio does Pinterest use?

2:3 for image pins, by Pinterest's recommendation. Carousels take 1:1 or
2:3. Video can run anywhere from 1.91:1 wide to 1:2 tall, and Pinterest
recommends square or vertical: 1:1, 2:3, 4:5 or 9:16. Its creator site
recommends 1080 by 1920 at 9:16 for video.

### What happens if my pin is too tall?

Pinterest says it might get cut off in people's feeds, and stops there: no
height where the cut starts, no size for a tall pin. The 1000 by 2100 "long
pin" that many size guides print isn't on any Pinterest page. Build at 2:3,
or split a long graphic into a carousel of two to five images.

### What are the Pinterest video pin specs?

MP4, MOV or M4V, encoded in H.264 or H.265, up to 2GB, from 4 seconds to
15 minutes, in any shape between 1.91:1 and 1:2, with square or vertical
recommended. Pinterest's pages disagree on the ideal length: 6 to 15
seconds for an ad, 15 to 60 seconds, or 45 to 90 seconds on its creator
site.

### How many images can a Pinterest carousel have?

Two to five, by Pinterest's specs, each a PNG or JPEG of up to 20 MB at 1:1
or 2:3. A carousel built from a product catalog under the sales objective
can hold 2 to 10 images and videos. The "up to 24" figure belongs to
collections, which take one hero and up to 24 smaller pictures.

### Can I schedule Pinterest pins from hubStudio?

No. hubStudio publishes to LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok (Beta) and
X, and doesn't connect to Pinterest. Make and download the file in
hubStudio, then use Pinterest's own Create Pin. A business account can
publish later from there, up to 30 days ahead by Pinterest's help on
scheduling.
