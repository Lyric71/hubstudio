# Meta and TikTok against Douyin and RedNote: the same campaign, twice

Meta and TikTok print their ad specs where anyone can read them. Douyin and
RedNote do not. Set the four side by side and a Western 9:16 cut survives the
frame. What it runs into next is a safe zone that leaks by a few dozen pixels,
and a cover nobody on the Western side asked for.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

## How do you adapt TikTok ads for Douyin?

Keep the 9:16 master and rebuild the export. On the published record the
frame survives, Meta's safe zone leaks a few dozen pixels on Douyin, a
9-second TikTok cut is too short for Douyin TopView, and RedNote needs a 3:4
cover the Western build never made. hubStudio plans that capture as a single
shoot day.

## How to read these numbers

These specs are not read from a creator backend. Douyin's creator and
advertiser surfaces serve no specification text to anyone not logged in.
Neither do RedNote's creator, advertiser and help surfaces, the help center of
its Juguang ad platform included. So no open source reaches the official value
for either app. The one readable exception, Douyin's upload API documentation,
is named below.

What follows is the most commonly published figure for each Douyin and RedNote
spec, collected across three separate collections of 12, 17 and 20 independent
sources in Chinese and English on 10 September 2026, with the number of sources
that agreed shown per row. Counts from different collections are never added
together. Rows marked consensus had broad agreement. Rows marked contested did
not, and those carry the full spread rather than a single number.

Treat this as the best available public reading, not as the rule. Platform
specs change without announcement and often without an English notice at all.
Before you build a batch to any number on this page, open your own creator
account and confirm it there. Reviewed 15 September 2026.

The Meta and TikTok half is different. Meta's Facebook Ads Guide serves one
undated page per placement under the Awareness objective, and TikTok's ads
help center prints a month on each page: auction in-feed (June 2026),
reservation in-feed (July 2025), TopView (June 2026) and creative best
practices (June 2025). Every one of them was read logged out on 10 September
2026 and again on 15 September. Those rows are primary readings for the
placement, objective or buying type named, and for nothing else. Douyin's Open
Platform documentation for its upload API is readable too, and its row says it
covers the API route only.

> RedNote's advertising help surfaces served no advertising specification text
> to an unauthenticated request on 10 September 2026, or again on 15 September.
> That covers the Juguang ad platform, its help center, a help document, its API
> documentation and the help center of its creator marketplace. Between them
> they returned page titles, a login page, a chatbot prompt, an empty-article
> notice and an API onboarding note, and no creative spec. The RedNote rows on
> this page therefore cover organic note formats only.
> Source: five direct unauthenticated fetches of RedNote-owned advertising and
> creator-marketplace surfaces, 10 September 2026 and again 15 September 2026,
> response content recorded per request. Primary observation, not a
> specification.

## Why aren't Douyin and TikTok one ad spec?

Because the apps publish differently, and where both publish the same product
the numbers differ. Both carry a TopView. They do not carry the same TopView.

> Douyin TopView runs a video of 10 to 60 seconds. The first 3 seconds play
> full screen with nothing on top, a conversion component fades in after that, and
> from the fourth second the ad sits as the first post in the feed. Four of five
> Douyin-specific sources give the 10 to 60 second window. The fifth gives 5 to
> 60, which is TikTok's published range.
> Source: 5 Douyin-specific published sources, Chinese-language, pages dated 2019
> to 2021 and 2025, collected 10 September 2026. Modal value, not a primary
> reading.

That fifth source is the pattern in miniature. TikTok's figures are easy to
find, so they get copied onto Douyin pages, and a buyer searching in English
can't tell which app a number belongs to. The white rule shows the same drift
in the other direction.

> TikTok bars plain white in the first 3 seconds of a TopView video, because its
> logo is hard to see against it. That rule belongs to TikTok. No Douyin source
> in the collection used for this page publishes it.
> Source: TikTok ads help center, TopView ad specifications, updated June 2026,
> read 10 and 15 September 2026, checked against a 17-source Douyin paid-ad
> collection of 10 September 2026. Primary reading of the TikTok page; the
> Douyin side is an absence in a counted collection.

Then there is the safe zone, which TikTok does not publish as a number at all.

> TikTok does not print a safe-zone inset on its in-feed or TopView spec pages.
> It says the safe zone shrinks as the caption gets longer and changes with
> orientation and add-ons, and it supplies template files instead. The pixel
> insets that circulate for TikTok come from other publishers, and five sets
> checked for this page did not match one another.
> Source: three TikTok ads help center specification pages read in full on 10
> and 15 September 2026, plus five third-party TikTok inset sets traced to their
> pages. Primary reading for what TikTok publishes, and an observation about the
> figures others publish.

So a producer arrives at Douyin with a TikTok template that TikTok says moves
with the caption (the downloaded file is the only way to check that half), and
a Douyin figure that no two sources agree on. The Douyin video specs and safe
zones article carries the full spread. The Douyin ad creative specs article
carries the paid formats by age of source.

## Where does a Meta-safe layout leak on Douyin and RedNote?

On the sides and at the top, by a few dozen pixels. Not at the bottom.

> Only two Chinese-language sources publish Douyin-specific safe-zone insets,
> and they do not agree. The top band appears as 220 pixels and as 15 percent
> of frame height, about 288 pixels, on two pages of the same site. The bottom
> band appears as 200, 288 and 380 pixels. No Chinese source publishes a width
> for the right-side button column at all.
> Source: 5 independent published references addressing a Douyin safe zone,
> Chinese-language, collected 10 September 2026, every inset converted to a
> 1080x1920 canvas before comparison. Range published, contested, not read from
> the platform.

Take the largest published figure on each side and Meta's percentages on the
same canvas, and the two boxes almost coincide.

> Text placed to Meta's Reels safe zone on a 1080 by 1920 frame can sit as close
> as 65 pixels to each side and 269 pixels to the top. The published Douyin
> figures, taken at their largest, keep critical content 108 pixels from each
> side and 288 from the top. That leaves a strip of about 43 pixels on each side
> and 19 at the top where a Meta-safe line falls outside the Douyin box. Meta's
> 35 percent bottom reservation clears every published Douyin bottom band.
> Source: arithmetic on Meta's Reels safe-zone percentages, read on its ads
> guide 10 and 15 September 2026, and the derived Douyin safe box from five
> Douyin sources collected 10 September 2026. Derived, not a measurement of
> either app.

Meta's own help article on the subject, which served no text on 10 September
and a full page on 15 September, adds one figure the ads guide does not.

> Meta's help article on safe zones states the rule in words: for 9:16 ads in
> Stories, Reels and Feed, keep the top, bottom and sides free of key creative
> elements, text and logos, and on screens taller than 9:16 the creative may be
> zoomed, cropping whatever sits outside. It adds one figure the ads guide
> pages do not: a Reels ad that carries a disclaimer should leave the bottom 40
> percent free.
> Source: Meta Business Help Center, About text overlays and the safe zone for
> ads in Stories and Reels, read 15 September 2026, a primary reading of that
> article only; the 14, 35 and 6 percent figures come from the ads guide pages.
> https://www.facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490

RedNote changes the shape of the question rather than the margin. Its feed
shows a 3:4 cover first, and the published accounts of how that cover is
cropped disagree with each other, so the region that survives all of them is
the center square. One box clears everything published for the four apps.

> On a 1080 by 1920 vertical master, keeping text, faces and product between 108
> and 972 pixels across and between 420 and 1248 pixels down clears Meta's Reels
> safe zone, every Douyin inset in circulation, and the center of a 3:4 RedNote
> cover above its deepest published bottom band. TikTok adds no number, because
> TikTok publishes none: check the file against its template for the caption you
> plan to run.
> Source: derived 15 September 2026 from Meta's Reels safe-zone percentages
> (ads guide, primary), a Douyin safe box from five sources, and a RedNote
> center square and 288-pixel bottom reservation from ten and eight sources,
> assuming centered cuts. A composition rule derived from published figures,
> not a platform rule.

That box is 864 by 828 pixels, smaller than any one platform asks for on its
own. Shooting once costs that margin.

## Which parts of a Western cut survive the move?

The input is a vertical ad built for Reels or TikTok in-feed: 9:16, 15 to 30
seconds, sound on, text placed to Meta's Reels safe zone, exported at Meta's
recommended 1440 by 2560 in MP4, with no separate cover. Every verdict below
is derived from the rows above. None is a platform rule, and centered cuts are
an assumption no source states.

| Asset or property | On Douyin | On RedNote | Inputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9:16 frame | Survives. Post 9:16 at 1080 by 1920, 4 of 4; in-feed 9:16, 4 of 4 | Survives as a video note. 9:16 on 5, contested on which vertical | Meta Reels page; Douyin video and ad collections; RedNote collection |
| 4:5 Facebook Feed cut, 1440 by 1800 | Fails. Not 9:16, so it needs the 9:16 master or a re-frame | Survives as a crop to a 3:4 cover. Full height keeps 1350 of the 1440 width, losing 45 pixels a side | Meta Facebook Feed page; RedNote cover, 14 of 14 |
| Text placed to Meta's Reels safe zone | Partly fails. Strips of about 43 pixels a side and 19 at the top fall outside the derived Douyin box; the bottom clears every published band | Survives on a full-card cover. Text in the top band of a centered 3:4 cut is lost under the hard 1:1 feed crop, 2 of 10, and the profile grid, 4 | Meta Reels percentages; Douyin safe-zone spread; RedNote crop accounts |
| 9-second cut, the short end of TikTok's recommendation | Survives in-feed. Fails Douyin TopView reuse, below its 10-second floor, 4 of 5 | Survives. Every published ceiling is in minutes | TikTok reservation and TopView pages; Douyin ad collection; RedNote collection |
| 15 to 30-second cut | Survives in-feed and Douyin TopView. Fails open screen, which shows a video for 5 seconds, 5 of 5 | Survives | Same |
| First 3 seconds | Douyin TopView plays seconds 0 to 3 full screen with nothing layered, 4 of 4. A slow open spends that window | Not addressed in the collection | TikTok best practices page; Douyin ad collection |
| Plain white opening | No white rule published for Douyin. The ban is TikTok TopView's own, because of its logo | Not applicable | TikTok TopView page; Douyin ad collection |
| File weight | A 4GB Meta-legal file fails TikTok's 500 MB before it reaches China. 500 MB sits above the smallest published Douyin in-feed cap, 100MB on one extract | 500 MB sits above the smallest published RedNote video cap, 50 MB on one source | Meta and TikTok pages; both China collections |
| Sound | Sound-on survives on the published record. The dated open-screen file says no sound, 2018 and 2020 sheets | Not addressed | TikTok reservation page; Douyin ad collection |
| Cover | A 9:16 frame grab serves the cover row, 2 of 2, thin | Fails. Nothing on the Meta or TikTok video pages sets a cover, and RedNote's default is 3:4 at 1080 by 1440, 14 of 14 | Meta and TikTok pages, by absence; RedNote collection |

Three of those rows decide the shoot. Duration first.

> One cut length fits every duration figure used here except Douyin's open
> screen: 10 to 60 seconds. TikTok's reservation and TikTok TopView formats stop
> at 60.
> Douyin TopView starts at 10, so a 9-second cut, the short end of TikTok's own
> recommendation, is too short to reuse there. Open screen shows a video for 5
> seconds and needs its own cut.
> Source: TikTok ads help center reservation and TopView pages read 10 and 15
> September 2026 (primary), and Douyin paid-ad figures from 5 Douyin-specific
> sources collected 10 September 2026 (modal). Derived by intersecting the
> published ranges.

Then the cover, which is where the Western build fails RedNote outright.

> Neither Meta's video ad pages nor TikTok's in-feed pages set a cover image.
> RedNote's most commonly published cover is a 3:4 frame at 1080 by 1440 pixels,
> so a Western cut arrives without the asset RedNote shows first. A centered 3:4
> cut of a 1080 by 1920 frame measures exactly 1080 by 1440, which makes the
> cover a moment to plan on set rather than a crop to find later.
> Source: Meta and TikTok specification pages read in full on 10 and 15
> September 2026 (primary, absence), and RedNote cover figures from 20 sources
> collected 10 September 2026 (modal). Derived; the fit is geometry.

Sound runs both ways, and the table already shows it: optional on Meta,
required on TikTok reservation and TikTok TopView, absent from the dated
Douyin open-screen file. One sound-on master and one silent short cut cover
all of it. The file row rests on the weakest input on the page, a RedNote cap
that a single source publishes, and the export line in the capture plan
carries that arithmetic with its label.

## Questions buyers ask about running one campaign on Meta, TikTok, Douyin and RedNote

**Can a TikTok ad run on Douyin?**

Not as the same ad. The apps run separate accounts and separate ad platforms.
The 9:16 frame carries over. What needs checking is the duration window, since
the most commonly published Douyin TopView starts at 10 seconds, the safe zone,
where no Douyin figure agrees, and the sound rule on the dated open-screen
format. Rebuild the export from the master, not from the TikTok file.

**Are Douyin and TikTok ad specs the same?**

No. TikTok publishes its specs, and its TopView runs 5 to 60 seconds. The most
commonly published Douyin TopView runs 10 to 60, on 4 of 5 Douyin-specific
sources. TikTok bans plain white in the first 3 seconds because of its logo,
and no Douyin source prints that rule. TikTok's safe zone is a downloadable
template. Douyin's is a contested set of pixel figures.

**What changes when a Meta campaign moves to RedNote?**

The cover. Meta's video ad pages set no cover. RedNote shows a 3:4 cover first,
1080 by 1440 on 13 of 20 published sources, with a center square lifted into
the profile grid. A 4:5 Feed cut crops to 3:4 with 45 pixels lost on each side.
RedNote's ad documentation is not readable logged out, so these are organic
note figures.

**How do TikTok and Douyin safe zones differ?**

TikTok publishes no inset. Its help pages say the safe zone shrinks with
caption length and supply template files instead, and every TikTok pixel figure
in circulation is third-party. Douyin has no official figure either, and the
published Chinese insets span 220 to 288 pixels at the top and 200 to 380 at
the bottom. Build to the largest, then check the TikTok template.

**How do I shoot one campaign for Western and Chinese platforms?**

Shoot one 9:16 master with critical content inside a box from 108 to 972
pixels across and 420 to 1248 down on a 1080 by 1920 frame. Plan a cut of 10
to 60 seconds and a cut of 5. Land the proposition by second 3 without a white
open, mix sound on stems, and hold a 3:4 cover moment. One shoot day, two
exports.
