# Douyin ad creative specs by format: open screen, TopView and in-feed

The pages ranking for Douyin ad specs are media-buying articles about ad types
and costs. The few that print dimensions are reprinting a spec sheet from the
end of 2020, and sometimes attaching it to the wrong format.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

## What are the Douyin TopView creative specs?

A video of 10 to 60 seconds, most commonly published, whose first 3 seconds play
full screen before it settles into the feed. That timing is well agreed. The
file specs are not published for TopView on any Douyin source at all, and the
sizes you may have been sent under that name belong to a different format.

<!-- SECTION: How to read these numbers -->

## How to read these numbers

These specs are not read from an advertiser backend. Ocean Engine, the platform
that sells Douyin ads, returns no specification text to anyone not logged in, so
no open source reaches the official value.

What follows is the most commonly published figure for each spec, collected
across 17 independent sources in Chinese and English on 10 September 2026, with
the number of sources that agreed shown per row. Rows marked consensus had broad
agreement. Rows marked contested did not, and those carry the full spread
rather than a single number.

Treat this as the best available public reading, not as the rule. Platform
specs change without announcement and often without an English notice at all.
Before you build a batch to any number on this page, open your own advertiser
account and confirm it there. Reviewed 10 September 2026.

> Ocean Engine does not publish its ad creative specs where a brand can read
> them. On 10 September 2026 its developer documentation returned a page title
> and no text, and its help center returned an empty page. The most detailed
> Douyin spec sheet anyone can open without an account is a rate card for the
> fourth quarter of 2020, valid for three months, which says its own online
> version takes precedence. Every figure below is the most commonly published
> value, not a value read from the platform.
> Source: four Ocean Engine surfaces fetched unauthenticated on 10 September
> 2026, plus 17 published sources carrying a Douyin paid-ad spec, 16
> Chinese-language and 1 English-language, counted per value. Modal values, not
> primary readings.

A three-month rate card that defers to an online version nobody outside can
read. That is the ground every Douyin ad spec in public circulation stands on.

## What are the open screen ad requirements?

Open screen is the ad that plays as the app opens, and it has the most settled
row on this page.

> Open screen timing is the one Douyin ad spec the sources agree on. A static
> image shows for 3 seconds, an animated image for 4, a video for 5. All five
> Chinese-language sources that give display times give the same three, on
> pages dated from 2018 to 2025.
> Source: 5 published sources, Chinese-language, collected 10 September 2026,
> counted per value with no contradicting source. Modal value, not a primary
> reading.

Everything else about open screen comes from spec sheets with a date on them,
and the date matters.

> The newest complete Douyin open screen spec sheet in public circulation dates
> from the fourth quarter of 2020. Between the 2018 and 2020 versions the static
> image cap rose from under 150KB to under 300KB and the largest canvas grew to
> 1080 by 2340. Nothing publicly reachable shows what changed after that.
> Source: two rate card generations, 2018 and 2020, both Chinese-language, read
> 10 September 2026. An observation about age, not a current value.

| Spec | Most published value | Spread and age | Sources agreeing | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display time | 3s image, 4s animated, 5s video | Unchanged on pages from 2018 to 2025 | 5 of 5 | consensus |
| Video file | MP4, 5 seconds, 5MB or under, silent | Both from rate cards, the newer dated 2020 | 2 of 2 | contested by age |
| Static image cap | Under 300KB | Under 150KB in 2018, under 300KB in 2020 | 2 generations | contested by age |
| Largest canvas | 1080 by 2340 | From the 2020 sheet | 1 generation | contested by age |
| Safe margins on a 1080-wide asset | Top 210, sides and bottom 120 | One source | 1 | contested |
| Title length | 9 Chinese characters or fewer | One source | 1 | contested |

The production implication follows from the display time rather than from any
dimension. A static open screen has 3 seconds and one frame. Whatever the brand
needs a viewer to take away has to be in that frame, readable at a glance, before
anyone has decided whether to tap.

## TopView, and the sizes that are not TopView's

> TopView runs a video of 10 to 60 seconds. The first 3 seconds play full
> screen with nothing on top, a conversion component fades in after that, and
> from the fourth second the ad sits as the first post in the feed. Four of five
> Douyin-specific sources give the 10 to 60 second window. The fifth gives 5 to
> 60, which is TikTok's published range.
> Source: 5 Douyin-specific published sources, Chinese-language, pages dated 2019
> to 2021 and 2025, collected 10 September 2026. Modal value, not a primary
> reading.

That hand-off is the landing behavior, and it shapes the edit. The first 3
seconds are the only unobstructed stretch. After that a conversion component
sits over the frame and the ad behaves like a feed post.

| Spec | Most published value | Spread across sources | Sources agreeing | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Duration | 10 to 60 seconds | One source gives 5 to 60, TikTok's range | 4 of 5 | consensus |
| Full-screen opening | First 3 seconds, nothing on top | None | 4 of 4 | consensus |
| Hand-off | Conversion component after 3 seconds, feed post from the fourth | None | 4 of 4 | consensus |
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 | Stated outright by two | 2 of 5 | contested |
| Resolution, file size, format, bitrate, title | Not published | No Douyin source gives any of them | 0 | not published |

The last row is the important one, and here is where the gap usually gets
filled from.

> The seven pixel sizes English guides list as TopView sizes, running from 480 by
> 800 to 1080 by 2340, are the video file set for the 5-second open screen in a
> Douyin rate card from the fourth quarter of 2020. No Chinese-language source
> attaches them to TopView. No Douyin-specific source publishes a TopView file
> size, resolution or bitrate at all.
> Source: the 2020 rate card read in full and compared with English search
> extracts on 10 September 2026, against 16 Chinese-language sources. An
> observation about provenance, not a primary reading.

And the Chinese-language search has its own substitution.

> Search in Chinese for Douyin's TopView specs and the top result is TikTok's
> own TopView help page. Its numbers are TikTok's: a 540 by 960 pixel floor, a
> 500MB cap, a 100-character title. It never mentions Douyin, and one of its
> rules, no pure white in the first 3 seconds, exists because of where TikTok
> puts its logo.
> Source: one Chinese-language query run 10 September 2026, the result read in
> full, page updated June 2026. An observation about the search results, not a
> Douyin spec.

So a TopView brief built from search results arrives with either a 2020 open
screen file list or a TikTok spec sheet attached. Neither is a Douyin TopView
spec, and the page publishes none rather than borrow one.

## What size is a Douyin in-feed ad?

A vertical 9:16 video, with the recent sources agreeing on the floor and the
older ones not.

> For in-feed video, the sources that agree are the recent ones. Pages dated 2023
> and 2024 put the vertical floor at 720 by 1280 in 9:16. Pages from 2018 to
> 2021 say 540 by 960. Duration moved the same way: 5 to 30 seconds in 2018, 5
> to 60 on four pages dated 2019 to 2023, and 4 to 300 seconds on the only page
> dated 2024.
> Source: 7 published sources, Chinese-language, collected 10 September 2026,
> grouped by publication date before counting. Modal values, not primary
> readings.

> No file size cap for in-feed video settles. It appears as 500MB on two
> sources, 1,000MB on the only 2024 page, and 100MB on one more. A file under
> 100MB clears every figure in circulation.
> Source: 4 published sources, Chinese-language, collected 10 September 2026,
> the full spread reported. Modal values, not primary readings; the last
> sentence is reasoning from the smallest published figure.

| Spec | Most published value | Spread and age | Sources agreeing | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orientation | 9:16 vertical, 16:9 also accepted | None | 4 of 4 | consensus |
| Resolution floor | 720 by 1280 | 540 by 960 on pages from 2018 to 2021 | 3 of 3 recent | consensus on recent pages |
| Format | MP4 | None | 3 of 3 | consensus |
| Duration | 5 to 60 seconds | 4 to 300 seconds on the only 2024 page | 4 pages to 2023 | contested by age |
| File size cap | 500MB | 1,000MB and 100MB, one source each | 2 of 4 | contested |
| Bitrate floor and resolution ceiling | 516kbps, 1440 by 2560 | Two sources each | 2 of 2 each | contested |
| Title length | No mode | 64, 55 and 30 characters, one source each | 1 each | contested |
| Source name field | 20 characters | None | 2 of 2 | contested, too thin |

Export under 100MB at 720 by 1280 or above and you clear every file and
resolution figure in circulation. Duration is the row to confirm in the account,
since the single 2024 page and the four older ones point in different
directions.

## Search ads

> Douyin search ads publish no creative spec of their own on any source found.
> On Douyin they run as video, with one ad mixed into results positions 2 to 10
> and the first position held for an exact brand-name search.
> Source: 2 published sources, Chinese-language, collected 10 September 2026.
> Modal value, not a primary reading, and an absence stated with its search date.

No separate spec means the in-feed video figures are the only published guide,
which is a reasonable working assumption to confirm rather than a rule.

## Why was my Douyin ad creative rejected?

No rejection notice was obtained for this page, and no source ranks why Douyin
ad creative gets rejected, so there is no list of top reasons to give. What has
been reported is how rejections now arrive.

> Rejection notices have become more specific. Since an AI review upgrade
> reported for October 2025, a rejected video is flagged at the second, or the
> line of dialogue, where the problem sits, rather than rejected as a whole.
> Source: 2 published sources, Chinese-language, one dated 2026, collected 10
> September 2026. Modal value, not a primary reading.

That changes the fix more than the cause. A flag at a timestamp means a recut of
a few seconds rather than a new edit, which is worth building into the schedule:
keep the project file open and the source footage to hand until review clears.

## Where this leaves a brief

Three rows are solid enough to build to: open screen display times, the TopView
3-second opening and hand-off, and the in-feed 9:16 frame at 720 by 1280 or
above. Everything else is either dated, contested, or not published, and this
page labels which.

## Questions about Douyin ad specs

**What are the Douyin TopView creative specs?**

The most commonly published TopView duration is 10 to 60 seconds, on four of five
Douyin sources, with the first 3 seconds full screen and the ad becoming a feed
post from the fourth second. No Douyin source publishes a TopView resolution, file
size, format or bitrate, and the sizes often circulated under that name are a
2020 open screen file set.

**What size is a Douyin in-feed ad?**

A 9:16 vertical video, with 16:9 also accepted. Pages dated 2023 and 2024 put the
floor at 720 by 1280, while older pages say 540 by 960. File caps are unsettled at
100MB to 1,000MB, so a file under 100MB clears every published figure. Confirm in
your advertiser account before exporting a batch.

**What are the open screen ad requirements?**

Display times are agreed on all five sources giving them: 3 seconds for a static
image, 4 for an animated image, 5 for video. The most detailed public sheet dates
from late 2020, giving a 5-second silent MP4 at 5MB or under and a static image
cap under 300KB. Treat those figures as dated rather than current.

**Why was my Douyin ad creative rejected?**

No source ranks rejection reasons, so there is no published top list. What has
been reported is the format of the notice: since an AI review upgrade reported
for October 2025, a rejected video is flagged at the second or line of dialogue
where the problem sits, which usually means a short recut rather than a new edit.

**How long can a Douyin ad video be?**

It depends on the format. TopView is most commonly published at 10 to 60 seconds.
In-feed video appears as 5 to 60 seconds on four pages dated to 2023 and 4 to 300
seconds on the only 2024 page. Open screen video runs 5 seconds. Confirm the
current limits in your advertiser account.
