# What a week of social content really costs: in-house, freelance, agency or an app

A week of social content costs about $2,200 in the US if one salaried
marketer at the national median makes it, employer costs included, and
anywhere from under $100 to more than $5,000 if an agency does. The spread isn't noise. Each route charges for something
different, and the costs nobody quotes (review hours above all) stay with
you whichever route you pick.

## What is the cost of social media content creation for one week?

For the week defined below, ten posts on four networks, the cost of social
media content creation runs about $2,200 for one loaded US marketing
specialist, $430 to $560 for each US freelance day, and $93 to $5,400 at
published agency per-post prices. In an app like hubStudio you pay per run
from a prepaid balance, with every price shown first.

| Route                    | What you pay for                                | Fixed or variable      | Hidden costs that stay with you                        | Who carries rework                   |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| In-house hire            | A person's time, every week, busy or not        | Fixed                  | Recruiting, tools, stock, a manager's review hours     | You: rework is paid time             |
| Freelance                | Hours or days, quoted per job                   | Variable               | Briefing, review hours, tools you supply               | You, past the agreed rounds          |

## What exactly is in the week we priced?

Most cost guides compare routes that produce different things. This page
fixes the output first, so every number below buys the same week.

| Item          | The week                                         |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Posts         | 10, captions written                             |
| Networks      | 4: LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok         |
| Still visuals | 8 posts with a designed picture or carousel      |
| Video         | 2 short vertical videos, under 30 seconds        |
| Approval      | One review round per post by a manager or client |
| Publishing    | Scheduled or posted on each network              |

That's a modest week: two posts a working day, spread across four
networks. It isn't a campaign launch. Nothing here includes paid media,
which every route bills separately. Every figure on this page was read at
its source on October 14, 2026.

## What does an in-house week cost?

In-house, you don't buy ten posts. You buy a person's week, and the week
costs the same whether it produces ten posts or four.

The closest occupation in US wage statistics is the marketing specialist.
Designers and video editors sit in their own series.

> At the May 2025 national median, a US marketing specialist earned $78,760
> a year, or $37.87 an hour; the 10th percentile earned $43,390 and the 90th
> $155,480.
> Source: US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage
> Statistics, May 2025, a national survey of employers that excludes the
> self-employed.

Salary is not the cost. An employer also pays for benefits, payroll taxes
and paid leave, and the federal series that measures them puts the total
at about 1.43 times the wage.

> Private employers paid $46.89 an hour worked in June 2026, of which $32.82
> was wages and salaries, so total compensation ran about 1.43 times the
> wage.
> Source: US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employer Costs for Employee
> Compensation, June 2026, released September 2026, a national establishment
> survey.

The method is two steps: the annual median times 1.43, divided by 52 weeks.

| US role, May 2025 median                      | Annual wage         | Loaded weekly cost, rounded |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Marketing specialist                          | $78,760             | $2,164                      |
| Graphic designer                              | $62,960             | $1,730                      |
| Public relations specialist                   | $74,750             | $2,054                      |
| Film and video editor                         | $75,420             | $2,072                      |
| Marketing specialist, 10th to 90th percentile | $43,390 to $155,480 | $1,192 to $4,272            |

One specialist can make the defined week alone if the visuals stay simple.
Add a designer for the carousels and the video edits and the week is about
$3,894, before a single tool or stock license.

Europe runs on the same arithmetic with its own statistics. Germany
publishes occupation medians, and Eurostat publishes the load.

> German employees in advertising and marketing earned a median 64,923 euros
> in 2025, full time, bonuses included; designers at specialist level 49,028
> euros; public relations staff 73,562 euros.
> Source: Destatis, the German federal statistics office, earnings survey
> 2025, a mandatory survey of up to 58,000 establishments.

> Germany's hourly labor cost was 45.0 euros in 2025, of which 34.5 euros
> was wages and salaries, a load of about 1.30. France's load was about 1.48,
> with the EU's highest non-wage share at 32.3 percent.
> Source: Eurostat, labour cost levels 2025, published March and April 2026,
> enterprises with 10 or more employees.

At those figures a German marketing employee costs about 1,629 euros a week,
a design specialist about 1,230 euros, and a public relations employee about
1,845 euros. This page applies no load to the UK figures, so they are pay
before employer costs.

> Full-time UK marketing associate professionals earned a median 33,412
> pounds in April 2025; graphic and multimedia designers 32,510 pounds;
> public relations professionals 37,563 pounds.
> Source: Office for National Statistics, Annual Survey of Hours and
> Earnings 2025, provisional, based on a 1 percent sample of employees.

That's about 643, 625 and 722 pounds a week in pay alone. In every market
the in-house week is the cheapest per post only when the person is fully
used, and the most expensive when they aren't. The in-house studio or
outsourced production analysis on this site works that utilization question
through for a full creative team.

## What do freelancers charge for social content?

No official series covers freelancers: the US, UK and German wage
statistics count employees only. So the rates below come from published
surveys and booking data, each with its sample.

> US freelance social media managers billed an average $54 an hour, graphic
> designers $57, motion graphics designers $64 and videographers $70, on
> bookings made in 2024 and 2025.
> Source: a freelance contractor marketplace's 2026 rates report, drawn from
> more than 182,000 of its own booking and rate data points; a market claim,
> since it sells the contractors it prices.

The same report gives UK day rates of 326 pounds for a social media manager,
348 pounds for a graphic designer and 385 pounds for a motion graphics
designer. For writing, a survey with a stated sample puts the UK day a
little higher.

> The median UK freelance copywriter's day rate was 440 pounds in 2026, up
> from 430 pounds a year earlier.
> Source: a UK copywriters' network survey, fieldwork February 2026, 338
> freelancers answering the question.

> German marketing freelancers averaged 86.70 euros an hour in early 2026; a
> second survey put marketing and communication at 94 euros and design and
> media at 89 euros.
> Source: two project marketplaces' 2026 freelancer surveys, of more than
> 3,300 and 1,310 freelancers; market claims.

The week's freelance bill is the day rate times the days the week takes.
That second number is yours. No published measure of hours per social post
carries a method; the figures that circulate come from sellers estimating
their own product.

| One freelance day, 8 hours, rounded | US   | UK         | Germany                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ---- | ---------- | ---------------------------- |
| Social media manager                | $432 | 326 pounds | 694 euros (marketing)        |
| Graphic designer                    | $456 | 348 pounds | 712 euros (design and media) |
| Motion graphics designer            | $512 | 385 pounds | not published                |
| Videographer                        | $560 | 427 pounds | not published                |

Say the defined week takes two days of a social media manager and one of a
designer. That's about $1,320 in the US. If it takes three days of each,
it's about $2,664. Swap in your own days; the rate is the only published
part. Neither figure covers the stock licenses, the tools a freelancer
expects you to provide, or the hours your manager spends briefing and
reviewing.

## What do agencies charge for social media content?

Agencies sell a monthly tier. The ones that publish prices print a post
count, a number of networks and what's excluded.

> Agencies that publish their social media management prices list monthly
> tiers from $99 to $8,280 in the US, 99 to 2,200 pounds in the UK, and 49
> to 3,000 euros in Ireland and Germany.
> Source: 17 agencies' own price pages, read October 14, 2026, tiers as
> printed, no agency named; seller-published, with no method behind them.

Dividing each tier by the posts it includes shows what the spread is made
of.

| Published tier type                              | Price per post, rounded                      | Defined week, 10 posts                              |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Volume tier, template graphics                   | $9 to $10; 3 to 16 pounds; 2 to 10 euros     | $93 to $99; 30 to 153 pounds; 23 to 100 euros       |
| Designed posts, some video                       | $38 to $63; 20 to 27 pounds; 19 to 167 euros | $375 to $625; 205 to 267 pounds; 191 to 1,667 euros |
| Full service with community management and video | $345 to $540                                 | $3,450 to $5,400                                    |

Per-post prices are the published monthly price divided by the posts each
tier includes, collected October 14, 2026. The week is ten times that.

The cheapest tiers buy a template, filled in at volume. The top tiers buy
someone managing the account: replies, monitoring, reporting, the video
edit. Read what each tier includes before the price, because the printed
terms carry costs of their own:

- **Ad spend is excluded** on the pages that mention it.
- **Setup or onboarding fees** of $200 and $300 appear on two US pages.
- **Minimum terms** run from none to three months and, on one German
  page, twelve.
- **Your own material.** Some entry tiers make content only from photos and
  video the client supplies.

That's why the real cost of brand content in 2026, which prices the making
of an asset, leaves social retainers out of its bands: a retainer bundles the
making with the managing, and the tier doesn't say how the price splits.

## What are the hidden costs of social media content?

They're the costs every route carries and no quote prints. The one all
five routes share is the review.

> A US marketing manager's median wage was $80.19 an hour in May 2025, which
> at the June 2026 load of 1.43 is about $115 an hour to the employer.
> Source: US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage
> Statistics, May 2025, and Employer Costs for Employee Compensation, June
> 2026.

Each hour that manager spends briefing, reviewing and sending back the
defined week costs about $115, on top of whatever route made it. If one
approval round becomes three, that hour gets paid three times.

| Hidden cost                  | In-house  | Freelance              | Agency                  | An app                |
| ---------------------------- | --------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------- |
| Manager review hours         | yes       | yes                    | yes                     | yes                   |
| Rework beyond agreed rounds  | paid time | billed hours           | by the tier's terms     | more runs             |
| Tool seats                   | yes       | by contract            | inside the tier         | none: no seat fees    |
| Stock licenses               | yes       | by contract            | check the tier          | only if you add stock |
| Recruiting and replacement   | yes       | finding the freelancer | no                      | no                    |
| Setup fees and minimum terms | no        | by contract            | on some published tiers | none                  |

Stock is the cheapest of these and the easiest to forget.

> Entry stock subscriptions start at about $29 to $30 a month, roughly $3 to
> $10 a standard image.
> Source: lowest monthly plans on three stock libraries' public price pages,
> collected October 12, 2026, category level.

Approval is where a week's cost grows without showing up on any quote. The
social media client approval guide on this site walks through running that
review in one place, with every version on one thread.

## What does the same week cost in an app?

An app has no weekly price to quote, only rules for what gets charged.
hubStudio has no subscription and no seat fees, and creating an account costs nothing. You top up a prepaid
balance in real currency, and each run is charged against it at the price
shown before it runs.

For the defined week, that means you pay for:

- **The images:** each run of the image studio, at the price shown first.
- **The two videos:** each render of the video studio, same rule.
- **AI writing, if you use it:** drafting posts with AI or improving a
  prompt is a paid run.
- **Posts sent to X**, if X is one of your networks: each one is charged,
  with the price shown before you send.
- **Storage and downloads:** a small charge against the balance.

A failed run is never charged. The Image editor is free. So is Validation,
where a manager or client approves work or sends it back with a comment, and
so are Campaigns, which keep one launch's files under one name. Publishing
to LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok (TikTok is in Beta) costs
nothing, which covers all four networks in the defined week.

What it doesn't remove is your team's time to brief and review, and the
manager hour from the section above. The pricing page on this site
sets out the same structure, with no amounts: the price of a run appears in
the app before you start it.

## When does a studio make more sense than any of the four?

When the week stops being routine. A launch, a new campaign look, a product
that has to be shot, or a market whose platforms you don't know: those weeks
need a senior art director's judgment more than another pair of hands.

hubStudio offers three ways to work, and the choice comes down to who you
want making the work:

- **Use the app.** Your team makes the week in hubStudio and pays as it goes
  from the prepaid balance.
- **Studio + app.** Our studio makes the work inside hubStudio. Your people
  review, comment and approve it in Validation and their Client space, then
  download or publish.
- **Studio only.** You send a brief and receive finished files, with no

A producer reads every brief within one business day, and a written proposal
follows within 48 hours. Studio work is quoted after the brief, as assets, a

## Social media content cost: questions buyers ask

### How much does social media content creation cost?

For ten posts a week on four networks, about $2,200 a week for one loaded US
marketing specialist at the national median, $430 to $560 a day for US
freelancers, and $93 to $5,400 a week at published US agency per-post
prices. The routes buy different things, so compare what each includes, not
just the total.

### Is it cheaper to hire a social media manager or an agency?

It depends on volume and use. A loaded US marketing specialist costs about
$2,164 a week at the median, whatever they produce. Published agency tiers
run from $99 to $8,280 a month. An agency is cheaper for a light, steady
schedule. A hire wins when one person is fully used across social and other
marketing work.

### What do freelancers charge for social media content?

On one marketplace's 2024 and 2025 booking data, US freelance social media
managers averaged $54 an hour and graphic designers $57. UK day rates were
326 and 348 pounds. German marketing freelancers averaged 86.70 to 94 euros
an hour in two 2026 surveys. The bill depends on how many days your week
takes.

### What are the hidden costs of social media content?

Manager review hours, rework past the agreed rounds, tool seats, stock
licenses, setup fees and minimum terms. Review is usually the largest: a US
marketing manager costs an employer about $115 an hour at the 2025 median
and the 2026 load. Every extra approval round repeats that hour on every
route.

### How does pay-as-you-go pricing work for AI content apps?

In hubStudio you top up a prepaid balance in real currency. Each run, an
image, a video render or an AI draft, is charged at the price shown before
it runs. A failed run isn't charged. There's no subscription and no seat
fee, and editing, approval, Campaigns and publishing to four networks cost
nothing.

### When should a brand use a studio instead of doing social content in-house?

When the week needs judgment more than capacity: a launch, a new look, a
product shoot or an unfamiliar market. A studio led by senior art directors
takes the brief and delivers finished work, inside your app account or
outside it. Routine weekly posting usually stays with your team or a
retainer.
