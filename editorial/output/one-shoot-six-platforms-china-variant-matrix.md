# One shoot, six platforms: the China variant matrix

Launch one SKU across Tmall, JD, Douyin, RedNote, WeChat and Weibo and, counted from
the published figures, it fills 24 slots for organic posts and marketplace listings,
or 27 with Douyin's paid formats. Four take a frame at the ratio it was captured in.
The rest are crops, re-exports and re-edits of those frames. The shoot needs four
set-ups, not six. Every one of those counts is arithmetic on published specs, and this
page shows the working.

<!-- SECTION: How to read these numbers -->

## How to read these numbers

Most of these specs are not read from a seller backend or a creator account. Tmall
and JD gate their rule text behind a seller login, and the platforms' creator and help
surfaces mostly serve no specification text to anyone not logged in, so no open source
reaches the official value for most rows.

What follows is, for most rows, the most commonly published figure for each spec,
taken from eight separate collections of 12 to 24 independent sources each, in
Chinese and English, on 10 September 2026, with the number of sources that agreed
shown per row. Counts from different collections are never added together. Rows marked
consensus had broad agreement. Rows marked contested did not, and those carry the full
spread rather than a single number.

Treat this as the best available public reading, not as the rule. Platform specs
change without announcement and often without an English notice at all. Before you
build a batch to any number on this page, open your own seller backend or creator
account and confirm it there. Reviewed 10 September 2026.

Rows marked primary are different. They come from four platform pages read on 10
September 2026: WeChat's Channels help-center article on posting requirements, WeChat's
Mini Program framework reference, Weibo's help-center video upload FAQ dated 1 July
2024, and Douyin's Open Platform upload API documentation. Each is primary only for
exactly what that page covers, and the row says which.

> Every value in this matrix comes from eight separate collections of published
> figures, one per platform or ad format, gathered on 10 September 2026. Each
> keeps its own source count, and counts from different collections are never
> added together. The composition boxes and the slot count further down are
> arithmetic on those values, not platform rules.
> Source: the Tmall product image, Tmall white-background, JD against Tmall,
> Douyin video, Douyin ad format, RedNote cover, WeChat and Weibo collections,
> each collected 10 September 2026 from pools of 12 to 24 sources. A statement of
> method, with no figure of its own.

## Can one shoot serve Tmall, JD, Douyin and RedNote?

Yes, if "one shoot" means four set-ups on one day rather than one frame. Three facts
from the published figures make that work.

> On the published record, Tmall and JD main images match. The most commonly
> published Tmall main image is a 1:1 square at 800 by 800 pixels, on 12 of 18
> sources for the ratio and 14 of 18 for the size. For JD, 6 of 6 pages give 1:1
> and 9 of 10 give an 800 by 800 floor. What changes is order: every page that
> names a slot puts the white-background image fifth on Tmall and first on JD.
> Source: the Tmall product image collection (18 domains) and the JD against Tmall
> collection (24 pages; slot row 5 of 5 per platform), both collected 10 September
> 2026. Modal values with counts kept per collection, not primary readings.

> The most commonly published vertical frames fit inside each other. Douyin's
> post frame is 9:16 at 1080 by 1920 pixels on 4 of 4 sources that name one.
> RedNote's cover is 3:4 on 14 of 14 sources that name a default, and 1080 by
> 1440 on 13. A full-width 3:4 cut from the center of a 1080 by 1920 frame
> measures exactly 1080 by 1440.
> Source: the Douyin video collection (12 domains) and the RedNote cover collection
> (20 domains), both collected 10 September 2026. Modal values with counts kept per
> collection; the fit is geometry, not a platform rule.

The third fact is the exception.

> The white-background image is the one listing asset a styled frame cannot
> supply. Fourteen of 15 sources that address it treat it as a deliverable with
> its own rules, and those rules exclude what a lifestyle frame holds: a model
> on 7 of 15, shadows on 6, text, logos and watermarks on 9. It wants one
> centered, front-view product on pure white, which means a separate set-up on
> the shoot day or a clean cutout in retouch.
> Source: the Tmall white-background collection, 15-domain white-background pool,
> 14 Chinese-language and 1 English-language, collected 10 September 2026, counted
> by domain. Modal values; the set-up conclusion is reasoning from those rows, not a
> platform rule.

collection: Tmall 18 sources (15 for the white-background image), JD 24 pages, Douyin

## Which China assets are re-crops and which need new capture?

Every slot falls into one of four kinds of work. The count below is one slot per
distinct platform surface, for organic posts and marketplace listings. Rows marked
optional in the matrix are left out.

| Cell type | What it means | Slots | Which |
|---|---|---|---|
| Native capture | The slot takes a frame at the ratio it was shot in; export only | 4 | Tmall white-background image, RedNote cover, RedNote inner images, Douyin post |
| Re-crop | The geometry changes, by a cut or a frame grab; nothing new is shot | 8 | Tmall main set, Douyin post cover, RedNote video cover, WeChat lead and secondary covers, Mini Program share card, Weibo grid images, Weibo video cover |
| Re-export | The same file, or a resize of it, goes to another slot, including a re-sequence | 8 | JD main set, JD white-background image, JD listing video, JD detail pages, RedNote video note, WeChat body images, Channels video, Weibo video |
| Re-edit | Timeline or layout work on existing footage or stills | 4 | Tmall listing video, Tmall desktop and mobile detail pages, size and specification chart |

> Built from the published figures, one SKU launched across Tmall, JD, Douyin,
> RedNote, WeChat and Weibo fills 24 distinct slots for organic posts and
> marketplace listings, or 27 with Douyin's in-feed, TopView and open screen
> formats. Of the 24, four take a frame at its captured ratio, eight are
> re-crops, eight re-use or re-export a file already made, and four are re-edits
> or page builds. Seven re-use an identical file, so 17 distinct files come out
> of the shoot.
> Source: arithmetic on this page's slot matrix, 10 September 2026, one slot per
> distinct platform surface, built only from rows counted in the eight spec
> collections. A scoping count, not a platform requirement and not a survey figure.
> Image counts inside a slot are ceilings and are not summed.

Apply a different scope, say every optional slot or every paid format on every
platform, and the number changes. That is why no image total appears here: carousel
and note ceilings are upper limits, not requirements.

The JD column holds the surprise. A separate JD detail page is a common line item, and
the published figures do not support it.

> A second detail page build for JD is not supported by the published figures.
> The most common JD desktop detail width is 790 pixels, on 5 of 9 pages, which
> is also the most common Tmall figure, and pages that publish both platforms
> usually give the same pair, 790 desktop and 750 mobile. The widely repeated
> split between the two may be a difference between sources rather than between
> platforms.
> Source: the JD against Tmall collection, 9 pages carrying a JD detail width and 6
> carrying a Tmall width, collected 10 September 2026. Modal values, contested, not
> primary readings. Confirm in both seller backends before re-using one build.

Douyin's paid formats sit at the other end. They cannot be cropped out of anything.

> Douyin's paid formats are edits of the vertical master rather than crops of
> it. TopView runs 10 to 60 seconds on 4 of 5 Douyin-specific sources, and 4 of 4
> that describe it say the first 3 seconds play full screen with nothing on top.
> Open screen shows a static image for 3 seconds, an animated image for 4 and a
> video for 5, on 5 of 5 sources. Each needs its own cut from the same footage.
> Source: the Douyin ad format collection, 17 sources, pages dated 2018 to 2025,
> collected 10 September 2026. Modal values, not primary readings; "its own cut" is
> reasoning from those durations.

> Those 24 slots need five aspect ratios, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16, 2.35:1 and 5:4, plus
> page and body widths that are builds rather than frames. Every square slot is
> a centered cut of the 3:4 still, so the shoot needs four set-ups: a 3:4 still,
> a 9:16 video, a wide landscape frame and a white background.
> Source: derived from this page's slot matrix, 10 September 2026, ratios as
> published in the eight spec collections with counts per collection in the matrix.
> Arithmetic, not a platform rule.

## What has to be captured on the shoot day?

Four set-ups, each composed so its crops survive. The checklist carries the
constraints from the collections above.

| Set-up | What it feeds | Composition constraint |
|---|---|---|
| 3:4 still, 1440x1920 or larger | RedNote cover and inner images, every square slot, Mini Program share card | Product and text between 288 and 1152 pixels down a 1080x1440 frame; all images in a note one size (contested) |
| 9:16 video, 1080x1920 or larger | Douyin post, RedNote video, Channels, Weibo video, three cover grabs, the square listing video cut | Critical content 108 to 972 across and 420 to 1392 down; a clean 3-second opening for TopView; a 5-second single-idea cut for open screen |
| Wide landscape | WeChat lead cover, Weibo horizontal cover if used | Subject inside a centered square of about 816 pixels |
| White background | Tmall fifth slot, JD first slot | One subject, front view, centered, no model, no shadow, no text, pure white |
| Category close-ups, only where they apply | Food label image, appliance certification mark and energy label | Each rests on one source, contested |

The capture sizes in the first two rows are production practice, not platform
figures. A 1440 by 1920 still simply leaves a 1440 square in hand if the contested
1440 upgrade applies. The composition figures come from three derived boxes.

> On a 1080 by 1920 vertical master that also has to yield a RedNote cover, a
> Weibo cover and square tiles, one region clears every published figure used
> here: 108 to 972 pixels across and 420 to 1392 pixels down. It sits inside
> every Douyin inset in circulation, inside the center square of a centered 3:4
> cut, and above the deepest bottom band published for RedNote covers.
> Source: derived 10 September 2026 from the Douyin video collection (its Douyin
> safe box, from 5 Douyin sources), the RedNote cover collection (its center-square
> intersection across 10 sources and the 288-pixel bottom reservation, the largest
> of 8), and the Weibo collection (its square-upload intersection). Assumes the 3:4
> and square cuts are centered, which no source states. A composition rule derived
> from published figures, not a platform rule.

The box stops at 1392 rather than at the foot of the center square for a reason. On
the most cautious published account, RedNote's interface overlay covers the lowest 15
to 20 percent of a cover, which reaches into the bottom of that square. The RedNote
cover specs article made the same point in a correction dated 10 September 2026.

> On a 3:4 still of 1080 by 1440 pixels, keeping the product and any text inside
> the full-width band from 288 to 1152 pixels down lets one frame serve the
> RedNote cover, a centered square for Tmall, JD, Weibo and WeChat secondary
> covers, and a centered 5:4 Mini Program share card. The band is the middle 60
> percent of the frame's height.
> Source: derived 10 September 2026 from the RedNote cover collection (3:4 at 1080
> by 1440 on 13; bottom reservation up to 288 pixels across 8), the Tmall product
> image collection (1:1 on 12 of 18), the JD against Tmall collection (1:1 on 6 of
> 6), the Weibo collection (square tiles on 4 references), and the WeChat collection
> (1:1 secondary cover on 11 of 11; 5:4 share image, primary, framework reference).
> Assumes centered cuts. Derived, not a platform rule.

> On a 1920 by 1080 landscape frame, a centered 2.35:1 cut for the WeChat lead
> cover keeps 817 pixels of height. Every published size for that cover's square
> crop equals the cover's own height, so the subject belongs in a centered square
> of about 816 by 816 pixels, roughly 552 to 1368 across and 132 to 948 down. The
> same square survives a 16:9 or a centered 4:3 Weibo horizontal video cover.
> Source: derived 10 September 2026 from the WeChat collection (2.35:1 lead cover on
> 8 of 11; square-crop principle on 6, with published square sizes of 383, 500 and
> 766 each matching its cover height) and the Weibo collection (horizontal video
> cover 16:9 or 4:3, Weibo help-center FAQ dated 1 July 2024, primary, scoped).
> Assumes centered cuts. Derived, not a platform rule.

## Questions about the China variant matrix

**How many assets does one SKU need for China?**

No platform publishes a required count. Counted from the published figures on this
page, one SKU across Tmall, JD, Douyin, RedNote, WeChat and Weibo fills 24 slots for
organic posts and marketplace listings, or 27 with Douyin's paid formats, and 17
distinct files come out of the shoot. That is arithmetic with a stated scope, not a
platform requirement.

**Can one shoot serve Tmall, JD, Douyin and RedNote?**

Yes, if the shoot covers four set-ups rather than one frame: a 3:4 still, a 9:16
video, a wide landscape frame and a white background. On the published figures, Tmall
and JD take the same square set in a different order, and a centered 3:4 cut of a 1080
by 1920 frame is exactly the most published RedNote cover size.

**Which China assets are re-crops and which need new capture?**

Of the 24 core slots counted here, four take a frame at its captured ratio, eight are
re-crops, eight re-use or re-export a file already made, and four are re-edits or page
builds. The white-background image is the one listing asset a styled frame cannot
supply, and Douyin's paid formats each need their own cut.

**What has to be captured on the shoot day?**

A 3:4 still at 1440 by 1920 or larger, a 9:16 video at 1080 by 1920 or larger with a
clean three-second opening, a wide landscape frame for the WeChat lead cover, and a
white-background set-up with one centered, front-view product. Add category close-ups,
such as a food label, only where the category calls for them.

**How many aspect ratios does a China launch need?**

Five on this page's count: 1:1, 3:4, 9:16, 2.35:1 and 5:4, plus detail page and
article body widths, which are builds rather than frames. Every square slot is a
centered cut of the 3:4 still, so no separate square set-up is needed. Most of the
ratios are commonly published values, not platform readings.
