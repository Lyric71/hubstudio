# The Tmall store content calendar, month by month

A Tmall store content calendar is the brand's own plan. The promotion dates
move from year to year, consumer guides print the wrong window, and the merchant
rules reported in the Chinese press ask a store for scores, deadlines and
prices. None of them asks for creative.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

## What goes in a Tmall store content calendar?

A Tmall store content calendar pins each promotion to the dates reported for
that year, then schedules store banners, listing images, detail-page slices and
short video ahead of them. None of five promotion rule sets reported in 2025 and
2026 names a creative asset, so the month-by-month plan below is hubStudio
production practice, not a platform list.

What breaks a plan is the date. It shifts by more than a week from year to
year, guides misprint it, and the rules land late. So every date here carries
its platform and its year.

## What are the main Tmall promotions each year?

Six events, five of them dated in the twelve months to September 2026. Times
are Beijing time. "Not dated" means no usable source dates that year's event.

| Event | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tmall New Year goods festival | Opened 8 p.m. 17 Jan 2024, ran to 31 Jan 2024 | Opened 8 p.m. 6 Jan 2025; end not reported | Presale warm-up 1 Jan 2026; spot sale 4 Jan to 11 Feb 2026 | Two outlets 2024; one report 2025; two outlets for the 2026 spot opening |
| Tmall March promotion | Not dated | Not dated | Presale 24 Feb to 3 Mar 2026; spot sale 24 Feb to 9 Mar 2026 | Two roundups, likely one origin |
| Tmall 618 | First wave 8 p.m. 20 May 2024, no presale | Presale 8 p.m. 13 May 2025 | Opened 21 May 2026; presale to 3 Jun, spot to 21 Jun 2026 | Triangulated |
| Tmall September sale | Not dated | Not dated | Not dated | No dated platform reporting found |
| Tmall Double 11 | Presale 8 p.m. 14 Oct 2024 | Presale 8 p.m. 15 Oct 2025; spot sale 20 Oct to 14 Nov 2025 | Presale 8 p.m. 15 Oct 2026; spot sale 20 Oct to 13 Nov 2026 | Triangulated to 2025; 2026 from two outlets, one origin |
| Taobao Double 12 | Not dated | Sign-up from noon 25 Nov 2025; warm-up 6 to 7 Dec 2025; sale 8 to 12 Dec 2025 | Not dated | Sale dates triangulated; sign-up single-source |

Every cell comes from Chinese press reporting the platform's own announcement or
merchant rules, January 2024 to September 2026. None was read from a rule center.

The New Year goods festival, nianhuo jie, shows why the year matters.

> Tmall's New Year goods festival sale opened on 17 January in 2024 and on 6
> January in 2025. In 2026 the spot sale opened on 4 January and closed on 11
> February, four days before the nine-day Spring Festival holiday began.
> Source: Chinese press reporting the platform's festival in January 2024,
> January 2025 and January 2026, against the State Council General Office
> notice on the 2026 public holiday schedule, published 4 November 2025. The
> four-day gap is date arithmetic.

The big two move too. Tmall's 618 opened eight days later in 2026 than in 2025.
The 11.11 and 618 production calendar article sets out both cycles in full.

No dated platform reporting was found for the September sale in 2024 or 2025,
for any summer promotion, or for a brand day schedule. Tmall's 2026 Double 11
rules were reported on 22 September 2026, and the spot sale closes on 13
November, one day earlier than in 2025.

## Why do published Tmall festival dates disagree?

Consumer guides outnumber press reports on every Chinese festival query, and in
two recent years they printed the wrong window.

> In both 2025 and 2026, Chinese consumer shopping guides printed New Year goods
> festival dates that did not match reporting of Tmall's own timetable: 17 to 31
> January for 2025, against a reported 6 January opening, and 19 to 31 January
> for 2026, against a reported spot sale from 4 January to 11 February.
> Source: Chinese consumer guides and ecommerce and technology press, December
> 2024 to February 2026. The conflict is published rather than resolved; the
> platform's timetable as reported is preferred because it carries every phase.

The 2025 guide window matches the 2024 festival to the day. That looks like a
page carried forward. Double 12 had the same problem at the other end.

> Some December 2025 consumer guides and one sponsored article gave Taobao's
> Double 12 an end date of 14 December. Reporting of the platform's merchant
> rules gave 11:59 p.m. on 12 December, and one guide listed 13 to 15 December
> as a separate return period.
> Source: Chinese trade reporting of the rules, 26 November 2025, against
> consumer guides and a sponsored article, 3 to 11 December 2025. The rules as
> reported are preferred; the conflict is published, not averaged.

Build to the timetable reported for that year's rules. When a guide and a rules
report disagree, the rules report wins.

## What do Tmall's promotion rules ask of a store?

Less than most briefs assume, and nothing about creative.

> For 618 in 2025, Tmall dropped merchant-level sign-up. Stores registered
> products only, from a list the platform recommended based on store data, and
> could submit it in one batch.
> Source: Chinese financial press reporting the platform's 2025 618 rules, 8
> May 2025. Rules as reported.

> Tmall's 2026 618 rules, as reported, required a store experience score of at
> least 4.2 and no more than 24 penalty points in the previous 90 days, products
> with at least one unit sold in the past 60 days and a product experience score
> of 60 or above, presale products listed before the presale warm-up and spot
> products listed by 10:00 p.m. on the first day of sale, price protection and
> free shipping, and shipment within 48 hours of payment.
> Source: Chinese ecommerce trade press reporting the platform's 2026 618
> merchant rules, 29 April 2026, republished by a Chinese technology news site
> the same day. Rules as reported, not read from the platform's rule center.

That listing deadline is the closest thing to a content deadline in any
reported rule set. It is reported for Tmall's 2026 618 only. Do not assume it
for Double 11, Double 12 or the New Year goods festival.

> None of five Tmall and Taobao promotion rule sets reported in the Chinese
> press between May 2025 and April 2026 names a required creative asset, a store
> decoration refresh, a livestream or a short video. The gates they report are
> store and product scores, sign-up windows, listing deadlines, pricing
> mechanics and shipping.
> Source: five Chinese press reports of platform merchant rules (Tmall 618 in
> 2025 and 2026, Tmall Double 11 2025, Taobao Double 12 2025, and the Tmall
> 2026 New Year goods festival), each read in full on 10 September 2026 for any
> content, decoration, livestream or video clause. An observation about the
> reporting, not a reading of the full rule text.

On the reported record, store content is a sales decision, not a compliance
item. The full rule text sits behind a seller login, so read each cycle's rules
in your own seller backend.

## How often should a Tmall store be redecorated?

No source says.

> No source found publishes how often a Tmall store should be redecorated for a
> promotion. There is no published campaign skin specification and no published
> size set for event entry modules. Nine Chinese-language sources carrying store
> decoration sizes publish no promotion skin sizes, and none of five reported
> 2025 and 2026 rule summaries mentions store decoration.
> Source: a nine-source Chinese-language store decoration collection read in
> full on 10 September 2026, five Chinese press reports of platform merchant
> rules from May 2025 to April 2026, and Chinese searches for a redecoration
> cadence run the same day. Stated as an absence, with the search date.

So the cadence is the brand's call. In production practice it follows the
promotions the store joins, plus one changeover each season. The Tmall flagship
store decoration specs article covers the module sizes the sources do publish.

## How much time sits between Tmall's promotions?

Not much, and the gaps are where production happens. Every figure here is date
arithmetic on reported dates, not a published lead time.

> Counted from reported dates, Taobao's 2025 Double 12 sign-up opened 11 days
> after Tmall's Double 11 spot sale closed on 14 November, and Tmall's 2026 New
> Year goods festival presale warm-up began 20 days after Double 12 closed.
> Source: date arithmetic, 10 September 2026, on Chinese press reporting of the
> platform's 2025 Double 11 rules (24 September 2025), its 2025 Double 12 rules
> (26 November 2025) and its 2026 New Year goods festival timetable (6 January
> 2026). Arithmetic on reported dates, not a published lead time.

The tightest squeeze is February.

> In 2026, Tmall's March promotion presale opened on 24 February, the day after
> the nine-day Spring Festival holiday ended.
> Source: date arithmetic, 10 September 2026, on Chinese news reporting of the
> platform's 2026 March promotion (25 February 2026) and the State Council
> General Office holiday notice (4 November 2025). Arithmetic on two dated
> sources, not a platform rule.

March work had to be finished before the break. Spring is looser: 51 days from
the March close to the reported 2026 618 rules, then 22 more to the opening.
Tmall's Double 11 rules came 21 days before the presale in 2025 and 23 days
before it in 2026. That is a
confirmation window, not a production window.

> No platform rule, government notice or survey found in September 2026
> publishes how long creative production should take before a Singles Day or
> 618 deadline. The lead times in circulation come from companies selling
> preparation services, with no sample and no method behind them.
> Source: search of Chinese- and English-language sources, 10 September 2026,
> covering reporting of platform rules and the pages ranking for four buyer
> phrasings. An observation about the published record, not a measurement.

## What does a flagship's content year look like, month by month?

What follows is production practice: one way to sequence a flagship's year
against the reported dates. No platform publishes a plan like it, and no
reported rule requires any row of it. Holiday dates are from the State Council's
2026 schedule.

| Month | Reported anchor, platform and year | Production practice |
|---|---|---|
| January | Tmall New Year goods festival spot sale from 4 Jan 2026 (6 Jan 2025, 17 Jan 2024) | Festival skin live; March promotion creative in approval |
| February | Tmall festival spot sale closed 11 Feb 2026; Spring Festival holiday 15 to 23 Feb 2026; Tmall March presale 24 Feb 2026 | March assets locked before the holiday; festival skin down |
| March | Tmall March promotion spot sale to 9 Mar 2026 | Season changeover on store modules; main image sets for new products |
| April | Tmall 618 rules reported 29 Apr 2026 | 618 hero and variants in production before the rules land |
| May | Tmall 618 opened 21 May 2026 (13 May 2025, 20 May 2024) | 618 skin live; presale products listed before the warm-up, as reported for 2026 only |
| June | Tmall 618 spot sale to 21 Jun 2026; Dragon Boat holiday 19 to 21 Jun 2026 | 618 skin down; store reset to always-on |
| July | No dated platform-wide sale reported | Always-on refresh; Double 11 hero and range shoot |
| August | No dated platform-wide sale reported | Double 11 detail-page slices, short video cuts and livestream backdrops from the same shoot |
| September | Tmall Double 11 rules reported 24 Sep 2025 and 22 Sep 2026 | Double 11 kit near final before the rules appear |
| October | National Day holiday 1 to 7 Oct 2026; Tmall Double 11 presale 8 p.m. 15 Oct 2026 (15 Oct 2025) | Double 11 skin and presale pages live |
| November | Tmall Double 11 spot sale to 13 Nov 2026 (14 Nov 2025); Taobao Double 12 sign-up from 25 Nov 2025 | Double 12 variants cut from the Double 11 master |
| December | Taobao Double 12 warm-up 6 to 7 Dec 2025, sale 8 to 12 Dec 2025; Tmall festival warm-up 1 Jan 2026 | Double 12 banners live before the warm-up; festival and March kits built before the Spring Festival break |

Two rows carry the year. December builds two promotions before the Spring
Festival holiday. July and August, with nothing dated, hold the Double 11 shoot.
Miss either and the plan runs a cycle behind.

## What gets reused and what gets rebuilt each cycle?

This is derived analysis from the published spec collections and the reported
rules, not a platform rule. It starts from what stays the same.

> Every Chinese production reference checked builds a listing from the same
> named parts: a main image set with a pure-white-background image inside it,
> a long scrolling detail page, a separate mobile detail page, a size and
> specification chart, store decoration banners, and short video. The parts
> are stable. The dimensions attached to them are not.
> Source: eight independent Chinese design, tool and seller-education
> references collected September 2026, compared on their deliverable lists
> only, with every number they carried set aside.

| Asset | Each cycle | Why | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| White-background image | Reused | Most sources describe it feeding app recommendation channels year-round; no reported promotion rule touches it | Derived |
| Main image set | Reused; rebuilt for new products or a season change | Promotional overlay text sits on the most published main-image prohibition list, so festival messaging lives elsewhere | Derived |
| Store banners and campaign skin | Rebuilt | No published skin size set; built at the widest published width and exported down | Derived |
| Detail pages | Body kept; campaign slice swapped | The listing's parts are stable; only the offer changes | Practice |
| Short video | Master kept; campaign cut rebuilt | One shoot, a new edit per promotion | Practice |
| Offer, price and shipping details | Checked for each cycle and year | Reported sign-up and listing rules differ by cycle and year | Derived |

Festival messaging belongs in store modules, where it comes down when the sale
ends. The Tmall product image requirements article and the Tmall
white-background image rules article carry the counted specs behind those rows.

## What should a Tmall store refresh checklist cover?

Production practice, run before each cycle the store joins.

- This cycle's rules report for this year, read in full.
- Sign-up window and listing deadline noted for this cycle only.
- Consumer-guide dates set aside in favor of the reported timetable.
- Campaign banners and skin built at the widest published width, then
  exported down.
- Main images free of promotional overlay text.
- White-background image unchanged and still compliant.
- Detail-page campaign slice swapped, body untouched.
- Short video campaign cut exported from the master.
- A removal date set for the skin and the campaign slices.
- Every size confirmed in your own seller backend on the day of production.

The Tmall platform page covers the store work itself, and the retailers
solutions page covers running a catalog through the year.

Reviewed 27 September 2026. Check each platform's rules for the current year
before planning to any date on this page.

## Questions about a Tmall store's content year

**What are the main Tmall promotions each year?**

On reported dates from October 2025 to September 2026, five platform-wide sales:
Tmall Double 11, Taobao Double 12, the Tmall New Year goods festival, the Tmall
March promotion and Tmall 618. The dates move every year. The 2026 New Year
goods festival spot sale ran from 4 January to 11 February. No dated platform
reporting of the September sale was found.

**How often should a Tmall store be redecorated?**

No source found publishes a cadence, a campaign skin specification or a size
set for event entry modules, and none of five rule sets reported in 2025 and
2026 mentions decoration. The cadence is a brand decision. In production
practice, the store changes for each promotion it joins and once each season,
then returns to an always-on layout.

**What content does a Tmall flagship need each month?**

No platform publishes a monthly list. In production practice, the months before
each promotion carry the work: store banners and a campaign skin, detail-page
campaign slices, short video cuts, and main image sets for new products. July
and August, when no platform-wide sale was dated, go to the Double 11 shoot and
the always-on refresh.

**How far ahead should Tmall campaign content be ready?**

No platform rule, government notice or survey found publishes a lead time. The
rules arrive late: Tmall's 2025 Double 11 rules became public 21 days before the
presale, and its 2026 618 rules 22 days before the opening. For 618 in 2026,
presale products had to be listed before the presale warm-up, as reported. So
production starts before the rules.

**What can be reused between Tmall promotions?**

On the derived analysis here, the white-background image and the core main
image set carry across cycles, because no reported rule touches them and
promotional overlay text sits on the most published main-image prohibition
list. Store banners and the campaign skin are rebuilt each cycle, detail pages
keep their body and swap a campaign slice, and short video keeps its master.
