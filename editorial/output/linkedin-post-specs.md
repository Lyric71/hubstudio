# LinkedIn image, video and document post specs for 2026

LinkedIn's help page for company page images, updated about two months ago,
lists the cover at 1512 by 256 pixels. Plenty of size guides still print an
older number.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

## What image size does LinkedIn want in 2026?

The LinkedIn image size for a feed post in 2026: at least 552 by 276 pixels,
1080 wide recommended, any shape from 3:1 to 4:5, and no more than 5MB. A
portrait at 1080 by 1350 fills the whole 4:5 frame. hubStudio's Image editor
marks that size Best for a LinkedIn post.

Reviewed October 8, 2026. Every number on this page comes from a LinkedIn
Help Center or LinkedIn Marketing Solutions page, read directly that day. The
help center shows each article's age instead of a date ("updated 2 months
ago"), so each row carries that age as it stood on October 8. Where two
LinkedIn pages disagree, both are printed, and the newer one is named.

## How long can a LinkedIn video be?

Fifteen minutes, by the newer of two LinkedIn pages. They don't agree.

> LinkedIn's video troubleshooting article, about two years old, gives a
> 15-minute maximum. Its video specs article for Pages and Career Pages, about
> four years old, gives 10 minutes and says: "We no longer support AVI,
> QuickTime, or MOV files." The file types article, updated about two weeks
> before this read, lists MOV on desktop, iOS and Android.
> Source: LinkedIn Help articles a548372, a1311816 and a564109, read
> directly, October 2026. https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a548372

An MP4 under 10 minutes satisfies all three pages. That's the safe build.
MOV is fine by the newest page; ProRes is the real trap, covered under
failures below.

## What crops in the LinkedIn feed on mobile?

LinkedIn's pages give five rules for what survives in the feed. Two are
written for mobile. The other three apply on every screen, phones included.

- **The 4:5 ceiling.** A photo taller than 4:5 is centered and cropped. A
  9:16 story frame loses its top and bottom, and text placed there goes with
  them.
- **The first picture sets the grid.** In a multi-photo post, a landscape
  first image sits on top with the rest side by side below it; a portrait or
  square one takes the left column. When the rows run past 4:5, the bottom
  row is cropped.
- **Link images pad, they don't crop.** On a Page post with a link, mobile
  shows a 1.91:1 image whole. Other shapes get thin white padding.
- **Vertical ads are phone-only.** LinkedIn's single image ad specs say paid
  vertical assets serve on mobile devices only, and square or vertical ad
  images may be cropped when the ad is shared organically.
- **Video edges belong to the interface.** Keep text and logos off the top,
  bottom and sides, where LinkedIn's own controls can cover them.

The company cover is trimmed too, horizontally or vertically depending on the
screen. LinkedIn says to keep key details in the center and away from the
lower-right corner.

## LinkedIn ad specs: single image, video and document ads

Paid specs live on a separate help center, LinkedIn Marketing Solutions, and
they don't track the organic ones. A video ad can run twice as long as an
organic video, yet it has to weigh a tenth as much.

| Ad format | Files and weight | Shapes and pixels | Length or pages | Source, age |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single image | JPG, PNG, GIF (250 frames); 5MB | 1.91:1 at 1200 x 628; 1:1 at 1200 x 1200; 4:5 at 720 x 900 | n/a | Single image ads specs, about 2 months |
| Video | MP4, H.264 or VP8; 75KB to 500MB | 16:9 at 1920 x 1080; 1:1 up to 1920 x 1920; 4:5 at 720 x 900; 9:16 at 720 x 1280 | 3 seconds to 30 minutes | Video ads specs, about 2 months |
| Document | PDF, PPT, PPTX, DOC, DOCX; 100MB | One page size throughout | 300 pages; 1 million words | Document ads specs, about 11 months |
| Carousel | JPG, PNG, still GIF; 10MB a card | 1:1 at 1080 x 1080, max 4320 x 4320 | 2 to 10 cards | Carousel ads specs, about 11 months |

> LinkedIn's video ad specs set a length of three seconds to 30 minutes and
> recommend 15 to 30 seconds "so they can qualify for all possible
> placements," with files between 75KB and 500MB. Read directly from the
> help article, about two months old.
> Source: LinkedIn Marketing Solutions Help, "Video ads advertising
> specifications," read October 2026.
> https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a424737

Ad copy has two numbers per field: where it truncates and where it stops.
Introductory text truncates after 150 characters on all three formats.
Headlines truncate at 70 and stop at 200. Single image ads under 401 pixels
wide show as a thumbnail.

Document ads with a lead generation objective take PDFs only. Links inside a
document work only after the viewer downloads it. LinkedIn's business spec
page for document ads recommends staying under 10 pages.

## Why LinkedIn uploads fail

LinkedIn's help pages name the common causes, and each one below comes from
them.

- **ProRes.** It may upload, LinkedIn says, "but it will not be able to
  process." Video ads don't take it at all.
- **iCloud and a backgrounded app.** The mobile app can't upload a video
  stored in iCloud, and an upload from iOS can stall if the app goes to the
  background.
- **Layered or mixed-size PDFs.** Flatten the layers. Fit every page to one
  size. Animations and embedded video play as still images.
- **No title.** LinkedIn's checklist for a failed document upload ends on
  it: make sure the document has a title.
- **Editing after posting.** A posted photo can't be resized or edited, and a
  posted document can't be swapped. Delete the post and start again.
- **A link and a picture together.** A post carries one or the other.
- **Video ads from a phone.** Campaign Manager takes video uploads from a
  desktop only, 25 in 24 hours.

LinkedIn gives document pages in inches, not pixels. Its document ad
guidance lists standard layouts such as Letter, A4 and Legal, and asks for
one page size throughout. Put the layout and that rule in the brief, whether
the deck comes from your own team or from the presentation design service.

## FAQ

### What is the best image size for a LinkedIn post in 2026?

LinkedIn recommends a photo 1080 pixels wide and accepts shapes from 3:1 to
4:5. A portrait at 1080 by 1350 is exactly 4:5, the tallest shape the feed
shows without cropping. Keep the file under 5MB, the limit LinkedIn's photo
help gives for an upload.

### What size is the LinkedIn company page banner?

LinkedIn's Page image help lists 1512 by 256 pixels as both the minimum and
the recommended cover, as a PNG or JPEG of 3MB or less. The article was
updated about two months before October 8, 2026. Older figures of 1128 by 191
and 4200 by 700 still circulate; neither appears on LinkedIn's current page.

### How long can a LinkedIn video be?

LinkedIn's video troubleshooting article gives 15 minutes, with a 3-second
minimum on desktop and 2 seconds in the mobile app. An older article for
Pages and Career Pages says 10 minutes. Video ads run longer: three seconds
to 30 minutes, with 15 to 30 seconds recommended so the ad qualifies for
every placement.

### What size should a LinkedIn carousel PDF be?

LinkedIn sets no pixel size for documents. It caps them at 100MB and 300
pages, asks for every page at the same size, and lists standard layouts such
as Letter and A4 for document ads. Flatten the PDF's layers first. Animations
and video inside the file show as still images.

### Can I post MOV files on LinkedIn?

Yes, by LinkedIn's newest page. Its media file types article, updated about
two weeks before October 8, 2026, lists MOV on desktop, iOS and Android. An
older article for Pages still says MOV isn't supported. Apple ProRes is the
problem codec: it may upload, LinkedIn says, but it won't process.

### What is the LinkedIn character limit for a post?

3,000 characters, according to LinkedIn's help article on posting updates.
Anything longer, LinkedIn suggests, belongs in an article. Ads count
differently: introductory text truncates after 150 characters on single
image, video and document ads, and a headline truncates at 70.

### Can hubStudio publish carousels to a LinkedIn company page?

Yes. Connect LinkedIn on My Connections, and every company page LinkedIn
lists you as an admin of comes back next to your profile. A post can carry
one image or a carousel of 2 to 8 slides, published now or on a schedule, to
up to 20 accounts at once. Video and document posts go through LinkedIn's
own composer.
