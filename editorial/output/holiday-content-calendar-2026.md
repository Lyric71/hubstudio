# Black Friday to New Year: the 2026 holiday content calendar, counted backwards

Brief Black Friday paid social after Friday, Oct. 30, and on the counting
below it spends Black Friday still learning. Every date here comes from the
same arithmetic: start from what the platforms, the carriers and the
regulators publish, then count back to the day the brief has to land.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

## What does the 2026 holiday content calendar look like, counted backwards?

Black Friday is Nov. 27, 2026, but the last day to brief its paid social is
Oct. 30, and a 20 to 50 SKU marketplace refresh needed briefing by Oct. 6. The
dates below count back from published ad review times, Amazon's deal window
and the USPS cutoffs, using the hubStudio studio's own published production
windows.

| Moment | 2026 date | In market by | Files delivered by | Brief by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Black Friday, paid social | Fri. Nov. 27 | Thu. Nov. 19 | Thu. Nov. 12 | Fri. Oct. 30 |
| Thanksgiving, email and posts (US) | Thu. Nov. 26 | Thu. Nov. 26 | Thu. Nov. 19 | Fri. Nov. 6 |
| Cyber Monday | Mon. Nov. 30 | Mon. Nov. 30 | Fri. Nov. 20 | Mon. Nov. 9 |
| December gifting | From Tue. Dec. 1 | Tue. Dec. 1 | Mon. Nov. 23 | Tue. Nov. 10 |
| Christmas, after the last ground-shipping day | Fri. Dec. 25 | Fri. Dec. 18 | Fri. Dec. 11 | Mon. Nov. 30 |
| Boxing Day (UK) | Sat. Dec. 26 | Sat. Dec. 26 | Fri. Dec. 18 | Mon. Dec. 7 |
| New Year sales | Fri. Jan. 1, 2027 | Fri. Jan. 1 | Thu. Dec. 24 | Fri. Dec. 11 |
| French winter sales | Wed. Jan. 6, 2027 | Wed. Jan. 6, 8 a.m. | Tue. Dec. 29 | Tue. Dec. 15 |

How the dates were counted: business days only, skipping the US federal
holidays on Nov. 26, Dec. 25 and Jan. 1. Two business days between approved
files and the in-market date, for platform review and scheduling. Three
between delivered files and approval, for one consolidated round of sign-off.
Seven of production, the top of the studio's published window for a full
launch batch. Two for the written proposal. The review and sign-off allowances
are this page's planning assumptions, not measurements, because no platform,
carrier or survey publishes a creative lead time. Swap in your own team's
numbers and the rows move with them.

> Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, Christmas on Friday, Dec. 25,
> and New Year's Day on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027.
> Source: US Office of Personnel Management, federal holidays 2026 and 2027,
> read Oct. 8, 2026. Government holiday list.

Black Friday and Cyber Monday follow from that: the day after Thanksgiving
and the Monday after it. Europe runs Black Friday on the same Friday without
the Thursday holiday, so a European team gains a working day in late November.

## Which platform deadlines actually bind?

Only a handful of dates in the season are set by someone other than you. They
are the ones worth building the plan around, because no amount of overtime
moves them.

| Platform or carrier | What it publishes | What it means for the plan |
|---|---|---|
| Meta (Facebook, Instagram) | Most ads reviewed within 24 hours; any creative edit starts a new review | Submit two business days before the ad has to run |
| TikTok | Most ads reviewed within 24 hours; a creative edit triggers a review of up to 24 hours | Same two days |
| Google Ads | Most ads reviewed within one business day; submit several days ahead for a fixed date | Same two days, more for new landing pages |
| Google Merchant Center | Submit promotions at least 24 hours before they start | One business day, Monday to Friday |
| Amazon (US store) | Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday deals submitted by Oct. 20; A+ review up to seven business days | Deals locked 38 days before Black Friday |
| USPS | Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail by Dec. 17 for delivery before Dec. 25 | "Order by" creative comes down Dec. 18 |

> Most ads are reviewed within 24 hours, although in some cases it may take
> longer, and any edit to the creative starts a new review.
> Source: Meta Business Help Center, "About ads in review," read Oct. 8, 2026.
> Platform help article, page undated.

> Most TikTok ads are reviewed within 24 hours; editing the creative triggers
> a review that may take up to 24 hours.
> Source: TikTok Ads Help Center, "Ad Review FAQs," updated February 2025,
> read Oct. 8, 2026.

> Most Google ads are reviewed within one business day, and an ad needed by a
> particular date should go in several days in advance.
> Source: Google Ads Help, "About the ad review process," read Oct. 8, 2026.
> Platform help article.

"Most" is doing work in all three sentences. A rejected ad goes back to the
designer, comes back as new creative and joins the queue again. That second
trip is why the table leaves two days, not one.

Google adds one condition worth a line in the plan: a new landing page has to
be finished before the ad pointing at it can be reviewed. If the Black Friday
page is in build, the ad waits with it.

<!-- SECTION: Learning week -->

## Why does Black Friday paid social go live a week early?

Because a new ad starts in a learning period, and Meta says how long that
period usually lasts.

> An ad set usually leaves the learning phase after about 50 results in the
> week after its last significant edit, and adding a new ad or changing the
> creative counts as a significant edit.
> Source: Meta Business Help Center, "About the learning phase" and
> "Significant edits and learning phase," read Oct. 8, 2026. Platform help
> articles, pages undated.

During that week, Meta says, delivery is less stable and the cost per result
is usually higher. Drop fresh creative into a running ad set on Black Friday
morning and the ad set spends Black Friday relearning.

So the calendar puts Black Friday ads in market on Thursday, Nov. 19, eight
days out. The learning week closes on Thanksgiving, and the files have to be
approved by Tuesday, Nov. 17, to clear review in time.

Cyber Monday is the trap. A Cyber Monday ad added on Nov. 30 restarts learning
on Cyber Monday itself. The cleaner route is to build it into the Black Friday
batch, approve both together and schedule the switch. Meta reviews a scheduled
ad when it's submitted, not when it starts running.

<!-- SECTION: Amazon -->

## What does Amazon's calendar add?

An earlier deadline than anything on the social side.

> The deal submission window for Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday runs from
> July 8 to Oct. 20.
> Source: Amazon Seller Central news post, "Holiday 2026: Same fees, same
> eligibility, earlier deadlines," US store, July 2026. The marketplace's own
> announcement.

That is 38 days before Black Friday. By Oct. 20 the deals themselves are in,
products and deal prices included. The images and A+ pages around them are
not, and they run on their own clock.

> A+ Content review takes up to seven business days.
> Source: Amazon Seller Central, A+ Content guide, US store, read Sept. 10,
> 2026. Marketplace help article, page undated.

Counted back from the same Nov. 19 in-market date, a refreshed A+ page has to
be submitted by Tuesday, Nov. 10. Add three days of sign-off and the files are
due Thursday, Nov. 5. On the windows the ecommerce design service page
publishes, roughly two weeks for a single-SKU pack and three to four weeks for
a 20 to 50 SKU catalog, that puts the brief at Oct. 20 for one product and
Oct. 6 for a catalog.

Oct. 6 has passed. On those numbers, a catalog refresh that hasn't been
briefed won't clear A+ review before Black Friday Week. Cut it to the hero
products, or keep the current pages.

Keeping them costs less than it sounds, because an A+ page can't carry the
deal anyway.

> A+ Content may not include pricing or promotional language, time-sensitive
> references, or shipping details.
> Source: Amazon Seller Central, A+ Content guide, US store, read Sept. 10,
> 2026, rechecked Sept. 14, 2026. Marketplace help article, page undated.

The Black Friday message lives in the deal itself and in the ads that point
at the listing. The A+ refresh is about the product, which is why it can be
briefed before the season's offers are final. Amazon hadn't published the 2026
start date of Black Friday Week when this page was reviewed. If it opens
before Nov. 19, every Amazon date here moves earlier. The Amazon platform page covers the
marketplace work in more detail.

<!-- SECTION: December -->

## What changes in December?

The deadline stops being a sale and becomes a delivery date.

> For expected delivery before Dec. 25 in the contiguous US, send by Dec. 17
> for Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail, Dec. 18 for Priority Mail and
> Dec. 19 for Priority Mail Express.
> Source: US Postal Service newsroom release, Sept. 22, 2026. The carrier's
> own recommended dates.

From Cyber Monday to that Ground Advantage date is 17 days. That is the whole
gifting window that ships at standard rates, and it needs its own creative:
gift guides, "order by" banners and email headers that name the date.

Then the message flips. On Dec. 18 the "order by" banners have to come down
and something else has to go up: express shipping, gift cards, store pickup.
That switch is the Christmas row in the calendar, in market Friday, Dec. 18,
with a brief due Monday, Nov. 30. That's Cyber Monday. Few retail teams have a
spare hour that day, so send the December brief out with the Cyber Monday one.

The December gifting row has the same problem from the other side. Its files
need approval by Friday, Nov. 27, Black Friday itself. Approve them before
Thanksgiving.

Royal Mail's 2026 last posting dates weren't available at source when this
page was reviewed, so UK "order by" dates aren't in the calendar. For
Christmas Day itself, anything organic can be written in November and
scheduled.

<!-- SECTION: Europe -->

## When do Boxing Day and the January sales start in Europe?

Boxing Day is Saturday, Dec. 26, in the UK. The bank holiday moves to Monday.

> In England, Wales and Scotland, Christmas Day falls on Friday, Dec. 25,
> 2026, and the Boxing Day bank holiday on Monday, Dec. 28, as a substitute
> day.
> Source: GOV.UK, UK bank holidays, read Oct. 8, 2026. Government data.

A Boxing Day sale that opens Saturday morning has to clear review by
Wednesday, Dec. 23, and its files land with approvers on Friday, Dec. 18, the
last full working week before Christmas. That's the real deadline. The calendar
counts Dec. 24 and Dec. 31 as working days; if your approvers are off, move
those rows a day earlier.

France regulates its sale dates, and the rule is fixed in advance.

> Winter sales open on the second Wednesday of January at 8 a.m., moved to the
> first Wednesday when the second falls after the 12th.
> Source: Service Public, the French government's information service,
> Dec. 12, 2025. The regulated rule, as published for the 2026 sales.

In January 2027 the second Wednesday is the 13th, so the sales open Wednesday,
Jan. 6, at 8 a.m. across most of metropolitan France. Four eastern departments
set their own dates. The official 2027 notice hadn't been published when this
page was reviewed; the date above is the rule applied to the calendar.

Anywhere in the EU, a sale visual that announces a reduction carries one more
dependency.

> Any announcement of a price reduction must show the prior price: the lowest
> price the trader applied in at least the 30 days before the reduction.
> Source: Directive (EU) 2019/2161, Article 6a of Directive 98/6/EC, applied
> from May 28, 2022. EU legislation; national rules vary in detail.

For a Jan. 6 sale, those 30 days run through December's own promotions. The
artwork can be finished early, but the price on it waits for whoever owns the
price history. This describes production practice, not legal advice.

## What assets does each moment need?

Four families, and most moments need all four.

| Moment | Feed | Reels and TikTok | Marketplace images | Email headers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Black Friday and Cyber Monday | Offer statics, carousels | Offer cuts, one per offer | Clean main images, A+ refresh without the offer | Teaser, launch, last call |
| Thanksgiving (US) | One brand post | Optional | None | One send |
| December gifting | Gift guides by price or recipient | Gift-guide cuts | Lifestyle secondary images | "Order by" date in the header |
| Christmas, after the cutoff | Gift cards, pickup | Short reminders | None | Express and gift-card sends |
| Boxing Day, New Year, January sales | Sale statics | Sale cuts | Product images only, the sale stays in the deal | Sale launch and reminder |

Each family has its own published frame, and the frames don't change for the
holidays.

> Meta's feed image ads run 4:5 at 1440x1800. Reels ads run 9:16 at 1440x2560,
> with the top 14 percent, the bottom 35 percent and 6 percent on each side
> kept clear of text, logos and key elements.
> Source: Meta's Facebook Ads Guide, feed image and Reels video ad
> specifications, read Sept. 10, 2026, rechecked Sept. 15, 2026. Platform
> documentation.

> TikTok recommends 9:16 at or above 540x960 for auction in-feed video.
> Amazon's main image sits on pure white, RGB 255, 255, 255, and 1,000 pixels
> or more on the longest side enables zoom.
> Source: TikTok Ads Help Center, auction in-feed specifications, updated
> June 2026; Amazon Seller Central, Product image guide, US store; both read
> Sept. 10, 2026.

Email answers to no ad review queue, so it's the one family that can move
late.

## How long does production take?

No platform or survey publishes a creative lead time, so the calendar uses the
windows the studio publishes on its own service pages. They're stated policy,
not measured averages, and they assume a standard brief.

| Asset family | Published window | Where it is published |
|---|---|---|
| Paid social launch batch (statics, video cuts, platform variants) | Five to seven business days | Ad creative service page |
| Email campaign template | About a week | Email design service page |
| Marketplace pack, one SKU, one channel | Roughly two weeks | Ecommerce design service page |
| Marketplace catalog, 20 to 50 SKUs, one channel | Three to four weeks | Ecommerce design service page |
| Studio shoot, standard scope | One to two weeks, brief to delivery | Video production service page |
| Brief to written proposal | Read within one business day, proposal within 48 hours | Pricing page |

A shoot adds its window on top of the batch that uses its footage. If the
Black Friday cuts need a new studio shoot, the brief moves back up to two
weeks, to Friday, Oct. 16.

## Where does AI production shorten the chain, and where doesn't it?

It shortens the making. It doesn't touch the queues.

What generation compresses is versioning: one approved idea turned into each
format and offer, in each market. A product photographed once can sit in a
gifting scene in early December and a sale scene on Boxing Day without anyone
going back on set. In the hubStudio app, a team edits from up to four source
pictures, runs 1 to 10 images at a time at up to 4K, crops each result to the
network formats in the Image editor and keeps the season's files together in a
Campaign. Approvals sit on one thread in Validation with every version kept,
so the Cyber Monday variant doesn't reopen the Black Friday conversation.
Organic posts for Thanksgiving and Christmas Day can be written in November
and scheduled to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and TikTok (Beta).

None of that moves anyone else's deadline. Meta, TikTok and Google review
every ad. The learning week runs whatever made the creative. Amazon's deal
window closes on Oct. 20, and A+ review can take up to seven business days.
The USPS ground date is Dec. 17. The prior price on a European sale visual
waits for the pricing team.

A physical product is physical, too. A SKU nobody has photographed
needs a shoot or a 3D model before anything can be generated from it. And
Amazon's product image guide asks for a photorealistic, fully AI-generated
person to be tagged as a synthetic performer in the image's metadata (one more
line on the checklist, not a reason to avoid it).

Our studio decides asset by asset what gets shot and what gets generated. The
ad creative service covers the paid social side of that split, the ecommerce
design service the marketplace side.

## What about Singles' Day and China?

China runs its own season. Singles' Day on Nov. 11 and the 618 festival in
June follow the Chinese platforms' published milestones, which open well
before the day itself, and the 11.11 and 618 production calendar counts those
back the same way this page does. A brand selling in both runs two calendars.
In November they overlap.

## Questions about the 2026 holiday calendar

**When should Black Friday 2026 creative be briefed?**

For paid social, by Friday, Oct. 30, on this page's counting, so files land
Nov. 12, sign-off finishes Nov. 17 and ads go live Nov. 19, a full learning
week before Black Friday on Nov. 27. A refreshed Amazon A+ page needed its
brief by Oct. 20 for one product, earlier for a catalog.

**How long does Meta and TikTok ad review take before Black Friday?**

Both platforms say most ads are reviewed within 24 hours, and both start a new
review when the creative changes. Google says most ads take one business day.
Plan two business days so a rejected ad can be fixed and resubmitted, and
remember Meta's learning period runs about a week after any new ad goes in.

**When is the Amazon deal deadline for Black Friday 2026?**

Amazon's US store takes Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday deal submissions
from July 8 to Oct. 20, 2026. A+ Content review takes up to seven business
days, so a refreshed A+ page in market by Nov. 19 has to be submitted by Nov.
10. Amazon hadn't published the Black Friday Week start date by early October.

**What is the last shipping day for Christmas 2026?**

In the contiguous US, USPS recommends sending by Dec. 17 for Ground Advantage
and First-Class Mail, Dec. 18 for Priority Mail and Dec. 19 for Priority Mail
Express. Your own carrier's cutoff decides the date in your "order by"
headers, and those banners need replacement creative ready for the day after.

**When do the January 2027 sales start in France and the UK?**

France's winter sales open on the first Wednesday of January when the second
falls after the 12th, which puts them at 8 a.m. on Wednesday, Jan. 6, 2027, in
most of the country. In the UK, Boxing Day falls on Saturday, Dec. 26, with
the bank holiday on Monday, Dec. 28.

**Can AI production shorten the holiday content calendar?**

It shortens production: variants, resizes and scene changes come from one
approved master instead of a new shoot. It doesn't shorten anything set by
someone else. Ad review, Meta's learning week, Amazon's deal window, A+ review,
carrier cutoffs and price sign-off all keep their own clocks, and the calendar
has to respect them.

**What assets does a Black Friday campaign need?**

Usually four families: feed statics and carousels, vertical cuts for Reels and
TikTok, marketplace images including A+ pages, and email headers for the
teaser, launch and last call. Cyber Monday and the December gifting set are
best briefed in the same batch, because their approval dates fall in
Thanksgiving week.
