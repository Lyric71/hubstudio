# The 11.11 and 618 production calendar, counted backwards

Every page ranking for this explains what Singles Day is or how much it sold. None
prints the platforms' own dated milestones, and none counts a production schedule
back from them. This page does both, and it is careful about which half is reported
and which half is arithmetic.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

## When do Singles Day assets have to be ready?

Before the presale opens, and the presale date is the one milestone the platforms
announce and the press reports with exact timestamps. For Tmall in 2025 that was
8:00 p.m. on 15 October. How long before that date production has to start is
published by no platform, no government notice and no survey. So everything that
follows separates the reported dates from the schedule counted back from them.

> No platform rule, government notice or survey found in September 2026
> publishes how long creative production should take before a Singles Day or
> 618 deadline. The lead times in circulation come from companies selling
> preparation services, with no sample and no method behind them.
> Source: search of Chinese- and English-language sources, 10 September 2026,
> covering reporting of platform rules and the pages ranking for four buyer
> phrasings. An observation about the published record, not a measurement.

## What are the Tmall presale milestones?

All times below are Beijing time, and every date carries its platform and year,
because the dates move and a date without a year is how a stale schedule ends up in
a brief.

> Tmall's 2025 Singles Day presale opened with a warm-up at 2:00 p.m. on 15
> October, took deposits from 8:00 p.m. that evening until 5:59 p.m. on 20
> October, and opened the spot sale at 8:00 p.m. on 20 October, running to
> 11:59 p.m. on 14 November.
> Source: Chinese technology and ecommerce trade press reprinting Tmall's
> published 2025 Double 11 rules, 24 September 2025. Two outlets carried the
> platform's timetable the same day, with identical timestamps.

The rules themselves arrive later than most planning assumes.

> Tmall's 2025 Singles Day rules became public 21 days before its presale
> opened.
> Source: Chinese trade press reporting the platform's rules on 24 September
> 2025, against a presale opening of 15 October 2025. Date arithmetic on two
> reported dates.

Twenty-one days between the rules and the presale is not a production window. It is
a confirmation window. Anything that needs to be shot, generated, retouched and
approved has to be underway well before the rules that govern it are public.

> Tmall opened its Singles Day presale at 8:00 p.m. on 24 October in 2023 and
> at 8:00 p.m. on 14 October in 2024, ten days earlier, then at 8:00 p.m. on
> 15 October in 2025, one day later.
> Source: Chinese financial press reporting the platform's announcements, 21
> October 2023, 13 October 2024 and 24 September 2025. Opening dates as
> announced by the platform for each year.

That sequence matters more than any single date. The start moved ten days earlier
into 2024 and then one day later in 2025. It does not simply move earlier every
year, and a plan that assumes it does will start earlier than it needs to, or be
wrong-footed the year it does not.

## JD and Douyin run their own clocks

> JD opened its Singles Day sale at 8:00 p.m. on 23 October in 2023, with no
> presale, at 8:00 p.m. on 14 October in 2024, and at 8:00 p.m. on 9 October
> in 2025, running to 14 November that year.
> Source: JD's own corporate blog, 11 October 2024, and Chinese financial and
> trade press reporting the platform's announcements, 21 October 2023 and 28
> September 2025. Opening dates as announced by the platform.

> Douyin's ecommerce arm scheduled its 2025 Singles Day sale to open at
> midnight on 9 October and close at 11:59 p.m. on 11 November, inside a
> combined promotion period that began on 16 September.
> Source: Chinese ecommerce trade press reporting the platform's published
> schedule, 28 September 2025. Dates as scheduled by the platform.

| Platform and year | Opening | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tmall Singles Day 2023 | Presale 24 October, 8:00 p.m. | |
| Tmall Singles Day 2024 | Presale 14 October, 8:00 p.m. | Ten days earlier than 2023 |
| Tmall Singles Day 2025 | Presale 15 October, 8:00 p.m. | Rules public 24 September; spot sale 20 October |
| JD Singles Day 2023 | Sale 23 October, 8:00 p.m. | No presale |
| JD Singles Day 2024 | Sale 14 October, 8:00 p.m. | |
| JD Singles Day 2025 | Sale 9 October, 8:00 p.m. | Runs to 14 November |
| Douyin Singles Day 2025 | Sale 9 October, midnight | Promotion period from 16 September |

In 2025 JD and Douyin both opened on 9 October, six days before Tmall's presale. A
brand selling on all three is not producing to one date. It is producing to the
earliest one.

No platform has announced 2026 Singles Day dates as of 10 September 2026. In 2025
the Tmall rules came out on 24 September, so the 2026 dates may land within weeks of
this page's review date. Check before planning to them.

## What does the 618 cycle look like?

> Tmall opened its 618 presale on 26 May in 2023. In 2024 it dropped the
> official presale and opened the first sales wave at 8:00 p.m. on 20 May. In
> 2025 the presale opened at 8:00 p.m. on 13 May. In 2026 the event opened on
> 21 May, eight days later than the year before.
> Source: Chinese state-wire, municipal and financial press reporting the
> platform's announcements, 24 May 2023, 7 May 2024, 8 May 2025 and 29 April
> 2026. Opening dates as published in each year's rules or launch notice.

Eight days later in 2026. That is the second counterexample on this page to the idea
that the calendar only ever moves forward.

> Tmall published its 2025 618 rules and opened product registration on 8
> May, five days before the presale opened at 8:00 p.m. on 13 May.
> Source: Chinese financial press reporting the platform's rules, 8 and 9 May
> 2025. Two outlets; the day count is arithmetic on the reported dates.

Five days. On the 2025 618 cycle, a brand waiting for the rules before starting
production would have had less than a week.

> Tmall's 2026 618 rules ran a presale from 21 May to 3 June alongside two
> spot-sale waves, 21 to 30 May and 31 May to 21 June, and, as reported,
> required presale products to be live before the presale warm-up began and
> spot products to be live by 10:00 p.m. on the first day of sale.
> Source: Chinese ecommerce trade press reporting the platform's 2026
> merchant rules, 29 April 2026, with the dates restated in a technology news
> roundup on 2 May 2026. Rules as reported, not read from the platform's rule
> center.

That listing rule is the closest thing to a published asset deadline anywhere in this
research, and it applies to Tmall's 2026 618 cycle as reported. It should not be
assumed for JD or for Singles Day.

One conflict in the 2026 record needs stating rather than resolving by average.

> Consumer shopping guides in May 2026 printed a 12 or 13 May start for
> Tmall's 618. Reporting of the platform's own 2026 merchant rules gave 21 May.
> Source: Chinese consumer guides and trade press, April and May 2026. The
> conflict is published rather than resolved by average; the reported rules
> are preferred because they carry the full wave structure.

> JD ran a warm-up season from 13 to 29 May 2026, opened its 618 sale at 8:00
> p.m. on 30 May, and ran it to 21 June.
> Source: Chinese technology press reporting the platform's launch
> announcement, 18 May 2026. Dates as stated by the platform's marketing
> center at launch.

| Platform and year | Opening | Notes |

| Tmall 618 2023 | Presale 26 May | |
| Tmall 618 2024 | First sales wave 20 May, 8:00 p.m. | No official presale; registration from 6 May |
| Tmall 618 2025 | Presale 13 May, 8:00 p.m. | Rules public 8 May |

## How long before 618 should production start?

No source says, so this section is arithmetic, and it is labeled that way throughout.
Choose a lead time you trust from your own past cycles, and count it back from a
reported opening. The table uses sixteen weeks only because it is the brief's working
window, not because any source recommends it.

> Counted back sixteen weeks from Tmall's reported 21 May 2026 opening, the
> plan starts on 29 January 2026, and the nine-day Spring Festival holiday
> lands between roughly T minus 14 and T minus 12 weeks.
> Source: date arithmetic, 10 September 2026, on the platform's reported
> opening and the State Council holiday notice, not a published lead time.

> China's 2026 Spring Festival holiday ran from 15 to 23 February, nine days,
> with Saturday 14 February and Saturday 28 February worked as makeup days.
> The Dragon Boat Festival holiday runs 19 to 21 June and National Day 1 to 7
> October.
> Source: State Council General Office notice on the 2026 public holiday
> schedule, published 4 November 2025. Official government notice.

| Week | Date, counted back from Tmall 618 2026 | What falls there |
|---|---|---|
| T minus 16 | 29 January 2026 | Start of a sixteen-week plan |
| T minus 14 to T minus 12 | About 12 to 26 February 2026 | Spring Festival holiday, 15 to 23 February |
| T minus 3 | About 30 April 2026 | Rules reported 29 April |
| T minus 0 | 21 May 2026 | Reported opening |

That is the answer to what has to happen before Chinese New Year in a 618 cycle.
On a sixteen-week plan, the Spring Festival holiday falls in the plan's first month.
Anything that needs a physical shoot, a casting or a supplier in China has to be
booked before the holiday, or the plan loses nine days early and the makeup
Saturdays after it.

The same arithmetic works for Singles Day.

> Counted back sixteen weeks from Tmall's 15 October 2025 presale, the plan
> starts on 25 June 2025.
> Source: date arithmetic, 10 September 2026, on the reported presale opening,
> not a published lead time.

On a sixteen-week plan for Singles Day, the start falls in late June, days after the
618 cycle ends. For brands running both, the two plans overlap, and the 618 wrap-up
and the Singles Day kickoff compete for the same production capacity.

## What happens if creative approval slips?

No source measures what a late approval or a late reshoot costs, so this page puts no
figure on either. The mechanism is worth stating, because both failures run into the
same wall.

**Late approval.** On Tmall's 2026 618 rules as reported, presale products had to be
live before the warm-up began. An approval that slips past that point does not delay
the listing. It removes it from the presale.

**Late reshoot.** A reshoot needs the product, the set and the people again. On a
sixteen-week plan for 618, the Spring Festival holiday sits early in the window, so a
reshoot discovered after it has less calendar left than the plan's length suggests.

Both are reasons to lock creative earlier than the rules require, and to treat the
rules' publication date as confirmation rather than as the starting gun.

## Questions about Singles Day and 618 timing

**When do Singles Day assets have to be locked?**

Before each platform's opening. For 2025, JD and Douyin opened on 9 October and
Tmall's presale opened at 8:00 p.m. on 15 October, with Tmall's rules published only
21 days earlier. No platform publishes a separate asset deadline for Singles Day, and
no 2026 dates were announced as of 10 September 2026.

**How long before 618 should production start?**

No platform, government notice or survey publishes a lead time. Counting back
sixteen weeks from Tmall's reported 21 May 2026 opening gives a start of 29 January
2026, with the Spring Festival holiday falling in the plan's first month. That is date
arithmetic on reported dates, not a recommendation from any source.

**What are the Tmall pre-sale milestones?**

For Singles Day 2025: a warm-up at 2:00 p.m. on 15 October, deposits from 8:00 p.m.
that evening to 5:59 p.m. on 20 October, and the spot sale from 8:00 p.m. on 20
October to 14 November. For 618 2026 the reported rules ran a presale from 21 May to
3 June alongside two spot-sale waves.

**How does Chinese New Year affect a 618 cycle?**

The 2026 Spring Festival holiday ran from 15 to 23 February, with two Saturdays worked
as makeup days. On a sixteen-week plan counted back from Tmall's reported 21 May
opening, that holiday falls about twelve to fourteen weeks out, so shoots, casting and
suppliers in China need booking before it.

**What happens if creative approval slips?**

No source measures the cost, so none is given here. On Tmall's 2026 618 rules as
reported, presale products had to be live before the warm-up began, so an approval
that slips past that point takes the listing out of the presale rather than simply
delaying it.
