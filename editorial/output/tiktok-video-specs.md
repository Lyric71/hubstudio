# TikTok video specs and safe zones for 2026

TikTok prints its safe zone. Not in the text of any help page, but on the
template files those pages hand out, in pixels, and almost nobody quotes them.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

## What are TikTok's video specs in 2026?

TikTok video specs for 2026, from TikTok's own pages: record up to 10
minutes in the app, upload up to 60. Auction in-feed ads take a vertical 9:16
video at 540 by 960 pixels or more, up to 10 minutes and 500 MB. hubStudio
publishes one clip of 3 seconds to 10 minutes to TikTok, a feature still in
Beta.

Reviewed October 8, 2026. Every number below comes from TikTok's Help Center,
its Ads Manager help pages, or the template files those pages link, read that
day. The consumer help center prints no update date, so its rows carry the
read date. The ads pages print a month, and each row shows it.

One absence matters before the tables. TikTok's help center publishes no
aspect ratio, resolution, file size, format or bitrate for an ordinary post.
Size guides that print one are quoting another source.

Most guides print organic file caps by device, a web upload ceiling, or a
minimum pixel count. None of those figures appears on a TikTok help page we
read. The web uploader itself, TikTok Studio, sits behind a login.

## Organic post against in-feed ad: what changes

| Rule | Organic post | In-feed ad |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 10 minutes recorded, 60 uploaded | Auction up to 10 minutes; reservation 5 to 60 seconds |
| Shape and size | Not published | 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1, at 540x960, 960x540 or 640x640 or more |
| Caption | Your own words | White, one font; auction captions take no links, @ or hashtags |
| Sound | A sound picked first caps the length | Clear audio at review; reservation ads must have sound |
| Safe zone | Not published | Template files that shrink as the caption grows |

Reservation ads add their own rules on the same page: no watermarks,
including TikTok's own, nothing that mimics the TikTok interface, and no
white or transparent backgrounds, because the app's names, captions and icons
are white. Captions show four lines before "See more." TikTok suggests staying
within 100 characters, or 50 in Chinese, Japanese or Korean.

> Reservation in-feed ads and TopView run 5 to 60 seconds, with 9 to 15
> recommended, at 2,500 kbps or more, and every reservation creative must
> carry sound. TopView bans plain white in the first 3 seconds because the
> TikTok logo disappears against it.
> Source: TikTok Ads Manager help, reservation in-feed specifications (July
> 2025) and TopView specifications (June 2026), read October 8, 2026.

## The TikTok safe zone, measured from TikTok's own templates

TikTok's spec pages describe the safe zone in words. Its size depends on the
shape of the video, the caption length and any add-ons, and "the longer the
caption, the smaller the safe zone will be." The numbers live in the zip files
the auction in-feed page links, and TikTok prints them on the templates.

| Template, on 1080x1920 | Top | Bottom | Left | Right |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| In-feed standard | 240 | 660 | 120 | 120, then 300 below y 840 |
| With anchor, 1 caption line | 252 | 812 | 120 | 120, then 240 below y 360 |
| With anchor, 2 lines | 252 | 878 | 120 | 120, then 240 below y 360 |
| With anchor, 3 lines | 252 | 946 | 120 | 120, then 240 below y 360 |
| With anchor, 4 lines | 252 | 1014 | 120 | 120, then 240 below y 360 |

> TikTok's in-feed standard template, a 720 by 1280 frame, shades 160 pixels
> at the top, 440 at the bottom and a column 120 pixels wide on the right from
> 560 pixels down, plus an 80-pixel strip down each side.
> Source: TikTok's In-Feed Standard LTR template, downloaded from the auction
> in-feed spec page (June 2026) on October 8, 2026; dimension labels in the
> file, shaded areas checked pixel by pixel.

> TikTok's anchor templates, on a 540 by 960 frame, keep 126 pixels at the top
> and 60 at each side, then give the bottom 406, 439, 473 or 507 pixels as the
> caption grows from one line to four.
> Source: TikTok's In-Feed with Anchor LTR templates, the same page, October 8,
> 2026; dimension labels printed in the four vertical files.

The table scales both files to 1080 by 1920. That step is our arithmetic, a
straight multiplication, not a TikTok figure. The standard file shades its
side strips in a different color from the top, bottom and button column, and
carries no legend, so we treat every shaded area as off limits for text and
logos. In the anchor files, the anchor is drawn as a link card just above the
username. The Arabic files mirror the layout, button column on the left.

Put the two families together and one box clears every vertical template,
whatever the caption length: 120 to 780 pixels across, 252 to 906 down. That
box is derived from TikTok's files, not published by TikTok.

TopView adds a second stage. The first 3 seconds play full screen, then the ad
settles into the feed, and TikTok's page says the in-feed safe zone is the
stricter of the two. It also says text left in the areas its preview tool
marks red "should be rejected."

There is no organic safe zone on any TikTok page. The ad templates are the
only map TikTok itself draws, and they show the same furniture an ordinary
post carries: the username, the caption, the sound line and the button column.

## Why a TikTok upload fails, in TikTok's own words

TikTok's help pages list only a handful of causes. These are the ones it
states.

- **The sound cut the clip.** Pick a sound before you record or upload, and
  the video's length becomes the sound's length.
- **The web upload took the whole file.** A browser upload sends the entire
  video. To trim it, TikTok says to upload in the app.
- **The app stalled.** TikTok's fix list: switch between Wi-Fi and mobile
  data, clear the app's video cache, restart the app and the phone, and update
  to the latest version. A new post missing from your profile may be set to
  private.
- **The browser stalled.** Refresh the page, then restart the browser.
- **Realistic AI content went out unlabeled.** TikTok requires the label and
  may remove content without it. Files that carry Content Credentials can be
  labeled automatically, and that label can't be taken off.

The ads side is stricter. The review checklist, last updated February 2025,
requires clear audio in video ads and rules out prompts for actions the app
doesn't support, such as swiping up or a mouse cursor. Media has to meet the
specs. The creative has to match the landing page. The reservation notes ask
for video that isn't stretched or compressed, and TopView rules out QR codes,
phone numbers and addresses.

## FAQ

### What size should a TikTok video be?

TikTok's ads help asks for a vertical 9:16 video at 540 by 960 pixels or more,
and its creative best practices recommend at least 720p. For ordinary posts,
TikTok's help center prints no size at all. A 9:16 file that clears the ad
floors, with text inside the template safe zone, is the safe build for both.

### How long can a TikTok video be in 2026?

Up to 10 minutes when you record in the TikTok app and up to 60 minutes when
you upload, per TikTok's help center on October 8, 2026. A sound picked first
caps the length at the sound's length. Auction in-feed ads run up to 10
minutes, while reservation in-feed ads and TopView run 5 to 60 seconds.

### What is the TikTok safe zone in pixels?

On TikTok's standard in-feed template, scaled to 1080 by 1920, keep text out
of the top 240 pixels, the bottom 660 and 120 on each side, plus a 300-pixel
right margin below the 840 mark. Anchor templates push the bottom up to 1,014
pixels with a four-line caption.

### What are the TikTok in-feed ad specs?

Auction in-feed ads take 9:16 at 540 by 960 or more, 16:9 at 960 by 540 or
1:1 at 640 by 640, in MP4, MOV, MPEG, 3GP or AVI. Files run up to 10 minutes,
500 MB and at least 516 kbps. Reservation ads run 5 to 60 seconds at 2,500
kbps or more, with sound.

### How many photos can a TikTok post have?

Up to 35, per TikTok's help page on making a post. One editing session takes
up to 35 photos and videos combined, up to 8 overlays and one sound. Carousel
ads take 2 to 35 images in JPG or PNG at 720 by 1280, 640 by 640 or 1200 by
628, with music required.

### Why won't my TikTok video upload?

TikTok's own fixes: switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, clear the app's
video cache, restart the app and the phone, and update the app. On a computer,
refresh the page and restart the browser. A clip that came out shorter than
planned may have been capped by the sound you picked first.

### Can hubStudio post to TikTok for me?

Yes, in Beta. Connect your TikTok account in My Connections, then publish a
clip of 3 seconds to 10 minutes now or on a schedule, with the caption, the
privacy setting and the AI-generated label set in the app. Publishing costs
nothing. Renders are charged to your prepaid balance at the price shown first.
