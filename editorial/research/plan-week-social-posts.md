## R1. What has to be true

Mapped from the brief before anything was looked up. Every claim below is
either a hubStudio app fact (help center or `hubstudio-positioning.md`, the
R5 first-party exception: the site already publishes it) or a network limit
(the network's own help, business or developer page).

- The four blocks of the afternoon map onto real app surfaces: Campaigns,
  the network modules (brief, copy, pictures, publishing), Re-purpose for
  other networks, Send for validation, Schedule.
- A campaign can feed a post draft, and what that draft reads.
- Where the price shows, and what is free.
- Which networks hubStudio publishes to, which takes what (pictures, video),
  and YouTube's by-hand route.
- Scheduling mechanics: chips, time zone, horizon, accounts per post, queue
  interval, retries.
- Validation locks a post until the validator decides; clients can approve.
- Network caption limits: LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook.
- Network daily caps: Instagram API, TikTok Direct Post.
- Any network-owned posting-frequency or best-time figure (brief: none
  unless a network page states it, dated).

## Cleared for use

> Instagram's developer guide allows 100 API-published posts in a 24-hour
> moving period, a carousel counting as one. The same guide's carousel
> section says 50, and the quota endpoint reports a total of 50.
> Source: Instagram Platform developer documentation, Content Publishing
> guide and content publishing limit reference, read October 11, 2026, the
> platform's own API pages.

> TikTok caps posting through apps at "typically around 15 posts per day"
> per creator account, shared across every app that posts for it.
> Source: TikTok for Developers, Content Sharing Guidelines, updated August
> 4, 2026, the platform's own developer rules.

> Instagram captions take up to 2,200 characters, 30 hashtags and 20 @ tags;
> TikTok video captions up to 2,200; LinkedIn posts up to 3,000; X posts up to
> 280, with emoji counting as two.
> Source: Instagram and TikTok developer references, LinkedIn Help and X's
> developer documentation, read October 11, 2026, each network's own page.

> Meta's planner suggests posting times from when your followers were most
> active on Facebook and Instagram over the past 7 days.
> Source: Meta Business Help Center, creating posts in Meta Business Suite,
> read October 11, 2026, a description of the network's own tool.

> "You don't need to post daily, but creating a content schedule and sticking
> to it will help you build momentum."
> Source: TikTok for Business, creative strategy for small businesses,
> updated September 2026, the network's own guidance, no figure attached.
