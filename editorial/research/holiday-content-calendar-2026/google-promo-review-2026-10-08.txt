Title: Promotion status FAQs - Google Merchant Center Help
Fetched: 2026-10-08 (curl, raw HTML, tags stripped; the headless render returned the FAQ index only)

Promotion reviews are typically completed within 12 hours of the promotion effective start time (the time that the promotion becomes active on your site), but may take up to 24 hours. 
Tip : We recommend that you submit promotions at least 24 hours before the effective start time to allow a smooth and timely review process. Otherwise, the promotion may be delayed by 24 hours. 
Submit promotions during business hours from Monday through Friday. After you submit a promotion, processing, review, and validation can take up to 24 hours but typically is completed within 12 hours. It may also take a few hours for your promotion to go live after it’s fully approved or quit showing in ads and free listings after you’ve stopped it.
