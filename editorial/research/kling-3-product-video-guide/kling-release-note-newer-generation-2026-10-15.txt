Meet All-New Kling 4.0!

Dear Kling AI Creators,

The all-new Kling 4.0 officially became available to everyone on October 15.

Realism, control, and professional quality are at the heart of this upgrade.

Built for frequent creation, Kling 4.0 Flash combines fast generation with reliable generation quality, helping you turn ideas into videos with ease.

2）Omni Reference for Greater Creative Control

● Richer Reference Options

● Multimodal References: Use up to 15 reference assets in a single generation, combining images, videos, and elements (including a specified voice for an element). You can include up to 10 images, 5 videos (up to 30 seconds in total, including video elements), and 7 elements (including elements defined by multiple images or videos).

● Image References: Use element images, three-view references, image grids, infographics, keyframes, wireframes, and more to define characters, scene layouts, visual styles, text and graphic elements, shot composition, and key story beats. This makes it easier to communicate your creative intent clearly and precisely.

● Video References: Use video inputs to guide character performances, actions, camera movements, and narrative pacing. White-model and depth-map videos are also supported, giving you more precise control over motion, camera movement, and timing.

All-new Creation Interface

A Simpler, More Efficient Way to Create Videos

We’ve redesigned Kling’s creation page to make the creative process clearer and the workflow more efficient.

● An All-in-One Creation Experience

One input box for every idea.

We’ve brought the input options previously spread across the toolbar into a single box. Combine images, videos, and audio as references in one place, and bring your ideas together in a single creative workflow.

● Switch Between Grid and List Views

Browse quickly in a grid. Focus on each creation in a list.

The creation interface now lets you switch between grid and list views. Grid view makes it easy to browse and compare multiple creations at a glance, while list view lets you review and edit them one by one.

● Continuous Timeline Creation

Preview and refine as you go, without breaking your flow.

Preview, edit, and add assets on a single timeline. Make changes or insert new material at any point without leaving your workflow or starting over, so you can keep creating as your ideas evolve.

● A New Canvas and Agent Experience

Connect every step on the canvas, with Agent to help you create.

The new canvas lets you create nodes, connect content, and arrange your workspace freely. It brings your assets, creative process, and generated results into one view, making even complex workflows easier to follow. You can also tell the Agent what you want to create. It can understand your request, use tools, and carry out tasks to help move your project forward.

Kling 4.0 Now Available via MCP / CLI

MCP and CLI now support Kling 4.0, including Omni Reference and video generation with keyframes. From marketing asset creation and short-form video production at scale to storyboard previsualization and character consistency, users can leverage Agents to automate Kling 4.0 workflows for more flexible, controllable video generation.
