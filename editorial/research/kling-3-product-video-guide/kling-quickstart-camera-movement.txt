Camera Movement
Add cinematic motion to your AI videos. Learn how to use all 6 camera controls (zoom, pan, tilt, roll) and 4 "Master Shots" to direct your scene with precision.
Kling AI
Nov 24, 2025
1 min read
The camera movement control now supports six basic camera movements, including 'horizontal, vertical, zoom, pan, tilt, roll', as well as four Master Shots: 'move left and zoom in, move right and zoom in, move forward and zoom up, move down and zoom out'.
Camera movement is a form of cinematic language. To meet the diversity of video creation and to allow the model to better respond to the users' control over the camera, the platform has added Camera Movement function. The function allow us to control the camera movement in the video frame with absolute commands, and select the extent of the camera movement by adjusting the displacement parameters.
