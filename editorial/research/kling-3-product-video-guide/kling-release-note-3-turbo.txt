Kling 3.0 Model Series Upgrade

Kling 3.0 Turbo is now live, along with upgraded video editing in Kling 3.0 Omni.

Dear Kling AI creators,

We’re excited to announce a series of updates to the Kling 3.0 model series!

Kling 3.0 Turbo is now available, offering creators a more efficient creating option while maintaining high-quality video results. Meanwhile, Kling 3.0 Omni brings improved video editing capabilities, now supporting longer durations and 4K resolution across both input videos and generated results.

1）Kling 3.0 Turbo is now available: outstanding generation quality and faster speed, striking the right balance between efficiency and quality.

Outstanding Generation Quality: Kling 3.0 Turbo maintains consistently strong performance in visual quality, motion dynamics, and prompt adherence. It accurately follows multi-shot storyboard logic, ensuring smooth continuity and a unified visual style across scenes.

Precise Audio-Visual Synchronization: Kling 3.0 Turbo delivers highly accurate alignment between audio and visuals, with smooth and natural lip sync that accurately matches the speech. It maintains stable synchronization even in longer videos, making it well suited for talking-head content, voice-driven storytelling, and other character-based formats.

Faster Generation: While preserving core model capabilities, Kling 3.0 Turbo delivers faster generation, reducing the overall cost of scaled creation and giving creators a more efficient option.

2）Kling 3.0 Omni Capability Upgrades: Kling 3.0 Omni brings comprehensive upgrades to video editing, with stronger consistency across outputs. Video editing now support input and output durations of up to 15 seconds. Video editing now also supports 4K input and output, enabling longer, clearer, and more professional creative workflows.

Improved Consistency: In video editing, the model now has a significantly stronger understanding of input videos and images. Editing results stay more faithful to the source material, while creative instructions are executed with greater precision.

Extended 11–15s video editing: Now supports video inputs ranging from 3–15s for editing, with outputs up to 15s, covering a more complete duration range.

4K input and output support: Video editing now supports both 4K input and 4K output, delivering higher visual clarity and finer details, better suited for large-screen display, HD devices, and professional production scenarios.
