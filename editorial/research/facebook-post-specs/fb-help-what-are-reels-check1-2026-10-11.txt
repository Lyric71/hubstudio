We’re gradually updating people to Meta Accounts. You may see either Accounts Center or Meta Account settings as the place to manage your account settings.
Update (September 2025): All videos on Facebook are now Reels. All videos posted to Facebook will be shared as reels, regardless of length or orientation. This unifies the video experience for creators and viewers. Learn more about this change.
Reels on Facebook is a video format complete with music, audio, AR effects, and other options. You can also view public reels from Instagram, if the creator chooses to recommend it on Facebook. Reels are recommended based on what may be relevant to you and may appear in places like your Feed and in Video.
Learn more about Reels and how to create a reel.
Differences between reels on Facebook and Instagram
Instagram names: Reels from Instagram have the creator's Instagram username instead of their Facebook name (if they have a Facebook profile). You’ll also see  Instagram icon next to their username.
Comments: You can view comments on reels created on both Facebook and Instagram. Leaving comments is different depending on where the reel was created.
Facebook Reels: If you’re watching a reel created on Facebook, you can leave comments and reply or react to comments.
Instagram Reels: If you’re watching a reel created on Instagram, you can leave a comment and reply or like comments if you have added your Instagram profile to your Meta Account. Your comment, reply or like will be posted with your Instagram profile.
Likes: You can see how many people have liked a reel. You can also see who on Facebook has liked a reel, but if it’s a reel created on Instagram, you can’t see who on Instagram liked it. You can only see who liked the reel on Instagram in the Instagram app or on Instagram.com.
To see a list of who on Facebook liked the video, use the Facebook app for iPhone and Android.
Views: The number of times your reel was played. A view is 1 ms of playtime.
Note: Videos published before 3/14/2024 define a view as 3 seconds of playtime. Reels use 1 ms regardless of when they were published. Learn more about how to view the number of times a reel or video has been played.
Creator Page: When you’re watching a reel created on Facebook, you can see other reels they’ve created by tapping on their name. If you’re watching a reel created on Instagram, you will be directed to their profile on Instagram instead.
Important information
When commenting on an Instagram reel with your Instagram profile, your comment will be visible on both Facebook and Instagram.
You can change the Instagram profile you’re using to comment on Instagram reels by visiting the Meta Account settings.
You can find comments you’ve left on Instagram reels in Your Activity on Instagram. They won’t appear on your Facebook Activity Log.
Comments left on Instagram reels can only be deleted on Instagram. If you delete the comment on Instagram, it’ll be removed from both Instagram and Facebook. Learn more about how to delete comments on Instagram.
