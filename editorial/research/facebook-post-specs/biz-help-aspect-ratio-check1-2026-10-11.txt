Best practices for aspect ratios

1.91:1: Horizontal
16:9: Horizontal
1:1: Square
4:5: Vertical
9:16: Vertical

When you create ads for mobile, images and videos should cover as much screen as possible to grab attention. Choose an aspect ratio that best suits different ad placements and formats (see what the placements look like) and remember to check which ad placements support which aspect ratios.

Note: Remember to check the Ads Guide for the correct aspect ratio if there is a specific ad format you want to use or ad placement you want your ad to be delivered.

Below are some recommended aspect ratios for different placements and formats:

For Feed placements: Both 1:1 and 4:5 aspect ratios are supported for both images and videos.
Note: Vertical 4:5 is recommended for single-image ads to be delivered to the ad placement Facebook Feed. 1:1 is recommended for single-image ads to be delivered to Instagram Feed.
For Stories, Status and Reels placements: Most people hold their phones vertically so 9:16 is recommended to capture the whole screen.
For the carousel format: 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratios are recommended for images. It's best to keep the aspect ratios of your product images consistent, such as all 1:1 or 4:5, to prevent automated adjustments. Square 1:1 and consistent ratios is recommended for all videos in a carousel.
Note: The 4:5 aspect ratio is only available for Advantage+ catalog ads that are created using the carousel format and with product images from your catalog. Learn more about the product image specification for catalogs and the supported aspect ratios.
For in-stream video: Horizontal 16:9 is recommended to fit the videos the ads are in.
Meta Audience Network placements: Vertical 9:16 is recommended.
Best practices for media customization
Note: This feature may not be available to you at this time.

Meta Ads Manager allows you to upload up to 10 images or videos for a single ad. Follow these best practices to ensure you upload media assets with aspect ratios that are best suited to placements you want to deliver to. Learn more about media customization.

When you create an ad in Ads Manager, your media is automatically adjusted to fit more placements. If you'd like more precise control over how your creative appears, you can upload assets in specific aspect ratios. For example, if Stories is an important placement for your campaign, upload a 9:16 asset or create one from an existing asset.
Review auto-generated crops before publishing to make sure important content isn't cut off. Learn more about the safe zone for ads on Facebook and Instagram.
If you want a specific asset to deliver only to certain placements, use placement opt-outs in addition to cropping. Cropping alone won't restrict delivery.
How aspect ratios and adjustments work

When you create or edit an ad, you can use the aspect ratios view to review and manage every aspect-ratio variation your ad needs  -  square, vertical, portrait or horizontal  -  in one place. This view lets you preview each variation, opt in or out and make adjustments before publishing.

If you don't provide all four aspect ratio variations, the system helps fill the gaps using adjustment options such as padding, media expansion, cropping and additional AI-enabled features. Learn more about these options and how to customize your ad creative.
