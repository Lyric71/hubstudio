Change your Page's settings to publish short videos as a reel or a post on Facebook
Copy link
More
Computer Help
When you publish a video that is up to 90 seconds long on your Page, it can be published as a reel or as a post. You can choose how to share short videos in your page’s settings.
If you use Facebook with multiple devices, your setting will be specific to each type of device you use. Example: If you change your setting on www.facebook.com using a computer, you can use a different setting on your mobile device.
You can choose to always publish as a reel, always publish as a post, or to have the Facebook app ask each time.
Note: Sharing a short video as a reel or a post isn’t available for the classic Pages experience.
Change your Page's short video publish settings:
While using Facebook with your Page’s profile, click your Page’s profile picture in the top right.
Click Settings & privacy, then click Settings.
Click Media.
Next to Create reels with videos less than 60 seconds, click the dropdown box then click your selection.
Learn how to change your short video publishing settings for your professional profile on the Meta Business Help Center.
