Update (September 2025): Facebook has unified all video uploads under the Reels format. All videos posted to Facebook will be shared as reels, regardless of length or orientation. This unifies the video experience for creators and viewers. Learn more about this change.
Privacy and sharing
All videos you post on Facebook (except Stories and Live) are now Reels and follow your selected privacy settings. If your account or a specific reel is set to private (or non-public), only your chosen audience (such as your friends or followers) can view your content. People who do not follow you will not be able to see your Reels, use original audio from your content, or share your Reels with others who are not in your audience.
Original audio from your private or non-public Reels cannot be reused by others.
Your Reels cannot be shared with people outside your selected audience.
If you change a Reel from public to private, any content that reused your original audio will be muted for users who are not in your audience.
