Anti-trust/Agency In-house language for Bids:
"[Production/Post production Company] submits this bid with the understanding that the
Agency's solicitation of this and other bids in connection with this project is in compliance
with all applicable laws. Unless notified otherwise in writing by the Agency prior to bid
submission, [Production/Post production Company] submits this bid with the
understanding that to ensure a fair and non-conflicted bidding process, no entity in the
bidding pool or that is a potential candidate to produce the project, directly or indirectly, is
a parent, subsidiary, division, affiliate or sibling of the Agency or Advertiser (each, an "In-
House Entity"). If notice of the participation in the bidding pool or consideration process
of an In-House Entity, is not given to [Production/Post production Company] prior to
submission of its bidding package, including treatment, and if the job is then awarded to
the In-House Entity, [Production/Post production Company] shall be reimbursed for its
bidding costs provided that reimbursement does not confer a license on agency/advertiser
or transfer any IP rights that [Production/Post production Company] has in the bidding
package."

Tariff language for bids:
When shooting outside the U.S.:
"Production company shall not be liable for the cost or payment of any tariffs whether now
in effect or imposed in the future on any portion or all of the production or deliverables."
When shooting in the U.S.:
"Production company shall not be responsible for price increases incurred after the award
of the bid due to tariffs, and agency shall approve an overage(s) for such additional costs."
