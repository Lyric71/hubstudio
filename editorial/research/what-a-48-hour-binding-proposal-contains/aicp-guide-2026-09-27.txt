                 AICP Bidding Reference Guide

          One of AICP's chief functions is to help ensure that in the fast-paced environment of this industry, the member
production/post companies can provide services in a straightforward manner for the agencies/advertisers that engage
them. These questions help ensure that all parties are well informed at the outset, and touch on basic areas of
information which all parties should be knowledgeable from the start.

          Time is money and preparedness leads to efficiency. The goal of this document is to provide a roadmap to make
sure that all parties are sharing the information necessary to achieve the best creative product in the most efficient way.
To this end, these questions have been fleshed out as much as possible to most efficiently engage, communicate and
manage the production and postproduction processes.

                                       Initial Questions & Considerations ­
                             First Call with Agency Producer or Brand Contact

     · Has the creative and/or campaign been sold through to the decision makers at the advertiser?
                o Has it gone through testing, or will it go through testing?

     · Has the director/artist been approved to bid on the project by the advertiser?

     · What is the proposed budget for this project or campaign? Is it "all in"?
                o Does that include social elements, stills, BTS, etc.?
                o Does that include post, talent, VFX, music, finish, etc.

     · Is this project a single bid or multi-bid?
                o If multi-bid ­ i.e., triple bid:
                      Who are the directors/companies bidding the project?
                      Are any of the directors/companies a preferred or rostered vendor?
                      Are any of the directors/companies In-House Entities? If so, what is the plan to keep the
                         independent production/post company's bid confidential (i.e., not shared with any competitors,
                         including an In-House Entity)?
                      Are any of the other bidders foreign based companies?
                      Are the other bidders union signatories (DGA/IATSE/Teamsters)?
                      Are any of the directors/companies in the bidding pool the incumbent on this advertising
                         campaign?

     · Has a Mutual NDA been provided for this project? (if not, the production/post company should ask for one to
         protect its IP)

     · Are there bidding specs/talent specs from the agency or advertiser?

     · Does the advertiser have production, post, or specialized guidelines (such as green guidelines)?

     · Is the talent SAG or non-union?

     · Is there a proposed timeline or calendar for this project? Are there any elements that would cause this project to
         shift in dates (e.g. celebrity availability)?

     · When is this expected to air?

     · Has the agency/advertiser had any calls with directors/editors or their company representatives yet? (Is the
         production/post company coming in late to the bidding pool, or concurrently with the other bidders?)

· Are there any print/stills/behind the scenes/or social elements to this project? If so, are there separate creative

· When would the project be fully awarded? Or when will the initial start-up be awarded?

· May the production/post company use the finished spot on its reel/socials with full credit?

· Will this job be covered by wrap-up insurance or should production bid for full insurance? (If working with a
    foreign production service company, the local production company should be notified of any insurance
    requirements or restrictions with agency/advertiser wrap-up insurance.)

· If the project is bid through delivery of the spot (instead of footage delivered to editorial), has the proper post
    insurance been put into place and/or bid for?

· Are helicopters, airplanes or drones required for filming? If so, proper insurance coverage should be provided by
    the agency/advertiser or included in the production insurance package.

· Is there celebrity travel? If so, who is paying and arranging for the celebrity travel? (There can be additional
    liability issues with arranging and/or paying a celebrity's travel on a project. See AICP newsletter article on
    talent travel.)

· Is a second unit needed for any elements, due to either time limitations (such as with a celebrity), or for
    print/stills/BTS/social elements?

· If animals are involved in a project, are there any animal monitoring requirements from the agency or advertiser?
    (or associations such as American Humane Society or MAP?)

· Are any product specialists needed for the project (e.g., "pour specialist")?

· Have any conversations taken place with prospective editorial, VFX, or other post elements? If so, please provide
    the contact information so that the production company's bidders and/or directors may speak to them.

· Is the agency/advertiser open to editorial, VFX or other post suggestions from the director?

· Are costs for any stock footage or licensed materials included in the budget, or needed?

                                              Tech and Deliverables

· What are the deliverables? Do the deliverables align with the amount of creative needed to be filmed, or the length
    of the creative? (e.g., the deliverable is :15, but the creative is written for a :60 or :90)

· On what format will the project be shot? (i.e., HD or film). If a look is being specified, such as film, this could
    increase the postproduction costs (e.g., film scanning).

· What is the shoot aspect ratio? 16:9, 4:3, 1.35:1, 2.35:1 (anamorphic)? Will all the deliverables have the same
    aspect ratio or format, or will it vary (such as a "vertical" format for social elements)?
