## Method statement, printed before anything else

Nothing in this file was read from an Ocean Engine advertiser backend. Every
number below is a **modal published value**, not a platform reading. For each
spec, every published figure found was recorded, the figures were counted by
independent domain, and the value published is the one that appears most often,
with the count shown and the spread kept where it is material.

**What this means for the page.** The platform's own reachable surfaces carry
either no spec text or a spec sheet that expired on 2020-12-31 by its own terms.
The 2020 document counts once in the modes below, as one domain, and it is
flagged by age everywhere it decides a value. It must never be described as the
current official spec.

### The collection

Seventeen sources carry at least one Douyin paid-ad spec value. Sixteen are
Chinese-language: nine read in full on 2026-09-10, four read through search
extracts tied to a domain, and three search extracts that could not be tied to a
single domain and are flagged wherever they touch a mode. One is an
English-language search extract that restates the 2020 Douyin sheet. A further
TikTok help page was read in full and is **excluded from every Douyin count**.
Six more pages were classified and carried no spec value.

### Four cautions that apply to every row

1. **Specs split by page age, not by disagreement.** The pattern brief 04 found
   holds here and is stronger. Open-screen image caps, open-screen canvas sizes,
   in-feed minimum resolution, in-feed duration and in-feed file size all change
   between 2018, 2020, 2023 and 2024 pages. Rows are split by age wherever that
   happens.
2. **TikTok contamination is live in the Chinese SERP.** The top Chinese result
   for a Douyin TopView spec query is TikTok's own TopView help page, in
   Chinese, updated June 2026. A search engine summary of that result set merged
   a TikTok-only rule into a Douyin answer.
3. **Cross-app contamination inside ByteDance.** Toutiao's open-screen safe zone
   and title limits (from the sibling 2020 sheet) and what looks like the
   off-app ad network's splash spec surface on Douyin queries. Neither is Douyin.
4. **English guides misattribute the 2020 sheet.** The seven-size video file set
   English pages print as "TopView sizes" is the 5-second open-screen video set
   in the 2020 Douyin sheet. No Chinese source ties it to TopView.

### What brief 05 owns

Organic post video specs (upload resolution, organic duration and file caps,
organic caption and title limits, organic safe zones) are brief 05. Where a paid
row coincides with an organic spec, this file notes it and stops. The 60
character "Douyin title limit" that surfaced on title queries is an organic post
figure and is left to brief 05.

## The bar

The Chinese ranking pages run 300 to 1,500 words and carry one table or none.
The English ranking pages run 1,500 to 3,000 words about formats and costs and
carry dimensions in one or two places, as a pasted list. Not one page in any of
the twenty-one result sets states how many sources a number came from, dates
its specs, separates Douyin from TikTok, or separates open screen from TopView
at the file level. None carries a rejection table.

The bar to beat is 1,800 words with one spec table per format carrying source
counts, an age flag on every figure that moved, a plain TikTok-is-not-Douyin
note, a rejection table, and a visible reviewed date. Two tables clears the
field. Per-format tables with counts win it, because nobody else shows the
working or the vintage.

**H1 consequence.** The gap is not another number. It is that the numbers in
circulation are old, misattributed between formats, or TikTok's, and the page
that says which is which is the one worth ranking.

## R1. What has to be true before looking anything up

Listed before searching. Each is resolved in the tables below.

1. Ocean Engine publishes creative specs per placement, and they are reachable
   or their absence is reportable.
2. Open screen has a stated display time per creative type.
3. Open screen has stated canvas sizes, a file weight cap and formats.
4. Open screen has a safe zone for the skip button and logo.
5. TopView has a stated video duration window and a stated full-screen phase.
6. TopView has its own resolution, file size and format limits, distinct from
   open screen and from in-feed.
7. In-feed video has a stated ratio, resolution floor, duration window, file cap
   and format list.
8. In-feed ads have a title character limit, read from the ad backend.
9. Search ads have creative specs distinct from in-feed.
10. The self-serve ecommerce tool, Qianchuan, has its own creative specs.
11. Published Douyin figures are not TikTok figures relabeled.
12. The figures in circulation are current, or their age can be shown.
13. Rejection reasons are published with the trigger that sets them.
14. Each format has a production implication a brand can act on.

Items 1 and 8 resolve as absences at primary level. Items 6 and 9 resolve as
**not published**. Item 11 resolves as **false in part**, and item 12 as
**false for most rows**, which together are the article. Items 2 to 5, 7, 10
and 13 resolve as modal, never primary. Item 14 is reasoning from modal values
and must be written as reasoning.

domains, and "2020 sheet" means the fourth-quarter 2020 Douyin rate card that

> TopView runs a video of 10 to 60 seconds. The first 3 seconds play full
> screen with nothing on top, a conversion component fades in after that, and
> from the fourth second the ad sits as the first post in the feed. Four of five
> Douyin-specific sources give the 10 to 60 second window. The fifth gives 5 to
> 60, which is TikTok's published range.
> Source: 5 Douyin-specific published sources, Chinese-language, pages dated 2019
> to 2021 and 2025, collected 10 September 2026. Modal value, not a primary
> reading.

**4. The TikTok problem.**

> Search in Chinese for Douyin's TopView specs and the top result is TikTok's
> own TopView help page. Its numbers are TikTok's: a 540 by 960 pixel floor, a
> 500MB cap, a 100-character title. It never mentions Douyin, and one of its
> rules, no pure white in the first 3 seconds, exists because of where TikTok
> puts its logo.
> Source: one Chinese-language query run 10 September 2026, the result read in
> full, page updated June 2026. An observation about the search results, not a
> Douyin spec.

**5. The sizes English guides get wrong.**

> The seven pixel sizes English guides list as TopView sizes, running from 480 by
> 800 to 1080 by 2340, are the video file set for the 5-second open screen in a
> Douyin rate card from the fourth quarter of 2020. No Chinese-language source
> attaches them to TopView. No Douyin-specific source publishes a TopView file
> size, resolution or bitrate at all.
> Source: the 2020 rate card read in full and compared with English search
> extracts on 10 September 2026, against 16 Chinese-language sources. An
> observation about provenance, not a primary reading.

**6. In-feed video, split by age.**

> For in-feed video, the sources that agree are the recent ones. Pages dated 2023
> and 2024 put the vertical floor at 720 by 1280 in 9:16. Pages from 2018 to
> 2021 say 540 by 960. Duration moved the same way: 5 to 30 seconds in 2018, 5
> to 60 on four pages dated 2019 to 2023, and 4 to 300 seconds on the only page
> dated 2024.
> Source: 7 published sources, Chinese-language, collected 10 September 2026,
> grouped by publication date before counting. Modal values, not primary
> readings.

**7. File size, where nothing settles.**

> No file size cap for in-feed video settles. It appears as 500MB on two
> sources, 1,000MB on the only 2024 page, and 100MB on one more. A file under
> 100MB clears every figure in circulation.
> Source: 4 published sources, Chinese-language, collected 10 September 2026,
> the full spread reported. Modal values, not primary readings; the last
> sentence is reasoning from the smallest published figure.

**8. The age of the open screen sheet.**

> The newest complete Douyin open screen spec sheet in public circulation dates
> from the fourth quarter of 2020. Between the 2018 and 2020 versions the static
> image cap rose from under 150KB to under 300KB and the largest canvas grew to
> 1080 by 2340. Nothing publicly reachable shows what changed after that.
> Source: two rate card generations, 2018 and 2020, both Chinese-language, read
> 10 September 2026. An observation about age, not a current value.

**9. Search ads.**

> Douyin search ads publish no creative spec of their own on any source found.
> On Douyin they run as video, with one ad mixed into results positions 2 to 10
> and the first position held for an exact brand-name search.
> Source: 2 published sources, Chinese-language, collected 10 September 2026.
> Modal value, not a primary reading, and an absence stated with its search date.

**10. How rejections are reported now.**

> Rejection notices have become more specific. Since an AI review upgrade
> reported for October 2025, a rejected video is flagged at the second, or the
> line of dialogue, where the problem sits, rather than rejected as a whole.
> Source: 2 published sources, Chinese-language, one dated 2026, collected 10
> September 2026. Modal value, not a primary reading.
