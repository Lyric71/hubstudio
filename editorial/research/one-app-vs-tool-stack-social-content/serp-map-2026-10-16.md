# SERP map (R2): One app or a stack of single-purpose tools for social content

Run date: 2026-10-16. Engine: web search tool (US results). Each query returned nine organic results, so the map holds 45 rows, not 50. Any publisher that sells social media, marketing, design or content software or services is described, never named. Neutral publications, analyst bodies, forums, review sites and non-marketing businesses keep their domain.

"Publisher sells" key: **Suite** (a multi-function platform), **Tool** (a point product), **Services** (agency, consultancy or implementation partner), **Neutral** (sells nothing in the category). "n/d" means no date visible in the result or on the page. Pages marked (opened) were read in full.

## The bar

* **Ranking listicles (Q1, Q3):** 4,500 to 8,500 words, 1 to 5 tables (one at-a-glance comparison table is the norm; the deepest page has five matrices), tool-by-tool sections, pricing per tool, a recommendation by team type or growth stage.
* **Comparison essays (Q2):** 1,200 to 3,000 words, no tables, five or six abstract criteria (integration, training, rollout time, flexibility, lock-in), a "it depends" conclusion.
* **Cost and consolidation guides (Q4, Q5):** 1,100 to 5,000 words, 0 to 4 tables, a phased audit framework or a cost model in categories, statistics borrowed from SaaS management studies.
* Depth: wide and generic. No page follows one piece of social content from brief to published post.

## The gap

No ranking page follows one social post through its life to count the hand-offs between single-purpose tools (export, rename, re-upload, re-approve), none treats the version and approval record as a buying criterion, and the only per-seat versus usage comparison comes from vendors pricing their own category.
