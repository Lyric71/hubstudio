Rohan Narayana Murty is the founder and CEO of Workfabric AI, a context engineering company that helps enterprises deploy AI agents by delivering precise organizational context. He holds a PhD in computer science from Harvard University.
Sandeep Dadlani is the Chief Digital & Technology Officer of UnitedHealth Group, where he drives enterprise-wide digital transformation. He was previously the Chief Digital Officer of Mars and the president of Infosys.
Rajath B. Das is a senior research analyst at Soroco, focusing on analytical insights derived from the work graph  -  patterns in how teams get work done in the enterprise.
