=== EXCERPT: Create a post in Meta Business Suite ===
Create and manage posts in Meta Business Suite on desktop In Meta Business Suite, you can create and schedule posts to publish on Facebook and Instagram. You can also manage posts and view insights about a post’s performance. Before you begin To create a post, you must have either: Full control of the Page or Instagram account where you want to post Content permission for the Page or Instagram account where you want to post Create a post in Meta Business Suite To create a post in Meta Business Suite on desktop: Go to Meta Business Suite . Click Create post . You can do this from the home page or the Content tab. Under Post to , select the destination where you want to post your content, such as your Facebook Page, Instagram profile, or Facebook Group. Under Media , add your photos. Note : The limit for posting to Instagram is 10 photos. Under Post details , write your text. You can customize the text for Facebook and Instagram. You can also add hashtags, a location, a feeling/activity or other options using the icons below the text box. Under Privacy settings, select an audience for your post: Public : Anyone on or off Facebook will be able to see your post. Restricted : Choose certain people on Facebook who can see your post. To schedule a post in advance: Under Scheduling options , select the date and time you want to publish. Note : Click Active times to select recommended times based on when followers were most active on Facebook and Instagram over the past 7 days. When you’re done creating your post, click Publish , Schedule , or Finish later .
