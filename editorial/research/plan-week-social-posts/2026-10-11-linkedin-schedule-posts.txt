=== EXCERPT: Schedule posts (a1347212) ===
Schedule posts

Last updated: 2 days ago

Scheduling posts helps you plan content in advance and share updates at a time that works best for you. You can create posts now, schedule them to be published later, and manage all of your scheduled posts from one place.
If you have scheduled posts in your queue, the Clock icon displays a badge displaying the number of scheduled posts to help you discover scheduled posts more easily.
...
In the Schedule post pop-up window, select the date and time when you want your post to be published.
Note:
You can schedule a post between 10 minutes and 3 months from the current time.
You can enter a specific time instead of selecting one of the 30-minute increments from the dropdown.
Scheduled times are converted to UTC based on your device's time zone settings.

=== EXCERPT: Schedule a LinkedIn Page post (a1419179) ===
Schedule a LinkedIn Page post

Last updated: 10 months ago

As a super admin or a content admin of a Page, you can schedule a post to be published at a date and time that you choose. You can schedule a post anywhere between an hour from the current time to three months in advance. Once scheduled, your post will be posted at the set date and time automatically.

With the option to schedule a post, you can plan your content in advance and streamline your posting strategy. You can schedule posts to be published at a regular cadence to increase audience reach, and create a more visible presence on LinkedIn.
