PAGE TITLE: Share a post with multiple photos or videos on Instagram
You can share a post with up to 20 photos and videos as a single post, also known as a carousel, to your Instagram feed.

=== EXCERPT: Step list (computer instructions) ===
* Select up to 20 photos and videos from your computer. To adjust how each photo or video is cropped, click and hold it, then drag to adjust how it fits in the frame. Keep in mind that the orientation you choose (square, portrait or landscape) affects all of the photos or videos in your post. You can't select a different orientation for each photo or video. When you're done, click Next in the top right.

=== EXCERPT: Caption note ===
* You can add one location and caption for your entire post, and tag someone in each individual photo. Click the photo to tag someone.
