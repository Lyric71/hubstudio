PAGE TITLE: Share photos on Facebook
## Share photos on Facebook

* At the top of your Feed, click Photo/video.

* Select the photos you want to share.

* If you want, you can:

* Click Photos to add more photos.

* Click Tag friends. Learn more about how tagging works.

* ClickCheck in to add a location.

* Click Feeling/activity to share what you're feeling or doing.

* Click Options to see more things you can add to your post.

* Click Edit All or Add photos/videos.

* Select your audience, then click Post.

Learn more about adding photos to an album or sharing photos to your story.
