=== EXCERPT: Schedule a post and manage scheduled posts for your Facebook Page ===
Schedule a post and manage scheduled posts for your Facebook Page You can create a post for your Page and schedule it to publish in the future. Scheduled posts can be created and edited by people with Facebook or Task access . All times for scheduling correspond to your current time zone. Schedule a post Switch to the Page you want to use. From your Page, click Meta Business Suite in the left menu. Click Create post underneath your Page’s profile picture. Add content for your post. Next to Scheduling options , click the Set date and time toggle. Set a date and time for your post. You can also click Active times to see recommended publication times. Click Schedule . You can schedule posts to publish between 20 minutes and 29 days away.
