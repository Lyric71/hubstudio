=== EXCERPT: About advanced X Pro features ===
Get the best out of X with X Pro’s advanced features - ideal for consuming and analyzing a lot of information at once. Customize your experience with post scheduling, collections, search, filtering, and more.

X Pro allows you to schedule posts in advance. If you manage multiple accounts through X Pro, you have the option to schedule posts for each of them.
For those who use X Pro in a corporate environment, note that all scheduled posts for accounts connected to your X Pro are visible to anyone with access.
To schedule a post:
Sign in to your X Pro account and click the post .
Confirm that the X account you'd like to post from is selected.
Compose your post. Include an image or video with the post by clicking Add images or video .
Click Schedule post and select the date and time you’d like the post posted.
Click Schedule post at [date/time] .
Tip: View and edit your scheduled posts by adding a Scheduled column. Read about adding columns to your X Pro.

Note
X Pro does not support scheduled Direct Messages.

=== EXCERPT: Scheduled Posts (X Ads help) ===
Scheduled Posts

Scheduled Posts
X Ads allow you to schedule Promoted-only Posts to go live at a specific date and time. You can schedule Posts within your ads account and add them to new and existing campaigns. This feature is great for Posts that need to be published on weekends, evenings, or other busy times when you don't have time to Post manually.

How to create scheduled Posts

Log in to ads.x.com .

Navigate to the “Composer” tab on the left.

Create your Post. Add your copy, photos, videos, and cards here.

Click on the drop down arrow next to “Post” on the upper right corner of Composer.

Select the date and time you want your scheduled Post to go live.

Once you’re finished, click “Schedule.”

Your Post won't be discoverable on X or by any data partners until the date and time you scheduled.
