=== EXCERPT: How to create and schedule Facebook posts with Meta One ===
How to create and schedule Facebook posts with Meta One
Updated: 3 weeks ago

Scheduling posts and reels up to a year in advance is only available to eligible public Instagram accounts with a Meta One subscription. This feature is only available on mobile devices.

If you have a Page or a profile in pro mode, you can create, schedule and manage posts directly on Facebook.

=== EXCERPT: Scheduling options ===
Scheduling options
You can choose to publish your post immediately, or schedule it to post later. From Post settings, you can:
Publish now: Your post will go live immediately.
Schedule for later: You can choose a time to publish your post or use the recommended times, which are based on when your followers are most active on Facebook. With a Meta One plan, you can schedule up to a year (365 days) in advance.
To see all your scheduled posts, tap Manage Posts on your Page or profile.
To change a post after you've scheduled it, tap Manage Posts , then delete the post and recreate it.
