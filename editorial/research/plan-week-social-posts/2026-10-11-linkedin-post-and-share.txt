Post and share updates

Last updated: 2 days ago

Sharing updates on LinkedIn helps you build your professional presence, engage with your network, and showcase your expertise. Whether you're sharing an idea in the moment, saving a draft to refine later, or planning content in advance, LinkedIn provides flexible tools to help you create and publish posts on your terms.

You can create a post and share updates using the share box at the top of your LinkedIn homepage. Posts can contain up to 3,000 characters. If you're not ready to publish right away, you can save your post as a draft and continue editing it later, including from another device.

If you're a super admin or content admin of one or more LinkedIn Pages, you can also share updates directly from your LinkedIn homepage.

Note :
Use Start a post from the main share box on the LinkedIn desktop experience to view additional sharing options.

Post suggestions are displayed below the pop-up window that appears when you click Start a post . Click on a suggestion, select an option, and follow the prompts to create a post.
If you're not ready to publish, you can save your post as a draft and continue editing it later, including from another device.
If you'd like your post to go live at a later time, you can schedule it in advance and manage your scheduled posts from the post composer.

Keep in mind :
If we detect excessive post creation that indicates inauthentic activity, or the use of an automation tool, we may limit the visibility of those posts.

=== EXCERPT: Mobile steps, closing note ===
If your post exceeds 3000 characters, you can instead write an article using LinkedIn’s Publishing Platform.
