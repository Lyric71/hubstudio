How to Post
iOS Android Desktop

Select the post compose icon

You can include up to 4 types of photos, a GIF , or a video (and up to 4 total media items, e.g. 2 photos, 1 GIF, and 1 video) in your post.

Compose your message (up to 280 characters) and select Post.

How to create a longer post
iOS Android Desktop

Longer posts are an X Premium feature that can be created the same way a regular post would be
Select the post compose icon

You can include photos, a GIF , or a video (and up to 4 total media items, e.g. 2 photos, 1 GIF, and 1 video) in your post.

Compose your message. When you reach 280 characters, the word count will switch from tracking your progress around the perimeter of the circle icon to filling it

Once you’ve finished writing (up to 4,000 characters), select post.
