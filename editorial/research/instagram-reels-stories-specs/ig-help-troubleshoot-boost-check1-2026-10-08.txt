Common Instagram boost errors and how to fix them
If you're seeing an error when trying to boost content or manage your ads, try these troubleshooting tips:
When you boost an Instagram post from your professional account, we may ask you to log into Facebook to connect to a Facebook ad account. Here’s how to check whether you’re using the right ad account:
You click Boost and nothing happens or you get an error saying, “Something went wrong. There's an issue on our end and we can't boost your post right now. Please try again later.”
You boost a post but the ad is not showing in Ad tools or you get an error saying your insights or promotions can’t load right now.
You get an error saying, “Something went wrong. Ad payment info is unavailable. Please try again later.”
You get an error saying, “We're unable to connect to your Meta ad account.”
You lost access to ads after switching from a non-Meta ad account to a Meta ad account.
The ad account you want to connect to isn't available to select.
You boosted content, but the ad isn't showing in Ad tools.
You get an error saying, “Boosting is currently unavailable in the app. Creating ads in the Instagram iOS app is unavailable. You can still use other Meta tools to create ads.”
You get an error saying, “Boost Unavailable. Reels containing tappable elements cannot be boosted. Choose a different reel to create an ad.”
You get an error saying, “Boost Unavailable. This video’s resolution is too low and can’t be used. Choose a different post or upload a higher quality video.”
Additional troubleshooting tips
If you’re still experiencing issues boosting on Instagram, try the following:
Check your ad specifications. Confirm that your ad meets Instagram’s platform requirements. Refer to Meta Ads Guide for specifications on Image, Video, Carousel and Collection ads.
Run and manage your ads from instagram.com. Use instagram.com from your computer or mobile web browser to run and manage ads.
Run and manage your ads from Ads Manager. Use the Ads Manager app or visit Ads Manager on a computer to run and manage your ads.
Run and manage your ads from Meta Business Suite. Use the Meta Business Suite app or visit Meta Business Suite to run and manage your ads.
