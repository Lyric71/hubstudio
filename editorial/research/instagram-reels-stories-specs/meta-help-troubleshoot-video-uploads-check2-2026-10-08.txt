Troubleshoot video ad uploads

When creating a video ad, you may need to upload a new video from your computer. Since video files are typically large, uploading your video may take a few minutes.

Note: If you select Awareness as your ad objective or choose Maximize reach of ads, Maximize 2-second continuous video views, Maximize number of impressions, or Maximize engagement with a post as your performance goal, the ad footer on your video ad won’t be displayed in the Facebook Feed on mobile devices. This includes the headline, description, call-to-action button, and website URL.

If you have trouble uploading the video, check:

The format. We recommend using an MP4, MOV or GIF file, but most file types are supported.
The size. There is a maximum file size of 4GB for all videos.
The duration. The maximum video duration varies across placements, ranging from 15 seconds to 240 minutes. Ensure your video meets the requirements for your placement.
Your permissions. You must have an appropriate page role to run ads.
Your browser. Make sure you are using the most recent version.
Your network connection. You will need a strong internet connection to upload files.
Your uploaded files. You may have already uploaded the video in your account videos.

If you still have trouble uploading the video in Ads Manager, try uploading it to Meta Media Library instead. Learn how to create a folder in Meta Media Library.

If your uploaded video is blurry, check:

The aspect ratio. Requirements may vary across platforms and placements.
The size. Small video files may appear pixelated.
The chosen placement. The required size and aspect ratio of your video will vary across placements. Review your chosen placement to ensure that the video you're uploading fits the requirements.

The most common reason for videos not rendering correctly is size. Make sure the video you upload is large enough for your chosen placement and has a minimum width of 600 pixels. Note that on the computer version of Facebook Feed, your video will be shown with a 1:1 aspect ratio, which may cause black bars to appear on either side of the video.

View the Ads Guide to see recommended video ratios for each placement. You can check that your ad is rendering properly by previewing your ad in Ads Manager.
