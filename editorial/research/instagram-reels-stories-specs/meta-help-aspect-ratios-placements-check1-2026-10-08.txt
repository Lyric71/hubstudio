Different ad placements require different image and video aspect ratios. Learn how to choose ad placements and how to upload and customize your ad creative for placements, including size variations. Remember to check the Ads Guide for the correct aspect ratio if there is a specific ad format you want to use or ad placement you want your ad to be delivered.

The following guidance focuses on image and video ads that you upload manually, including single image ads, video ads and carousel ads. Learn more about the design specifications for Advantage+ catalog ads and the supported aspect ratios.

Note: Static image ads with catalog items added are not considered Advantage+ catalog ads.
