Image file preparation

This page explains what file settings to use when preparing product images for upload to Amazon, including color mode, compression, and format requirements.

Note: Your images must also meet the image specifications and requirements outlined in Product image guide.
Image color mode

Customers use a wide variety of displays that render colors differently. It is likely that colors you see on your screen are different from what someone else sees. To ensure that your images display accurately, follow our image color specifications.

Color mode	Description	Accepted?
RGB	RGB is the color mode native to the web and most color screen displays. RGB stands for red, green, and blue at varying intensities to create over 16 million colors at varying intensities to create over 16 million colors.	Yes
CMYK	CMYK is designated for printed materials. It tends to show color tonal variations when displayed on a website.	Not preferred. Convert to RGB before uploading.
Grayscale	Grayscale is a color mode that is used both in print and on the web. The grayscale color space uses 256 shades of grey to create images.	Acceptable only for product detail page images that accurately depict gray or silver metal products.

Color profiles and color display

Do not rely on ICC color profiles for accurate color display on Amazon. ICC color profiles are useful in the printing industry, but are not commonly used for web display.

Images that rely on a specific color profile for accurate color display may not appear as intended on Amazon.

To ensure color accuracy, submit files that use the RGB or working color format. To convert your image profile to RGB or Working RGB you must have image-editing software that has that capability.

Unsupported image file formats

Our servers can only accept images that are formatted to our specifications.

We reserve the right to reject images that do not meet our content standards, even if the images meet the technical formatting specifications.

We do not accept images submitted in the following formats:

EPS (encapsulated postscript file)
BMP (Windows bitmap)
PDF (portable document format)

We do not accept file formats that are proprietary to a specific software application, including, but not limited to, the following:

PSD (native Photoshop files)
IA (Adobe Illustrator)
ART (America Online)
CDR (CorelDRAW)
CPT (Corel Paint)
FH (Macromedia Freehand)
FLA (Flash), .PICT (MAC OSX default)
PSP (Paint Shop Pro)
DOC (Microsoft Word)

Image file preparation

Follow these best practices before you upload images:

Review the Image specifications outlined in the Product image guide.
Submit images with minimal or no compression.
For JPEG files, save at the highest possible quality level. Avoid saving multiple times in JPEG format during editing, as each save reduces image quality.
For TIFF files, do not apply any file compression.
Remove all layers from images before you submit.
