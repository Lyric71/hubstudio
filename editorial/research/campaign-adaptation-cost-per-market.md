### The verdict

Across six phrasings and forty-nine results, **one page publishes a price for an
adaptation with a stated inclusion list.** One. Everything else sells software,
prices media, prices origination, defines the word without costing it, or
asserts a saving with no denominator.

**Vendor-published share.** Of the forty-nine results, forty are published by a
party selling the service, the software or the media being described, which is
roughly 82 percent. Six are off-intent non-commercial pages (three patents, two
encyclopedia entries, an education page), one is an academic paper, one is trade
press and one is a law firm article. Not one result is a buyer-side benchmark, a
trade body instrument or a survey with a stated sample.

**Age.** Roughly two thirds of the commercial results carry no visible
publication date. Where a date exists it is usually a "2026" stamped into the
title of an annually refreshed pricing guide, which is a freshness signal rather
than a fielding date.

**The bar.** These pages run roughly 1,000 to 2,200 words with zero to one table
and no dated citation. The brief's 2,500-word target with an answer table, a
format-family rate structure, a worked twelve-market example and a glossary
clears the field on depth rather than length. The bar to beat is one table that
defines the unit before it prices it, and no page on this SERP has one.

**The gap, in one sentence.** Nobody publishes a tiered definition of
adaptation, so the word covers a resize, a retitle, a recut, a recast and a
reshoot at once, and every page that answers the query either prices a media
budget instead of a production one or asserts that versioning saves money
without ever saying what one adaptation unit contains.

## The bar, restated as instructions

1. Define the unit in the first screen, above any number.
2. Five named tiers, each with what it includes, what it excludes and the
   denominator it scales on.
3. Two tables minimum: the tier table and the worked twelve-market example.
4. Every currency figure carries a category attribution, a collection date and a
   one-sentence method, or it does not run.
5. The arithmetic is shown on the page, step by step, and labeled as arithmetic.
