## Cleared for use

> YouTube counts a square or vertical video of up to three minutes, uploaded
> on or after October 15, 2024, as a Short.
> Source: YouTube Help, "Understand three-minute YouTube Shorts", read October 2026.
> https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877

> A Short over one minute with an active Content ID claim of any type is
> blocked globally on YouTube.
> Source: YouTube Help, same page, English version, undated, read October 2026.

> From September 24, 2026, new Shorts over one minute but under three minutes
> with an active Content ID claim are no longer blocked automatically.
> Source: YouTube Help, same page, French version, read October 2026, our
> translation. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=fr

The two are published together (R6), each attributed to its language version.

> All TikTok creators can post 3-minute videos through an app, and some can
> post 5 or 10 minutes.
> Source: TikTok for Developers, Content Posting API media transfer guide,
> updated August 2026. https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-media-transfer-guide

> Reels can add up to 20 minutes, but reels over 3 minutes are not
> recommended to new audiences.
> Source: Instagram, Reels feature page, read October 2026.
> https://about.instagram.com/features/reels

> Instagram makes low-resolution, watermarked, muted, bordered or
> majority-text reels, and reels already posted on Instagram, less visible.
> Source: Instagram, "Instagram Ranking Explained", May 2023.

> TikTok's ad guidance puts the proposition in the first 3 seconds and the
> hook in the first 6, and recommends captions or text overlays.
> Source: TikTok Ads Help Center, creative best practices, updated June 2025.

> YouTube's automatic captions can misrepresent speech; YouTube says to
> review and edit them.
> Source: YouTube Help, "Use automatic captioning", read October 2026.
