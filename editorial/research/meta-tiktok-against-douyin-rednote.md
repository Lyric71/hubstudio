## Method notice, binding on the writer

This page has two halves, and they carry two different kinds of evidence. Never
let the stronger half lend its confidence to the weaker one.

1. **Meta and TikTok rows are primary readings, scoped to the page read.** Each
   was fetched unauthenticated on 2026-09-10 from the platform's own ads guide or
   ads help center. Meta's ads guide pages carry the objective in their title
   (all read under Awareness) and print no last-updated date. TikTok's pages
   print a month. A row is primary for that page, that placement and that buying
   type only. Meta pages were first served in a Chinese locale to the fetcher and
   were re-read with `?locale=en_US`; the English wording is what is quoted here,
   and the two renderings matched on every number.
2. **Douyin and RedNote rows are reused verbatim** from
   `douyin-video-specs-safe-zones.md` (DV, brief 05, 12 non-platform domains plus
   2 Open Platform API doc pages), `douyin-ad-creative-specs-by-format.md` (DA,
   brief 06, 17 sources) and `rednote-note-cover-specs.md` (RN, brief 07, 20
   domains), all collected 2026-09-10 under deviation 7. They are modal values,
   not platform readings. Nothing was re-counted. Counts from different
   collections are never added (rule from `one-shoot-six-platforms-china-variant-matrix.md`, key M).
3. **No figure crosses the border between apps.** A TikTok figure never sits on
   a Douyin row, and a Douyin figure never sits on a TikTok row. A Meta figure is
   never offered as a RedNote figure.
4. **Everything in "Survives or fails" and "Capture plan" is derived**: arithmetic
   or reasoning on the rows above, each naming its inputs. Not a platform rule.
5. **Disclaimer.** Douyin and RedNote rows carry the social-platform form of the
   SPEC.md block. The readable-page paragraph names the Meta and TikTok pages
   below and the Douyin Open Platform upload API doc (DV), each primary for its
   own scope only. NN is not one number: DV 12, DA 17, RN 20, never summed.

New sources met on this brief's searches that carry RedNote or Douyin numbers
(for example a 2026 Chinese seller-media page giving RedNote video 1GB and 15
seconds to 5 minutes) were **not** added to the DV, DA or RN pools. Adding them
would re-count a closed collection. They are listed under "Do not publish".
