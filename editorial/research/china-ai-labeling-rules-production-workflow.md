Note for the writer: the duty in Article 4 attaches to the **explicit** label
on export, not the implicit one. The implicit metadata duty sits in Article 5
and is not restated in the export clause. Do not blur the two.

> The Measures for Labeling of AI-Generated Synthetic Content were issued
> jointly by the Cyberspace Administration of China, the Ministry of Industry
> and Information Technology, the Ministry of Public Security and the National
> Radio and Television Administration, released 14 March 2025 and in force
> since 1 September 2025.
> Source: Cyberspace Administration of China, March 2025.
> https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm

> The mandatory national standard GB 45438-2025, Cybersecurity technology,
> Labeling method for content generated by artificial intelligence, was
> published 28 February 2025 and took effect 1 September 2025, the same day as
> the Measures.
> Source: national standards register, State Administration for Market
> Regulation, February 2025.
> https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=F32EA2A561F1886CD8D606513512D547

> Article 5 of the Measures puts the implicit label in the file metadata and
> says it carries content attribute information, the service provider name or
> code, and a content ID, among other production element information. The same
> article defines file metadata as descriptive information embedded in the file
> header in a specific encoding format.
> Source: Cyberspace Administration of China, March 2025, instrument text.
> https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm

> Article 4 of the Measures says that when a service provider offers download,
> copy or export functions for generated synthetic content, it must ensure the
> file contains a conforming explicit label.
> Source: Cyberspace Administration of China, March 2025, instrument text.
> https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm

> Article 6 of the Measures gives a distribution platform three cases in which
> it adds a prominent notice: the implicit label in the metadata confirms the

## Do not publish

### The three hard blocks

| Item | Reason it is blocked |
|---|---|
| **Any fine or penalty figure under the Measures.** No number, no range, no "up to" | The Measures do not set a new penalty schedule. Article 13 refers violations back to the existing authorities and existing law. Enforcement is described in the sources as running through existing instruments and is still unclear in practice. Every ranking page that names a fine is inferring from other statutes. Do not publish a figure, and do not write "fines of up to." |
| **The allocation of labeling duties between brand, agency and tool vendor.** Write "generally treated as", never "requires" | This allocation is law-firm interpretation, not an explicit regulator statement. The Measures assign duties to 服务提供者 (generation and synthesis service providers), 传播服务提供者 (distribution platforms), app distribution platforms and 用户 (users). They do not name brands or agencies, and they do not say which of those roles a brand or an agency occupies. Every sentence on the page that maps a duty onto a brand or an agency must read "generally treated as", "in practice falls to", or "sits with whoever the platform treats as the publisher". Never "the brand is required to". |
| **Anything not verified on a primary source** | R3 and R7. Everything below failed that test. |
