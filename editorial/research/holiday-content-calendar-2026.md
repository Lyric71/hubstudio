**The bar:** the ranking pages run 1,500 to 4,000 words, mostly lists, with
one date table at most and no cited platform deadline. Seller playbooks print
lead times (6 weeks, 16 weeks, briefs due 15 October, creative settled by 30
September) without a sample or a source. None cites a platform review time,
the Amazon deal submission close or a carrier cutoff, and none covers Europe's
regulated sale dates.

**The gap, in one sentence:** nobody counts back from the deadlines the
platforms, the carriers and the regulators actually publish to a dated last
day to brief and deliver each holiday asset.
