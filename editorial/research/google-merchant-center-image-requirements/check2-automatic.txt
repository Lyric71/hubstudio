How do automatic image improvements attempt to fix promotional overlays?
Product images that contain promotional elements or overlays aren’t allowed and may cause your products to be disapproved. If you turn on automatic image improvements, Merchant Center will try to fix these images by removing any text, watermarks, overlays, or logos detected in the image. If successful, the system replaces the image and your product may be approved. If you're not satisfied with the improved image, upload a new image without the promotional overlay.

Next steps and what to expect
Automatic image improvements may not resolve all issues. If the system can’t fix the image without lowering its quality, the original image will remain. This may occur with:
Complex multi-panel collages
Heavy text overlapping main product features
Strong or dense watermarks
