Reasons why the issue is happening
Some of your products have images with promotional text (retailer logos or calls to action) or watermarks, which can obstruct the view of your product.
To prevent your product from being disapproved, ensure your image avoids the violations listed below:
Don’t use an image that contains promotional elements or overlays
: Your product image must offer an unobstructed, clean view of the item for sale. Adding post-production text, marketing callouts, or graphics distracts shoppers from your product and will cause an image disapproval. Keep your main image focused solely on the product itself.
What to avoid:
Calls to action
: Text prompting action, such as "Buy", "Shop now", or "Order today".
Price and shipping details
: Mentions of price, discounts, or offers like "Free shipping".
Service-related information
: Extended service or warranty claims, such as "Includes 2-year warranty".
Promotional adjectives
: Subjective marketing words, such as "Best", "Cheap", or "Top quality".
Condition or compatibility text
: Added labels describing state or fit, such as "New", "2-piece set", or "Adaptable".
Barcodes and tracking codes
: UPC barcodes, QR codes, or serial tags overlaid on the photo.
Overlays and watermarks
: Stock photo watermarks, copyright symbols, or graphic stamps.
Added brand, manufacturer, or retailer names and logos
: Superimposed logos or store names added in post-production (allowed only if they are an inherent, physical part of the product itself).
How to fix the issue
All products on ads and free listings require an image with an unobstructed view of the product. Products that don’t meet this requirement are disapproved and remain disapproved until their images are updated.
Tip
: You can turn on
automatic image improvements
to allow Merchant Center to apply changes to your product images. Note that changes are made automatically.
If you want to fix the steps directly in Merchant Center, check the steps below.
Fix products one by one directly in Merchant Center
In your
Merchant Center account
, select
Products
under the “Products & store” section in the left navigation menu.
Under the “Needs attention” tab, use the filter icon
above the table to find all products affected by your issue.
Select on the title of the product you want to fix, then click
Edit product
.
Select on the main product image. In the window that opens, change the URL that you've submitted via the
image link
[image_link]
attribute so that it points to a valid product image. Make sure the image file located at the submitted URL meets all image overlay requirements.
Select
Save
.
Fix multiple products at once
In your
Merchant Center account
, select
Products
under the “Products & store” section in the left navigation menu.
Under the “Needs attention” tab, use the filter icon
above the table to find all products affected by your issue.
Select the download icon
to download a list (.csv) of all affected products for all issues or this particular issue.
Cross reference the downloaded list with your uploaded product data, and make sure the URLs that you’ve submitted for each affected product via the
image link
[image_link]
attribute lead to valid product images. Alternatively, make sure the images at the end of the provided image URLs meet the overlay requirements.
Reupload your corrected product data to Merchant Center using your chosen upload method.
Next steps
After making the requested changes, your changes may take 24–72 hours to be reflected on the “
Needs attention
