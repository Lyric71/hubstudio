**Standards cited.** Of the three pages read in full, none cites ISO 17100, ISO
18587 or any standard. The other 28 were not read in full, so the page may not
claim the whole SERP cites no standard.

**The bar.** The two measured pages run about 1,100 and about 2,500 words. The
longest puts its steps in narrative, not a table, and lists brief contents in a
sentence. No page read carries a stage table, a brief template laid out as a
template, or a pricing-unit comparison sourced to anyone but the publisher. The
brief's 1,600 words with a stage table, a definitions table and a brief template
clears the field on structure, not length.

**The gap, in one sentence.** Twenty-eight of thirty-one results are published
by sellers of translation software or language services, and none of the pages
read in full sets the one published transcreation code of practice beside the
translation and post-editing standards, prices the work in the units that code
allows, or runs visual adaptation and the compliance check as stages of the
same line as the copy.

**The H1 consequence.** The gap supports the working angle (a production line,
not a per-word buy). But SPEC requires the H1 to carry the target query in the
buyer's words, and the working H1 carries neither "process" nor "marketing".
See Notes for the writer.

**Definitions**

> The Institute of Translation and Interpreting defines transcreation of
> advertising and marketing content as a professional practice that combines
> translation, copywriting and intercultural consultancy, and that usually, but
> not always, involves adaptation.
> Source: Institute of Translation and Interpreting, Transcreation Code of
> Practice, August 2026. Guidelines drafted from a member survey run October
> 2024 to April 2025 and focus groups with academics, transcreators and
> transcreation companies from January to September 2025.

> As a process, the same code describes transcreation as taking an agreed brief,
> including the source copy and other brand and company documents, and producing
> at least one option built to maximize appeal to and impact on the target
> audience. It classes transcreation as a translation-related activity, not a
> type of translation.
> Source: Institute of Translation and Interpreting, Transcreation Code of
> Practice, August 2026. Association guidelines, no data behind the definition.

> Source: Institute of Translation and Interpreting guidance article, published
