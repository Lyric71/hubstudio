TITLE: Help Center
If you're experiencing an issue on Instagram, please follow these troubleshooting steps. Keep in mind that you should always make sure you're running the latest version of  the Instagram app as well as the latest version of your phone or tablet's operating system before following these steps.
Restart your phone or tablet
Always try restarting your phone or tablet first if you're having trouble with Instagram.
Check Internet connection
If restarting your device doesn't help, please try using Instagram both on Wi-Fi and on your mobile data connection to see if the problem is because of a weak Wi-Fi or mobile data connection.
Uninstall and reinstall
If restarting your device and checking your connection doesn't help, try deleting and reinstalling Instagram:
iPhone and iPad
From the Home screen, tap and hold the Instagram app icon.
Remove App
Go to the App Store, reinstall Instagram and then log in with your username and password.
