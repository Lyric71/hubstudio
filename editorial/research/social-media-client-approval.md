## R1. What has to be true

Mapped from the brief before anything was looked up. Every claim below is
either a hubStudio app fact (help center or `hubstudio-positioning.md`, the
R5 first-party exception: the site already publishes it) or a plain
description of how an email thread behaves, which carries no figure.

- Approval by email fails on two questions: which version was approved, and
  whether the post changed after the approval. (Description of email
  behavior; no figure needed or used.)
- Set-up: add a client company under Clients, give its people a login with
  the Client role, tag work Made for that client so it reaches the Client
  space.
- What a client login sees and does not see (work grouped by day, Download,
  no prompts, engines, costs or balance).
- Where Send for validation sits (image studio, video studio, publishing step
  of a post) and who can be named (a teammate or one of the client's people).
- The thread: versions v1, v2 on one page; Validate or Send back with a
  required comment; who is emailed at each event.
- The lock on a post waiting for validation; what approval and send-back do
  to the post.
- The permanent record: nothing on a thread can be deleted; an admin can
  decide in the validator's place.
- Roles: who can send, comment, validate and change the validator; the
  Viewer exception.
- Cost: Validation free and no AI; storage and downloads billed as usual.
- Studio + app: the client approves in the same Validation and Client space.
- Optional market statistic on time lost to approval rounds: only from a
  published survey with a stated method and sample.
