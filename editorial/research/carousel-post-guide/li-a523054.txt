Document uploads on LinkedIn FAQ
Last updated: 3 years ago
Document uploading allows you to share and consume documents directly on LinkedIn. Check out the answers to some of the top questions about sharing a document .
How can I troubleshoot a failed document upload?
You’ll receive a notification if your document fails to upload. If you’ve received this notification, you can use these troubleshooting tips and try to re-upload your document.
Make sure your document is formatted as one of the following: PPT, PPTS, DOC, DOCX, and PDF.
Make sure that the document’s file size is 100 MB or less.
Make sure that the document is 300 pages or less.
Make sure your document has a title .
How do I edit or delete a document I have uploaded on LinkedIn?
You can edit the title and description of your document, but you can’t change or edit the document in the post. You can always delete the post entirely and create a new post with the updated document.
Can I add multiple documents to the same post?
Not at this time. You can only add one document to a post.
How do I find documents I’ve uploaded on LinkedIn?
You can find documents you’ve uploaded on LinkedIn on the Activity page of your profile.
How do I download a document I find on LinkedIn?
To download a document you find on LinkedIn:
View the document in full screen mode.
Click the Download icon in the top right corner.
