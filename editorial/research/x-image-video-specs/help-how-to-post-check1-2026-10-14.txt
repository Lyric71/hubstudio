How to Post

Follow step-by-step instructions for composing and sharing posts on >x< from your iOS, Android, or web browser.

A post may contain photos, GIFs, videos, links, and text.

Looking for information on how to post at someone? Check out our article about how to post replies and mentions on >x<.

How to Post
iOS
Android
Desktop

Select the post compose icon

You can include up to 4 types of photos, a GIF, or a video (and up to 4 total media items, e.g. 2 photos, 1 GIF, and 1 video) in your post.

Compose your message (up to 280 characters) and select Post.

How to create a longer post
iOS
Android
Desktop

Longer posts are a feature of X Premium and the Plus, Premium, Super, and Ultra plans on The X Subscription, and can be created the same way a regular post would be

Select the post compose icon

You can include photos, a GIF, or a video (and up to 4 total media items, e.g. 2 photos, 1 GIF, and 1 video) in your post.

Compose your message. When you reach 280 characters, the word count will switch from tracking your progress around the perimeter of the circle icon to filling it

Once you’ve finished writing (up to 4,000 characters), select post.

You can learn more about >x< Premium here.

Post source labels help you better understand how a post was posted. This additional information provides context about the post and its author. If you don’t recognize the source, you may want to learn more to determine how much you trust the content.

Click on a post to go to the post details page.
At the bottom of the post, you’ll see the label for the source of the account’s post. For example, >x< for iPhone, >x< for Android, or >x< for Web.
posts containing the >x< for Advertisers label indicate they are created through the >x< Ads Composer and not whether they are paid content or not. Paid content contains a Promoted badge across all ad formats.
In some cases you may see a third-party client name, which indicates the post came from a non->x< application. Authors sometimes use third-party client applications to manage their posts, manage marketing campaigns, measure advertising performance, provide customer support, and to target certain groups of people to advertise to. Third-party clients are software tools used by authors and therefore are not affiliated with, nor do they reflect the views of, the post content. Posts and campaigns can be directly created by humans or, in some circumstances, automated by an application. Visit our partners page for a list of common third-party sources.
Deleting posts
Read about how to delete a post.
Note that you can only delete your own posts.
You cannot delete posts which were posted by other accounts. Instead, you can unfollow, block or mute accounts whose posts you do not want to receive.
Read about how to delete or undo a repost.
Keyboard shortcuts

The following are a list of keyboard shortcuts to use on x.com.

Actions
n = new post
l = like
r = reply

enter = open post details
o = expand photo
/ = search
