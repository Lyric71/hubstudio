This page explains how to upload product images to your listings individually or in bulk, including country-specific images, and what to expect after upload.

Every product on Amazon must have at least one image. The first image on the product detail page is the "main" image and is shown to customers in search results.

Upload images individually or in bulk:

Use the Upload Images tool to upload product images in bulk or individually. You can upload up to 1,000 images and 1 GB per zip file. You can also upload multiple individual files and multiple zip files simultaneously.
To upload a single image to a specific product, use Image Manager. Search for your product by ASIN or SKU, select a variant code, and upload the image directly. To learn more about Image Manager, go to Manage images.

You can upload additional images to your listings at any time.

Before you upload

Files must be in JPEG (.jpg or .jpeg), TIFF (.tif or .tiff), PNG (.png), or non-animated GIF (.gif) format.
Review the Product image guide to ensure your images meet all technical and content requirements.
If your images don’t meet the requirements or violate the Selling policies and seller code of conduct, Amazon may remove them.
Note: Uploading an image doesn't guarantee it will be displayed on the product detail page. We select and arrange images from multiple selling partners to ensure each product has enough high-quality images for buyers. To maximize the chance of your images being displayed, ensure they comply with our product image requirements.

Global or US upload vs. country-specific upload

If you sell products in more than one country, you can use the same images in all countries or upload country-specific images for each country.

Using the Upload Images tool, you can upload your images to the Global store or to a specific country store.

Decision guide

Your situation	What to do
You sell in the US only	Upload as Global
You sell in multiple countries and want the same images everywhere	Upload as Global
You sell in multiple countries and need different images per country (for example, language-specific text on images)	Upload as Global first, then upload country-specific images for each country

Important information for country-specific uploads:

To upload images for the US store, use the Global option. Country-specific uploads don’t support US-specific images.
If your images contain text, it must be in the country's official language or English.
When uploading country-specific images, you must include a main image (variant code: MAIN) in the image set. If the main image is missing, none of the submitted images will be considered for display in that country.
For example, if you upload Germany-specific variant images to the German store without a main image, none of the submitted images will be considered for display. If you have previously uploaded Global images for that same product, the system will default to the Global images for the German store.
Subsequent global image uploads will not override your country-specific images.
For example, if you upload images to Germany, and later provide an updated set of global images, your global images will not affect the Germany-specific image set.
Images are only applied to products you have a listing for within the country or region of the account you are logged into.
To upload images to other countries or regions, sign in to the respective account.
Some image variants may be required to prove your compliance with applicable laws, regulations, standards, and our policies related to those products and product listings. If this is the case, you will receive an email with instructions.

Upload images to your products
Note: Before you begin, review Before you upload section on this page for image requirements and policies.

When uploading images, you can name your files in two ways:

Legacy file naming: Name your files as Product ID + variant code + extension (for example, B000123456.MAIN.jpg). Images are assigned to products automatically based on file name. This option is best for experienced sellers with automated workflows or large catalogs. For a list of image variant codes go to Image variant on the Product image guide help page.
Note: Note: Images with a product ID in the file name but no variant code will be treated as a main image. For example, B000123456.jpg will be uploaded as the main image.
Flexible file naming: Name your files as you like (for example, image1.jpg) and assign them to products after upload. This option is best for new sellers, one-time uploads, or when you don't have product IDs ready.
Note: Upload one set of images per color or style variation. You can copy the media set to sibling products later in Assign Images. This removes the need to rename and re-upload images for every product sibling.

Upload your images in bulk


Go to the Upload Images tool.
Select or drag and drop files from your computer.
If you use legacy file naming, make sure your files follow the format: Product ID + variant code + extension (for example, B000123456.MAIN.jpg).
If you use flexible file naming, you can name files however you like.
You can upload multiple individual files or zip files. Maximum 1,000 images and 1 GB per zip file.
(Optional) Assign images to the Global store or a specific country or region. (You can also make these assignments later in Assign Images.)
Select the image or images you want to assign.
Select Global or a specific country or region.
Select Seller Code.
If you use flexible file naming, input a product identifier in ASIN or SKU or Other (barcode number, ASIN, SKU, UPC, ISBN 10 or 13, EAN, GTIN, or JAN). Variants are assigned in Assign Images.
Click Upload.

After you upload

Immediately after upload, Amazon checks file format, permissions, and offer status. Review the Upload Results prompt:
A Batch ID is provided for each upload submission. Use this ID to filter your images in Assign Images.
Review the Results Breakdown showing assigned images, unassigned images, and failures.
If there are failures, download the Failure Report to identify which files failed, along with failure reasons and suggested next steps.
Next, we perform additional checks for technical compliance and product image requirements. Processing can take up to 24 hours.
Note: Some image variants may be required to prove your compliance with applicable laws, regulations, standards, and Amazon policies. If this applies to your products, you will receive an email with instructions.
If you're getting technical errors when uploading images, such as file format, file size, or naming issues, go to Image upload errors and display issues.
If your image is selected for display, it appears on the product detail page.

Check your image status

To check if images were successfully uploaded, go to Submission status. ASINs displaying "Image upload completed" have been successfully processed. If an image has an issue, a red exclamation point icon appears in the image tile. Select the Content compliance issues report type for details.

Manage your images

Go to Image Manager to preview how your images appear on the product detail page, copy a media set to sibling products, upload individual images, or delete images. Go to the Manage images help page to learn more..

Complete your assignments

If you did not assign your images to a country or region when uploading your images, do so from Assign Images. If you used flexible file naming, go to Assign Images to assign variants to your images and make further adjustments to images with incomplete assignments. Go to the Assign images help page to learn more.
