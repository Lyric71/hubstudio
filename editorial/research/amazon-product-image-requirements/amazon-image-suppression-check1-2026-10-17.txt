Image suppression

The following covers the most common reasons Amazon will suppress (hide) product listings or remove images due to policy violations. Understand how to identify your issue and learn how to submit a compliant replacement image.

When you upload images, we check them against our product image requirements. If your images don't comply with our policies, we may:

Suppress (hide) your product listing from search until you provide a compliant image
Remove or modify non-compliant images (for example, removing a watermark)

Note: If you're getting technical errors when uploading images, such as file format, file size, or naming issues, go to Image upload and display issues.
Check your suppression status

To view products removed from search due to image issues, go to the Fix tab in Manage Products.

To view files that failed to meet requirements, go to Submission status and select the Content compliance issues report type.

Fix non-compliant images

Image suppression occurs when your main image or product images are found to be non-compliant with Amazon's policies, including the product detail page rules.

All ASINs require a main image. We recommend that you replace a non-compliant main image rather than deleting it. To replace your main image, upload a new image with the MAIN variant code. Allow up to 24 hours for the change to appear. For detailed steps on how to upload images, go to Upload images.

Uploading an image doesn't guarantee it will be displayed on the product detail page. We select and arrange images from multiple selling partners to ensure each product has enough high-quality images for buyers. To maximize the chance of your images being displayed, ensure they comply with our product image requirements outlined in Product image guide.
