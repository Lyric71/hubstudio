### Pre-shoot compliance checklist, source per line

1. Every on-image and scripted claim mapped to the product's registration or
   filing and its published efficacy summary (Regulation Art. 22; Beijing and
   Shanghai guidelines).
2. Special cosmetics claims only on registered special cosmetics (Regulation
   Art. 16; Beijing guideline).
3. No medical, procedure or medical-figure wording in copy, supers, props or
   set dressing (Advertising Law Art. 17; labeling measures Art. 19; Xingtu).
4. Before-and-after only from unedited capture of real use, retouch log kept
   (Advertising Law Art. 28; Shanghai Art. 28). The retouch log is practice.
5. Every percentage carries its source on the asset (Advertising Law Art. 11).
6. Endorsers and on-camera creators have used the product and are ten or older;
   use recorded (Advertising Law Art. 38).
7. Likeness consent covers China, the platforms and the term (Civil Code Arts.
   1019, 1021, 1022); separate consent for face or voice editing (deep synthesis
   Art. 14).
8. Generated or AI-edited skin carries the explicit and implicit labels (CAC
   Measures), and never shows more than the product does (Taobao rule, derived
   reading).
9. Posts with a purchase link marked "advertisement"; livestream roles
   assigned (Internet Advertising Measures Arts. 9 and 19).
10. Celebrity portrait and IP authorizations and proof for data, patents and
    awards ready for platform review (Xingtu manual).
