## R1. What has to be true (mapped before looking anything up)

1. The 2026 season dates: Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas,
   the UK Boxing Day and its bank holiday, the French winter sales opening.
2. What the US rule on former-price ("was/now") comparisons says, and whether
   it sets a fixed number of days.
3. What the EU prior-price rule says, its period, and since when it applies.
4. Whether a marketplace refuses a product image that carries a price or a
   promotional overlay (the reason to keep offers off the clean render).
5. What engine makers say about preserving product details in an edit, and
   about their models' limits on brand elements.
6. App facts: the Edit an image job, source pictures per engine, the browser
   resize, shape following the first picture, images per run, the price line
   and the failed-run rule, several runs at once, Improve with AI and its
   cost, the Catalog skills, the Image editor's Text, Social and Crop panels,
   what Save does to the original, Campaigns and the Campaign choice, Send for
   validation, Reuse prompt, the Mask.
7. Any figure on how many visuals a season needs, or on the lift a holiday
   visual brings. (Expected to fail the bar; see Do not publish.)

## Cleared for use

> Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, and Christmas on Friday,
> Dec. 25.
> Source: US Office of Personnel Management, federal holidays 2026, read
> Oct. 19, 2026. Government holiday list.

> A former price supports a price comparison only if it is "the actual, bona
> fide price at which the article was offered to the public on a regular
> basis for a reasonably substantial period of time."
> Source: FTC Guides Against Deceptive Pricing, 16 CFR 233.1(a), eCFR text
> current as of Oct. 15, 2026. Federal guidance under section 5 of the FTC Act.

> "Any announcement of a price reduction shall indicate the prior price,"
> meaning "the lowest price applied by the trader during a period of time not
> shorter than 30 days prior to the application of the price reduction."
> Source: Directive (EU) 2019/2161, Article 6a of Directive 98/6/EC, applied
> from May 28, 2022. EU legislation; member states may adjust it for
> perishable goods, new products and progressive reductions.

> Google Merchant Center wants "an unobstructed, clean view of the item for
> sale" and lists among the overlays it does not allow "mentions of price,
> discounts, or offers like 'Free shipping'."
> Source: Google Merchant Center Help, Image link [image_link], page undated,
> read Oct. 19, 2026.

> Google's image generation documentation tells users that to preserve
> critical details such as a logo during an edit, they should "describe them
> in great detail along with your edit request."
> Source: Gemini API image generation documentation, the maker's own page,
> last updated Oct. 6, 2026.

> OpenAI says its image model "may occasionally struggle to maintain visual
> consistency for recurring characters or brand elements across multiple
> generations."
> Source: OpenAI image generation guide, the maker's own documentation, page
> undated, read Oct. 8, 2026.
