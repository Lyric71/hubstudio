## Cleared for use

Use paraphrases in attributed blocks, not lengthy verbatim quotations.
The six EU criteria can be named. Dates and statute identifiers are legal
identifiers, not market statistics. The age20 timing may appear only as the
case study's own published account, never as a new delivery promise.

The article can recommend retaining real product references and recorded
tests. It must not imply that ordinary photography alone proves efficacy,
that all before-and-after advertising is nationally banned, that all filters
are prohibited, or that synthetic-person labeling cures an untrue claim.

## Do not publish

- Adoption shares, conversion lifts, savings, client budgets and rates:
  unnecessary and no method-supported dataset needed for this decision guide.
- Blanket permission to generate packaging, texture, shades or ingredient
  effects: disproved by visual-claim and evidence rules; brief corrected.
- Every market has one universal AI label: statutes and platform requirements
  have different scope; describe the checked obligations separately.
- A product photograph or generated demonstration is efficacy test evidence:
  no support; distinguish illustration, observation and substantiation.
- Any claim that the app automatically checks cosmetics law, preserves every
  required metadata field, or provides a regulated claims database: absent
  from product documentation.
- Numerical China platform specs: out of scope, none cleared or planned.
- Noyz imagery as an independent laboratory finding; age20 as a China-only
  project; Shiseido's creative brief as evidence of regulatory authorization:
  all excluded.
