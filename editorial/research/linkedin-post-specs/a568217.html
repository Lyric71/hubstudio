Add or change the cover image on your profile

Last updated: 7 months ago

Adding a cover image to your profile make your professional story more visually appealing. It also helps you stand out on LinkedIn to recruiters, future employers, prospective clients, and other members. Your cover image appears behind your profile photo in the introduction section on your profile.

To add a cover image:

  1. Click the Me icon at the top of your LinkedIn homepage.

  2. Click View Profile.

  3. Click the Camera icon on the upper-right corner of your introduction section.

  4. From the add a cover image pop-up, you can:

    • ﻿﻿﻿ Upload a single photo
    • ﻿﻿﻿ Create Slideshow (﻿﻿﻿ LinkedIn Premium feature available for Premium Business, Recruiter Lite, Sales Navigator Core, Premium Pages, and LinkedIn Learning)
    • ﻿﻿﻿ Choose an image

  5. After uploading or creating a slideshow, you can:

    • Edit cover image: Crop the photo, use photo filters, adjust, change the position and size, or rotate your cover image
    • Change cover image: Click Change photo and follow prompts to change your cover image

  6. Click Apply.

    • The image will upload automatically.

To reposition, delete, or change a cover image:

  1. Click the Me icon at the top of your LinkedIn homepage.

  2. Click View Profile.

  3. Click the ﻿﻿﻿ Edit icon on the upper-right corner of the cover image in the introduction section.

  4. From here, you can:

    • Edit cover image: Crop the photo, use photo filters, adjust, change the position and size, or rotate your cover image
    • Change cover image: Click Change photo and follow prompts to change your cover image
    • Delete cover image: Click Delete photo and confirm your action to delete your cover image

  5. Click Apply (if applicable).

To add a cover image:

  1. Tap your profile photo.

  2. Tap your profile photo again to access your profile.

  3. On your profile, tap the Camera icon on the upper-right corner of your introduction section.

  4. From the Add a cover image page, you can:

    •  ﻿﻿﻿ Upload a single photo rom your mobile device’s photo library
    • Choose an image from the images displayed below
  5. After uploading the photo, you can:
    • Crop the photo, use photo filters, adjust, change the position and size, or rotate your cover image.

  6. Tap Save.

To change, edit, or delete your cover image:

  1. Tap your profile photo, then tap View profile.

  2. Tap your profile photo again to access your profile.

  3. On your profile, tap your cover image in the introduction section.

  4. From here, you can:

    • Edit: Tap ﻿﻿﻿ Edit to crop the photo, use photo filters, adjust, change the position and size, or rotate your profile photo
    • Add photo: Tap ﻿﻿﻿ Add photo to either select an image from your mobile device’s photo library or take a new one
    • Delete: Tap﻿﻿﻿ Delete to delete and confirm your action to delete the cover image

  5. Tap Save on the upper right (if applicable).

To add a cover image:

  1. Tap the Profile icon.

  2. Tap the cover image section.

  3. Tap Change on the lower left from the Add background photo page.

  4. Follow the prompts to take a new photo or upload one.
  5. Change the position and size by dragging the photo.

  6. Tap Save on the upper-right corner.

To change, edit, or delete your cover image:

  1. Tap the Profile icon.

  2. Tap the cover image.

  3. From the Edit Background Photo page you can:
    • Change cover image: Tap Change and follow the prompts to take a new photo or upload one
    • Delete cover image: Tap Delete and confirm your action to delete your cover image

  4. Tap Save on the upper-right corner (if applicable).

Live Video stream on the cover image

When a LinkedIn Live Video broadcaster begins a live stream, the cover image will automatically change to display the live stream. This cover image change is temporary and the image will revert after the stream has ended.

Note: Currently there is no option to opt out of this feature.

Cover image specifications:

  • JPG or PNG file
  • Lesser than 8MB
  • 1584 (w) x 396 (h) pixels (recommended)

If your cover image appears blurry or pixelated, please choose an image with a larger file size. Photos will also look better than images with logos. If your image is still blurry or pixelated, you could use a compression tool such as Trimage for Windows or ImageOptim for Mac before uploading it to LinkedIn.

Notes:

  • LinkedIn doesn’t provide support for any third-party tools that you use

  • How your image appears may change based on the size of your web browser window and screen resolution

  • Cover image that you upload must comply with our User Agreement
