## YouTube Help, "Understand three-minute YouTube Shorts"

https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en

- "You can now create YouTube Shorts up to three minutes in length."
- "Videos uploaded after October 15, 2024: Any videos uploaded on or after
  this date with a square or vertical aspect ratio up to three minutes in
  length will be categorized as Shorts on YouTube."
- "Videos uploaded before October 15, 2024: Any videos uploaded before this
  date will remain the same, as a long-form video on YouTube."
- "Any Short that is over one minute in duration with an active Content ID
  claim of any type, including manual claims, will be blocked globally on
  YouTube."
- Uploading three-minute Shorts: "the YouTube app and YouTube Studio".
- "Shorts that are blocked globally on YouTube will not be eligible for
  monetization." "Once the claim is resolved, your Short will be viewable and
  eligible for monetization." (re-read 2026-10-08, the English page is still
  undated)

## YouTube Help, "Upload YouTube Shorts" (computer)

https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12779649?hl=en

- A Short is a short video file "With a square or vertical aspect ratio",
  up to 3 minutes.
- Steps: sign in to YouTube Studio, CREATE, Upload videos, select the file.

## YouTube Help, "Use automatic captioning"

https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6373554?hl=en

- Automatic captions are available for long-form videos and Shorts in a
  listed set of languages.
- "automatic captions might misrepresent the spoken content due to
  mispronunciations, accents, dialects, or background noise."
- "Always review automatic captions and edit any parts that haven't been
  properly transcribed."

## TikTok for Developers, Content Posting API, media transfer guide

https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-media-transfer-guide
(page states last updated August 4, 2026)

- "All TikTok creators can post 3-minute videos, while some have access to
  post 5-minute or 10-minute videos."
- "The longest video a developer can send via the initialize Upload Video
  endpoint is 10 minutes."
- Formats MP4 (recommended), WebM, MOV; maximum 4 GB.

## TikTok Ads Help, "Creative best practices" (updated June 2025)

https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices

- "Introduce your content proposition in the first 3 seconds for better
  recall and awareness."
- "Prioritize your hook in the first 6 seconds to boost engagement and
  increase watch time."
- "Use captions or text overlays to provide context to your ad."
- Basics: vertical 9:16, at least 720P, sound or music.
