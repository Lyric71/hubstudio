You can edit and delete the Pins you’ve created and saved on Pinterest. Before deleting a Pin, make sure you no longer want the Pin to appear on your profile or board since deleted Pins cannot be recovered. 

Depending on the Pin, you may be able to edit a Pin’s title, details, description, board, section and comment settings. The details you can edit vary based on how the Pin was created. 

Keep in mind, you cannot edit a Pin’s image, video or audio contents once it’s been published. Instead, you’ll need to create a new Pin.
