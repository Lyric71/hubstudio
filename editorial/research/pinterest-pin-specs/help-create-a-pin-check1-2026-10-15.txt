Publish a draft

You can save a Pin as a draft if you’re not quite ready to publish it yet. Here are a few key things to know about drafts:

Drafts expire 30 days after you've first stored them. After 30 days, you will be unable to retrieve them.
You cannot edit drafts across different devices. If you start creating a Pin on your phone using the Pinterest app, you need to finish creating it there.
If you want to publish multiple Pin drafts at the same time, use a desktop device. You can save up to 50 drafts on your desktop and can publish them all at once.
You need at least 100 MB of storage space available on your mobile device to create a new draft.
