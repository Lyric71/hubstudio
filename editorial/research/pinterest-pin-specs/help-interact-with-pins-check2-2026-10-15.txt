View closed captions on video Pins

Closed captions are time-synchronized text that represents the audible speech in a video. Viewers can choose to enable or disable closed captions. Captions on Pinterest videos are auto generated and cannot be edited or customized by the creator.

Please note that variances in background noise, pronunciation, accents and dialects may lead to speech being misrepresented in automatically generated captions.

The CC (closed captions) option will be shown on all video Pins. However, if captions are not available for a particular video, you won't be able to turn captions on or off.
 

Tap into a video Pin
Tap  
the captions-outline icon
  at the bottom right of the Pin to toggle closed captions on or off

When you turn on captions for a Pin, captions will automatically show up on other Pins that have captions available. If you decide to turn off captions, they'll be disabled for all Pins where captions are available.
