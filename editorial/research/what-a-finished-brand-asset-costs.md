## The unit. A defensible definition of one finished asset

The piece must define the unit before any number appears. There is no ISO
standard and no trade-body definition of one finished asset. What exists is
three published conventions that each draw the boundary for their own purpose,
and they do not agree. That disagreement is the article's opening move, not a
weakness in the research.

| Convention | Where it comes from | What it treats as one unit | What it does not treat as a new unit | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The identity code | Registry run by a joint venture of the US advertiser and agency trade bodies, mandated as the sole standard identifier for union commercials | One registered advertising asset, coded and carried across every platform | No published rule on lengths, cuts, language versions or resizes | Site copyright 2025 |
| The production bid | Standard bid form of the US commercial production trade body | One production, estimated across sections A through P, with stills, social elements and behind the scenes bid as separate breakouts | Alternate setups shot inside the same production | Form updated January 2023 |
| The labor agreement | US performers' union commercials agreement | One commercial, for session-fee purposes | Alternate scenes shot for that commercial in the same session, and edits or lifts inside defined parameters | Effective 1 April 2025 |

**Definition the article should adopt and state plainly.** One finished asset is
one deliverable file, in one specified format and one specified duration or
dimension, approved for use and cleared for the channel it will run on. Under
that definition a resize is a new asset, a language version is a new asset and
an alternate cut is a new asset, while an alternate take shot in the same
session is not. The article must say it is adopting this definition because no
industry body publishes one, and show the three conventions above so the reader
watches the choice being made rather than assumed.

Every published price collected here is quoted against its own card's unit, and
the article must say what that unit was on each card. That is the single most
important honesty move in the piece.

|---|---|---|

| Sets, props, wardrobe | Sections E, G and H, props wardrobe and animals, art department labor, art department expenses | Art department labor and art department expenses are separate sections |
