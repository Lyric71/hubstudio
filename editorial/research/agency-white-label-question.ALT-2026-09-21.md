managing partner deciding this quarter whether to buy production capacity
under their own brand. The deliverable is a document structure they can hand
to counsel tomorrow, not a legal opinion.

usable tables and instruments named by number. Depth, not length, is the bar

## Cleared for use

Ready to drop into blockquotes in the exact attribution form they will carry.

> A specially ordered or commissioned work is a work made for hire only if it
> satisfies all four criteria: it falls within one of nine listed categories,
> there is a written agreement, the parties expressly agree it is a work made
> for hire, and all parties sign. "If a work fails to satisfy any of these
> requirements, it is not a work made for hire."
> Source: US Copyright Office, Circular 30, Works Made for Hire, revised
> August 2024, the registering office restating the statutory test.
> https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ30.pdf

> The nine categories are a contribution to a collective work, a part of a
> motion picture or other audiovisual work, a translation, a supplementary
> work, a compilation, an instructional text, a test, answer material for a
> test, and an atlas.
> Source: 17 U.S.C. 101, read against US Copyright Office Circular 30,
> August 2024, the enumerated list matched across both surfaces.
> https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/101

> Notice of the trade-secret immunity must appear in any contract or agreement
> with an employee that governs the use of a trade secret or other confidential
> information, and "employee" includes any individual performing work as a
> contractor or consultant. Without the notice, exemplary damages and attorney
> fees are unavailable against the person who never got it.
> Source: 18 U.S.C. 1833(b)(3) and (b)(4), applying to contracts entered into
> or updated after 11 May 2016, statutory text.
> https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1833

> Ownership of a commissioned work is settled by the contract between the two
> parties. Where the contract does not clearly provide for it, or where there
> is no contract at all, copyright belongs to the commissioned party.
> Source: Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China, Article 19, third
> amendment adopted 11 November 2020, statutory text on the state council's
> own surface.
> https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647633.htm

> Where copyright in a commissioned work sits with the creator, the
> commissioning party may use the work within the agreed scope, and where no
