**The bar:** the ranking set is short (600 to 1,500 words), vendor-authored,
and carries zero tables that a producer could fill in. Not one page names an
instrument with an article number, an effective date and a retention period in
the same document. Every page treats "audit trail" as system telemetry. The
bar to beat is 2,000 words with a per-asset field schema traceable to a
published standard, a three-regime comparison table, and one filled worked
example.

**The gap, in one sentence:** Everything ranking for this query logs an AI
system (agents, models, prompts, tokens) or summarizes one regime at a time,
so nobody has published the per-asset record a creative production workflow
keeps, field by field, that answers China, the EU and a US state statute from
a single row.

**H1 consequence:** the ranking pages all miss the asset, so the per-asset
record is the H1, not the regulation and not the logging stack.
