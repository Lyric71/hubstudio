Specifications for Carousel Ads
Last updated: September 2026

Each Carousel format has requirements for the image, music, or ad you select. Learn more about Carousel Ads.




	

For Standard Carousel

	

For VSA Carousel




Image Requirements

	

- min 2 - max 35 images

- JPG/.JPEG or .PNG

- File size ≤100 KB suggested

- Image resolution:

- Horizontal 1200*628px | Square 640*640px | Vertical 720*1280px

	

- Minimum images shown: 2

- Maximum images shown: 20




Music Requirements

*See Music Options for Carousel Ads below

	

- Music is a must (will play loop playback)

- Duration >=2s,

- Supported type: .mp3

- Can support both CML music and music upload (File size: Up to 10M; Music format: MP3)

	

- Music is a must (will play loop playback)

- Duration >=2s,

- Supported type: .mp3

- Can only support music upload (File size: Up to 10M; Music format: MP3)




Ad Requirements

	

- Only allows one ad caption and call-to-action for all images

- One URL for all images (Not powered by a feed/product catalog)

	

- Only allows one ad caption and call-to-action button

- Each product image can be led to a unique product link (powered by Catalog)



Music Options for Carousel Ads on TikTok and TikTok Ad Network (formerly TikTok Pangle) Placements

Note: CML (Commercial Music Library) is not available for TikTok Ad Network Carousel Ads placement.




Music is essential for Carousel Ads. You can either choose music from the CML or upload your own.

CML music, however, is not available for TikTok Ad Network placements. If you select CML music for your Carousel Ads, it will only play on TikTok, making the ads silent for TikTok Ad Network placement. For carousel ads for TikTok Ad Network placement only, we only support uploaded music, and CML music options are disabled in the Ads Manager platform.




Music Options by Placement

In the TikTok Ad Manager platform, during Carousel Ads creation, you can select music from the Music Builder page, which offers four different options for music sources. Click on one of these options to choose the music for your Carousel Ads.

All music provided by the platform, unless uploaded by you, comes from the Commercial Music Library (CML).

For You: Contains music from the Commercial Music Library (CML), which can be used on TikTok but is unavailable for TikTok Ad Network Carousel Ads placement.

Liked: Includes music from CML, which can be used on TikTok but is unavailable for TikTok Ad Network Carousel Ads placement.

History: Features a mix of CML music, which can be used on TikTok but not on TikTok Ad Network, and uploaded music, which is available for use on both TikTok and TikTok Ad Network.

Uploaded: Advertisers can upload their own music, which is compatible with both TikTok and TikTok Ad Network placements.




Avoiding Silent Carousel Ads

Option 1: To have consistent music across both TikTok and TikTok Ad Network, upload your own music instead of using CML music.

Option 2: If you prefer using CML music, create two separate Carousel Ads:

For TikTok: Select "TikTok only" at the Ad group level and choose CML music at the Ads level.

For TikTok Ad Network: Select "TikTok Ad Network only" at the Ad group level and upload your own music at the Ads level.
