How to post a video on TikTok  •  How to post a photo on TikTok  •  How to post text on TikTok • Troubleshooting
You can create different types of posts on TikTok, including video, photo, and text posts.
Important things to know about making posts on TikTok:
•  You can choose who can view your posts on TikTok.
•  Your new posts will retain your previous post's privacy setting. For example, if you select "Only you" for the privacy setting of your previous post, the post you upload after that will automatically apply the same setting.
How to post a video on TikTok
TikTok app
1. In the TikTok app, tap the Add post + button at the bottom.
2. Select a maximum length for your video. You can also tap Upload to add videos from your device, then continue to step 4.
3. Tap the Record button, and when you're finished, tap the Continue ✓ button at the bottom.
4. Add a filter, sound, text, stickers, effects, and more, or use advanced editing tools.
5. Tap Your Story to post to your Stories or Next to make additional edits, including:
  ༚  Add description
  ༚  Add hashtags
  ༚  Tag people
  ༚  Add a location
  ༚  Add link
  ༚  Manage privacy settings
6. Tap Post to publish your video, or tap Drafts to save it. Your drafts are visible only to you on your profile.
Web browser
1. Click Upload at the top.
2. Click Select video to upload or Select file.
3. Choose the video you'd like to upload.
Note: When you upload a video on your web browser, your entire video will be uploaded. To change the length of the video, upload your video in the TikTok app.
Learn more about how to manage who can view your posts.
How to post a photo on TikTok
To post a photo from your device:
1. In the TikTok app, tap the Add post + button at the bottom.
2. Tap Photo, then tap Upload to select photos from your device. You can add up to 35 photos.
3. Tap Next. Swipe to switch photos and make edits. You can add a filter, sound, text, stickers, or crop your photos.
4. Tap Your Story to post to your Stories or Next again to make additional edits, including:
  ༚  Edit cover
  ༚  Add description
  ༚  Add hashtags
  ༚  Tag people
  ༚  Add a location
  ༚  Add link
  ༚  Manage privacy settings
5. Tap Post to publish your post, or tap Drafts to save it. Your drafts are visible only to you on your profile.
To post a live photo:
1. In the TikTok app, tap the Add post + button at the bottom.
2. Tap the Record button to take a photo.
3. Add a filter, sound, text, stickers, effects, and more, or use advanced editing tools.
4. Tap the post settings at the bottom to manage who can view your post.
5. Tap Your Story to post to your Stories or tap the Done ✓ button at the bottom to post to the feeds.
How to post text on TikTok
1. In the TikTok app, tap the Add post + button at the bottom.
2. Tap Text.
3. Enter your text and adjust the text size, style, alignment, font, and color. Tap Done to continue.
4. Tap Stickers to add stickers, emojis, or search for animated GIFs.
5. Drag to move the sticker. To delete, drag it to the Delete button at the top of the screen until it's selected, then release.
6. Make additional edits, such as the background color and post settings.
7. Tap Post to feed or Your Story to complete. Otherwise, you can tap the Drafts button to save and edit it later.
Troubleshooting
If you're having trouble uploading a post, try these steps:
1. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
2. Clear the app video cache.
3. Restart the app and your device.
4. Make sure you're running the latest version of TikTok.
If you can't find a newly uploaded post on your profile:
1. Check the video's privacy settings. Only public videos are displayed under the posts tab.
2. To find your private videos, tap the Lock icon on your profile.
If your camera screen is blacked out:
A black camera screen may mean that the camera and mic have not been enabled in your device settings. To enable the TikTok camera and mic:
1. Open your device or system settings.
2. Tap on the TikTok app.
3. Turn on both the Camera and Mic.
If the problem persists, exit out of any other camera apps and restart TikTok.
