About Carousel Ads in TikTok Ads Manager
Last updated: September 2026

Carousel Ad is an ad format that supports images for TikTok-in-feed ads. Multiple images display in order, and people can manually choose content and convert by swiping through images. Carousel Ads are designed to provide an alternative ad format to video if you don't have video creatives available or you want to reuse creative assets from other platforms.



Availability

Available regions: All regions where performance auction is available.

Supported ad types:

Traffic

Web Conversions

App Promotion

Lead Generation

Product Sales - Catalog

Brand Auction Reach

Placement: Both automatic placement and TikTok only placement

Note: When you select automatic placement, it will filter out TikTok Ad Network and Global App Bundle traffic.

Supported ad format: Spark Ads and Non-Spark Ads for Standard Carousel. Non-Spark Ads only for VSA Carousel.



Benefits

Personalized product recommendations: Dynamically display products based what people are most likely to engage with.

Showcase multiple products: Highlight a variety of products in a single ad. Each frame within the carousel links to a unique landing page, providing users with more options and increasing the potential for conversions.

Lower creative production cost: Create scalable campaigns without requiring extensive creative production.



Next steps

You can select Carousel under the Ad Format section while creating manual Catalog Ads. For Smart+ Catalog Ads, a Carousel is created automatically.
