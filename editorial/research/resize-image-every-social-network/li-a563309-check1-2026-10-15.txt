Image specifications for your LinkedIn Pages and Career Pages
Last updated: 2 months ago

When updating your LinkedIn Page images, we recommend the following specifications. All images must be PNG or JPEG files and have a maximum size of 3MB.

Tab	Module	Minimum image size	Recommended image size
Page	Logo image	268 (w) x 268 (h) pixels	400 (w) x 400 (h) pixels
Page	Cover image	1512 (w) x 256 (h) pixels	1512 (w) x 256 (h) pixels
Life	Main image	1128 (w) x 376 (h) pixels	1128 (w) x 376 (h) pixels
Life	Custom modules	502 (w) x 282 (h) pixels	502 (w) x 282 (h) pixels
Life	Company photos	264 (w) x 176 (h) pixels	900 (w) x 600 (h) pixels
Important to know

The Life tab modules are only available if you’ve enhanced your LinkedIn Pages with Career Pages. The dynamic cover image slideshow is available with a Premium Company Page subscription.

Since members view LinkedIn Pages on different devices and screen sizes, consider the following tips when adding images to your LinkedIn Page or Career Page. 

To achieve the best image resolution, use the recommended dimensions and choose a high-resolution JPEG instead of a PNG file.

Your cover image might be adjusted to fit the screen, which might trim the image horizontally or vertically. We recommend uploading a high-quality image with limited text to ensure an optimal display on all devices and screen sizes.
You should place key details away from the edges of your cover photo, especially in the lower-right corner. Since images may get cropped on different screen sizes, placing important information in the center ensures that it is always visible.
Your logo image will be displayed on both light and dark backgrounds. If your logo is transparent, it will be shown on a white background. We recommend testing your logo to ensure it displays correctly on both light and dark backgrounds.
If your dynamic cover image appears blurry or pixelated, please choose an image with a larger file size. We recommend using photos, which look clearer than images with logos. If your image is still blurry or pixelated, you could use a compression tool such as Trimage for Windows or ImageOptim for Mac before uploading it to LinkedIn.

You can also use Vimeo or YouTube URLs for the main image or video and custom module sections of your Life tab. The static image for the video is taken from the thumbnail image on Vimeo or YouTube. To change the static image, update the thumbnail for that video on Vimeo or YouTube.

Here's a tip

Members can click an image from the Company photos section of the Life tab to view a larger version of the photo. If you follow the minimum image size guidelines, rather than the recommended guidelines, the image will likely appear small when a member click it.

Custom image specifications

When adding custom images to a page post with a URL, consider the following image display guidelines:

Use a 1.91:1 ratio (1200 x 627 pixels).

Image must be more than 200 pixels wide.

If your image width is less than 200 pixels, it will appear as a thumbnail on the left side of the post.
This doesn’t apply if you’re posting an image only or an image and text. The image will be enlarged to fit the feed.

Images on mobile won’t be cropped. Images of other ratios will appear in full size with subtle white padding.
