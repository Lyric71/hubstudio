Add custom thumbnails on YouTube

Custom video and Shorts thumbnails let your audience view a quick snapshot of your content. You can choose from auto generated suggested options, or upload your own if your account is verified. Make sure your thumbnail follows our Community Guidelines.

Note: Custom thumbnails for Shorts are currently only available to add in YouTube Studio on a computer. Your account must be verified to use this feature.

Add a custom video thumbnail
Sign in to YouTube Studio.
From the left menu, select Content .
Go to the video you’d like to edit  click .
Below “Thumbnail,” click Upload file to create a custom video thumbnail from an image on your device or select Get suggestions to generate a thumbnail.
Click SAVE.
Add a custom Short thumbnail
Sign in to YouTube Studio.
From the left menu, select Content   Shorts at the top.
Go to the Short you’d like to edit  click .
Below “Thumbnail,” click Upload file to create a custom Shorts thumbnail from an image on your device.
For uploaded files, the recommended aspect ratio is 9:16.
Click SAVE.
Note: It may take time for your thumbnail changes to appear on YouTube.

Generate thumbnails using Ask Studio

Use Ask Studio to get suggestions on custom thumbnails for your videos. Ask Studio analyzes your video’s transcript and themes to suggest thumbnails that are tailored to your video.

To generate a thumbnail:

Open Ask Studio within your publishing workflow.
Enter a prompt like "Create a thumbnail."
In the chat box, click Add  to select your video for suggestions or upload reference images.
Use follow-up prompts to make any changes.
For example, you can prompt Ask Studio to make the text on your thumbnail larger.
Review the generated suggestions.
