Labeling and identifying AI content is different for ads.
As more generative AI tools become available, it’s important to be able to recognize when something may have been created or modified with generative AI.
Why label your AI-generated content on Instagram
Labeling your AI-generated or AI-modified content on Instagram helps establish transparency and trust. In some cases it’s required. Learn more about this requirement in the Transparency Center.
Content may be labeled automatically when it contains AI signals, or you can label AI-generated or AI-modified content when you share it on Instagram.
How AI content is identified on Instagram
There are many AI tools that can create or modify content. Some content that is made or modified using one of these tools may have an industry-standard signal that identifies the content as being created or modified using AI tools. For example, you might create an image collage using a common photo editing tool to share with your followers.
Photo editing tools that use AI to change the size or color of an image may add a signal showing that the content was modified using AI. These signals are read by Meta’s systems to determine if the content needs a label.
When content will have a label
When Meta AI tools are used. Content that is created or modified using Meta AI tools and shared to Instagram directly as a post, story, or reel may automatically be labeled above or on the content. For example, some content may have a visible watermark that says Imagined with AI. Content that is created using Meta AI tools, downloaded to a device and then uploaded to Instagram will also receive an AI content label.
When created content has AI signals. Content with industry-standard signals indicating that it was created with AI will be labeled with AI content. This includes content that is created using third-party AI tools.
Content with industry-standard signals indicating that it was modified with AI will not receive an AI content label directly on the post, story, reel or thread.
Information about AI used to create or modify content can be found by clicking the Options menu on any post, story, reel or thread, regardless of the type of signal Meta receives.
This approach will evolve as people’s expectations and the technology evolve.
Note: Not all AI content contains the information needed to identify it.
When the AI content label is required
Meta requires you to label content you share that has photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio that has been digitally generated or altered, including with AI. This means that if a digital creation or AI tool was used to create or modify this kind of content, you must label the content before you share it.
Meta does not require you to label images that have been created or altered with AI. Images will still receive a label if Meta’s systems detect they were AI-generated.
Note: There may be penalties if you do not label content as required.
Here are some examples of digitally created content that requires a label:
A video that appears realistic of a group of people walking around an outdoor market
An audio file of two people talking
A song created using AI-generated vocals
A reel narrated with a realistic AI-generated voiceover
Here are some examples of digitally created content that does not require a label:
Video of an outdoor landscape, created in a style resembling a cartoon
Image of person riding a bull (Meta does not require you to label images)
You can read more about this requirement in the Transparency Center.
How to label your Instagram content or profile as AI
When you label your content, people will see an AI content label on your post. When you label your profile, people will see an AI-generated profile label on your profile and alongside your content if your specific media doesn’t already have a label indicating that it was created or edited with AI.
Note: If you choose not to identify AI use in the content you share, an AI content label may be applied if Meta detects that your content was AI-generated or AI-edited.

Label AI-generated posts
Create a post.
Tap Toggle next to Add AI label to label your content.
Label your profile as AI-generated profile
Go to your profile.
Tap Edit profile.
Tap Toggle next to AI-generated profile to label your profile.
Learn more about the AI-generated profile label.
Label AI-generated Stories
Share a photo or video to your Instagram story.
Tap Options in your story, then tap Label AI.
Tap Toggle next to Add AI label to label your content.
Label AI-generated Reels
Create a reel.
Tap Toggle next to Add AI label to label your content.
