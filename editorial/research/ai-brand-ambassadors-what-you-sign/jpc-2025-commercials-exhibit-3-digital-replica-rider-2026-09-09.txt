              SAG-AFTRA COMMERCIALS CONTRACT DIGITAL REPLICA RIDER

Instructions:  Producer to complete this Rider as follows:

            Section 1: Producer seeks consent to create a Digital Replica. Any use thereof is subject to Section 3.

            Section 2: Producer seeks consent to use a pre-existing Digital Replica. Any use thereof is subject to
            Section 3.

            Section 3: Sets forth the details regarding Producer's permitted use(s) of the newly-created or
            pre-existing Digital Replica as detailed in this Rider.

            Table 1: Provides a reasonably specific description of Producer's intended use(s) of the Digital Replica.

Once complete, Producer should provide to Performer within the timeframes outlined in the 2025 Commercials Contract
to initial and execute. Subject to the terms of the 2025 Commercials Contract, Performer may refuse to consent to the
creation and/or use of a Digital Replica by modifying and initialing their modifications or declining to sign.

3. Use of the Digital Replica in Commercials.

         a. [___]  Use in Commercial for Which Performer is Already Engaged. Performer is granting consent
              for use of the Digital Replica as set forth in Table 1 of this Rider. For each such use, Producer must
              provide the date that performer was engaged for the commercial where indicated.

         b. [___]  Use in Additional Commercials. Performer is granting consent for use of the Digital Replica
              to generate performance(s) in future commercials for which Performer has not yet been engaged as set
              forth in Table 1 of this Rider. Producer should leave blank the column titled "Date Performer
              Engaged" or enter "not previously engaged." Pursuant to the 2025 Commercials Contract
              Memorandum of Agreement, the use of the Digital Replica to generate a performance in commercials
              for which Performer has not yet been engaged constitutes engagement of the Performer.

         c. [___] Copy of Commercial. By checking this box, Performer requests that Producer provide
              Performer with a copy of any commercial that contains a performance generated using their Digital
              Replica, pursuant to the 2025 Commercials Contract Memorandum of Agreement.
