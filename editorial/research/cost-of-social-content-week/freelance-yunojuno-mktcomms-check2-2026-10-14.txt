Introduction

Marketing & Communications contractor rates are shaped by the growing importance of brand visibility, audience engagement, performance marketing, communications, and customer insight across digital channels.

AI is increasingly being integrated into marketing and communications workflows, particularly across content creation, analytics, campaign optimization, research, and audience targeting, although adoption remains focused primarily on improving speed, efficiency, and workflow enhancement.

Rates + Experience

Within this discipline, pricing varies according to channel specialization, strategic responsibility, stakeholder exposure, research complexity, and campaign scope. As organizations continue to invest in digital marketing, brand positioning, communications, customer insight, and audience growth, demand spans both hands-on execution and more strategic, insight-led roles focused on performance, reputation, and long-term business value.
