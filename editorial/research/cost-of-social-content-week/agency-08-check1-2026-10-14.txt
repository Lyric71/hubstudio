How Our Social Media Management Service Works

We keep the process simple and transparent:

1. Quick call & clear quote

You tell us about your business, platforms and goals. We talk through options and give you a clear monthly cost based on your chosen package.

2. Brand & content handover

You share your branding, any existing content, offers and key messages. If you don’t have much yet, we’ll guide you on exactly what we need.

3. Content ideas & sample posts

We propose content ideas and draft example posts so you can see the style, visuals and tone before anything goes live.

4. Short approval phase

For the first few weeks, you approve posts so we can fine-tune tone and messaging. Once you’re comfortable, we can agree a process whereby you can maintain whatever level of control you’d like or move towards autonomous posting.

5. Ongoing management

We continue planning, creating and scheduling posts each month. You can add reporting if you’d like regular summaries of what’s working.

We can usually get you started as soon as we’ve had an initial call and you’ve shared enough content for us to represent your business properly.
