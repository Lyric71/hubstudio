Introduction

Creative contractor rates reflect a broad spectrum of work, from high-volume content production through to concept development, multimedia storytelling, and creative leadership.

AI is increasingly being integrated into creative workflows, particularly in areas such as generative content, ideation, editing, and production support, although its impact on rates remains limited.

Rates + Experience

Pricing within this discipline is shaped by creative ownership, seniority, technical execution, and the scope of work delivered. As organizations continue to invest in brand, video, content, and creative output, demand spans both high-volume production work and higher-value creative directions.
