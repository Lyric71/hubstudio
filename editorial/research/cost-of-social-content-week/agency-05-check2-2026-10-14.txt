Our Social Media Package Process
Step 1: Package Selection & Goal Review

We review your business goals, preferred platforms, posting requirements and budget before helping you select the most suitable social media management package.

Step 2: Profile Audit & Content Planning

We review your profiles, audience, competitors, branding and existing content, then plan suitable topics, formats, hashtags and a monthly publishing calendar.

Step 3: Content Creation & Publishing

We create platform-specific captions and branded visuals, request approval when agreed, and schedule posts across the social networks included in your package.

Step 4: Monitoring & Monthly Reporting

We monitor reach, engagement, audience activity and content performance, provide a monthly report and refine future posts using available platform insights.
