Hidden Social Media Management Costs

Understanding and knowing your monthly marketing expenditures should be straightforward in an ideal world. However, if you opt for the services of a social media management agency, additional charges come into play. Let’s delve into the potential costs.

Social Media Advertising Costs

Paid social media ads cost money, including expenses related to creating the ad, paying for clicks or impressions, and targeting specific demographics. The cost varies depending on the platform, the target audience, and the type of ad used.

Influencer Costs

When working with influencers for brand promotion, you must consider their fees, which vary depending on the influencer’s reach and engagement.

Onboarding Fee Costs

Social media management companies may charge an initial onboarding fee, including setting up accounts, developing a strategy, and creating content. The fee is usually a one-time charge for new clients.

Additional Channel Costs

Additional fees may apply for those requiring the management of more social media channels than what’s included in their package. As an example, X charges businesses for access to its API.

Posting Frequency Costs

Some packages restrict the number of posts allowed per month. Should you wish to post more frequently, an upgrade to your package may be necessary. Increased posting frequency leads to more fees.

User-Generated Content (UGC) Costs

To effectively leverage user-generated content in your campaign, invest in a platform that can collect and manage it. While UGC is cost-effective, it needs close monitoring for quality and relevance, and compensating users for their content is a priority.

Video Content Creation Costs

Developing high-quality video content can be expensive, often requiring professional equipment and editing software.

Channel Upgrade Costs

Paid social media upgrades like Facebook’s Meta Verified Subscription offer additional features that can improve business visibility. Still, they come at an extra cost and add to overall social media management expenses.

Customer Support Costs

Using social media for customer support may require hiring more staff or investing in a dedicated customer service platform as your presence on social media expands, and the number of inquiries and support requests increases.

Photo or Video Editing Software and Equipment Costs

Quality photo or video editing software is a necessary investment for creating visually appealing content. Professional editing software remains essential even if you’re not generating your video content. However, this software can be expensive and require additional equipment, such as a camera or microphone.

Take note that not all businesses may incur these costs. Nonetheless, it’s important to factor in these elements when budgeting for social media management to prevent unforeseen expenses.

How Much Will Social Media Management Make for My Business?

Expected ROI of Social Media Management Services

Calculating your social media ROI involves aligning collected data with your goals. Key social media ROI metrics include impressions, engagements, and link clicks. Compare these metrics with the cost of your campaign to determine your ROI.

Here’s a simple formula:

Social media ROI = ((Value generated from social media – Costs of social media investment) / Costs) x 100

An ROI percentage greater than 0 indicates that your social media efforts have yielded more value than the resources invested. However, this formula may not be as effective if you’re not dealing in monetary values on both ends, a common scenario in social media.

For instance, if your social media ROI is monetary, your Key Performance Indicator (KPI) should reflect how well you meet your targets. When focusing on customer-centered activities, an improved social media ROI could manifest as increased engagements, extended reach, and enhanced consumer loyalty.

Remember that actual ROI can vary based on factors such as the effectiveness of your social media strategy, audience engagement rates, and the overall impact on your business. It’s a dynamic metric influenced by various elements.

Social Media Management Pricing FAQs

How much do you pay someone to manage social media?

You pay around $500 to $5,000 monthly to manage social media. The cost varies on the scope of work you require and your niche competition.

What is a good price for social media management?

A good price for social media management falls between $750 and $1,550 monthly. Factors like business size, work scope, and the manager’s experience influence the cost.

How much do small businesses spend on social media management?

Allocate about 8.7% of your small business’s annual revenue to social media marketing, with 12-18% for social media, depending on your business and industry.

How much does it cost to hire a social media marketer?

Freelance social media marketers charge $50 to $80 per hour. Social media managers’ average annual base salary is around $72,000+.

How much do beginner social media managers charge?

Beginner social media managers typically charge social media service fees ranging from $15 to $50 per hour. Monthly rates can vary from $300 to $1,000 based on workload and experience.

How much does it cost to outsource social media management on a small budget?

Basic outsourced social media management can start at $500 for tight budgets.

What does a social media management package include?

A basic social media management package includes social media strategy development, content creation, and basic analytics and reporting. LYFE Marketing’s services include a dedicated account manager, 24/7 analytics reporting, custom content creation, pre-approved content, monthly reporting, and more.

How much should a small business pay for social media management?

Small businesses can expect to pay $500 to $2,500 monthly for social media management.

How much does a SMM package cost?

A SMM package (social media management package) costs anywhere from $500 to $2,500 a month depending on the scope of work, the amount of platforms being managed and what is included in the package.

Is it worth paying for a social media manager?

Yes, a social media manager is worth paying for so long as they have experience and a track record for generating ROI like LYFE Marketing has.

How much do marketing agencies charge for social media management?

Marketing agencies charge anywhere from $500 to $2,500 a month for social media management.
