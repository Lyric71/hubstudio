On 4 December 2024, the joint committee voted to enter into interinstitutional negotiations on the basis of Parliament's first reading position. The first trilogue was held on 28 January 2025. The co-legislators presented their negotiating positions, and a mandate was given to the technical level to start work. The second trilogue was held on 24 April 2025. The negotiations focused on claims and labels and labelling schemes, information to consumers and verification, and access to justice and penalties.

On 20 June 2025, the Commission announced that it intends to withdraw the proposal. The third trilogue, scheduled for 23 June 2025, was cancelled.

The 2026 Commission Work Programme, adopted on 21 October 2025, lists the proposal as pending.
