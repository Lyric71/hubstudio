1) Ad Format and Functionality | TikTok Advertising Policies
URL: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-advertising-policies-ad-creatives-landing-page-ad-format-and-functionality?lang=en
Last updated (as shown): April 2026
Section title: "Language, currency and content relevance for target market"

"An ad's setting language is the ad targeting language, and an ad's country/location is the target market."

"The language, currency and any other relevant elements of your ad content and landing page must either be consistent with the setting or be acceptable/relevant for the target market and its local context."

Markets with a named language:
  Australia: "English"
  Japan: "Japanese" (note: "acceptance/relevance of language, currency and other elements of ad content and landing page are enforced")
  Brazil: "Portuguese; language requirement applies exclusively to LATAM markets for Mission Ads"

Group rule (France, Germany, Italy and Spain are all in this list):
"Albania, Andorra, Argentina, Armenia, Austria, Azerbaijan, Bahrain, Belarus, Belgium, Bolivia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Canada, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Egypt, Estonia, Finland, France, Georgia, Germany, Greece, Guatemala, Hungary, Iceland, Iraq, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Kosovo, Kuwait, Latvia, Lebanon, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Macedonia, Malta, Mexico, Moldova, Monaco, Montenegro, Morocco, Netherlands, Norway, Oman, Pakistan, Panama, Paraguay Peru, Poland, Portugal, Qatar, Romania, San Marino, Saudi Arabia, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sri Lanka, Sweden, Switzerland, South Africa, Taiwan, Turkey, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, United States:"
"We trust you to make the decision to use the language of the target market in ads, and to comply with applicable local laws, as well as ensure users have a good experience when accessing landing pages, including having the landing page in a language they understand."

US note: "For the US, language requirement is not enforced but recommended"

2) Review Ad Language | TikTok Advertising Policies
URL: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/ad-review-checklist-ad-language?lang=en
Last updated (as shown): August 2026

"We review each ad to make sure it aligns with the acceptable languages in the country/region where the ads are targeted."
Checklist:
  "All countries/regions selected in an ad group's Targeting > Location share at least one acceptable language"
  "Ad's text/caption matches one acceptable language in all the countries/regions targeted"
  "Spoken words and subtitles in your ad's video match the acceptable languages"
  "Language on any landing page or app store page linked to your ad matches the acceptable languages"

Reading: TikTok publishes no "official language of the targeted country"
rule for France, Germany, Spain or Italy. For those four markets it leaves the
language choice to the advertiser ("We trust you...") and points to local law.
Practical consequence from the checklist: one ad group targeting all four
countries needs a shared acceptable language across them; a per-language ad
group per market avoids the conflict.
