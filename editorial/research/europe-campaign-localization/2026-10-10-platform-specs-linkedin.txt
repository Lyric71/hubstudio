Capture: LinkedIn single image ad specs and ad language
Fetched: 2026-10-10 (WebFetch)
Note: quoted strings are verbatim from the pages.

1) Single image ads advertising specifications (Help Center)
URL: https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a426534/single-image-ads-advertising-specifications?lang=en
Last updated (as shown): "2 months ago" (about August 2026)
  File type: "JPG, PNG, or GIF. Animated GIF images must be 250 frames or shorter."
  File size: "5 MB"
  Horizontal/Landscape (1.91:1): "1200 x 628 pixels" recommended; "Minimum: 640 x 360 pixels; Maximum: 7680 x 4320 pixels"
  Square (1:1): "1200 x 1200 pixels" recommended; "Minimum: 360 x 360 pixels; Maximum: 4320 x 4320 pixels"
  Vertical (4:5): "720 x 900 pixels" recommended; "Minimum: 360 x 640 pixels; Maximum: 2430 x 4320 pixels"
  Introductory text: "Use up to 150 characters including spaces, emojis, and punctuation to avoid truncation (3,000 character maximum)"
  Headline: "Use up to 70 characters to avoid truncation (200 character maximum)"
  Description: "Use 100 characters to avoid truncation (300 character maximum)"
Country variation: none; one set of specs, no country selector.

2) Single Image Ads Specifications | Advertise on LinkedIn (marketing site)
URL: https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/ads/sponsored-content/single-image-ads-specs
No date shown.
  Ratios: "1.91:1 (horizontal, desktop and mobile)" | "1:1 (square, desktop and mobile)" | "1:1.91 (vertical, mobile only)"
  Headline: "70 characters"; Introductory text: "150 characters"; Description (LAN only): "70 characters"
Country variation: none; no country or region selector.
(The marketing page and the Help Center page differ slightly on the vertical
format; the Help Center page, updated more recently, recommends 4:5 at 720 x 900.)

3) Supported languages for LinkedIn Ads (Help Center)
URL: https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a427089
Last updated (as shown): "2 months ago"
  "We recommend that the language of the ad and landing pages should match the language you select."
  "When you select English, your ad set will target all member accounts in the location your ad set is targeting, regardless of the language member accounts have selected on their profiles."
  "After you create and launch an ad set, you won't be able to change the language. To use a different language, you'll need to create a new ad set."
  French, German, Spanish and Italian are in the supported list.
  "The Chinese language is not supported for LinkedIn Ad sets."

4) Ad guidelines for ad review and approval (Help Center)
URL: https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a468679
Last updated (as shown): "11 months ago"
  "Ad creative and landing page language should match the audience language selected in ad set audience settings."
