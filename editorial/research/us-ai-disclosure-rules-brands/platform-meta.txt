4. POLITICAL / SOCIAL ISSUE ADS: Business Help Center "About media created or edited with AI for ads about social issues, elections, or politics"
URL: https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1486382031937045
Page date: undated, read 2026-10-18
Read: 2026-10-18 (collapsed sections expanded and read)

Verbatim:
"Advertisers must disclose when a social issue, elections, or political ad contains a photorealistic image, video, or realistic sounding audio that was created or edited using AI tools to:
Depict a real person as saying or doing something they did not say or do.
Depict a realistic-looking person that does not exist or a realistic-looking event that did not happen, or alter footage of a real event that happened.
Depict a realistic event that allegedly occurred, but that is not a true image, video, or audio recording of the event."

"If you do not disclose these specified scenarios, your ad may be removed. Repeated failure to disclose may result in penalties on the account."

"Advertisers running these ads do not need to disclose when content is created or edited using AI in ways that are inconsequential or immaterial to the claim, assertion, or issue raised in the ad. This may include: Image size adjusting, Cropping an image, Color correction, Image sharpening"

"In Meta Ads Manager, ensure that Social issues, elections or politics is selected in the Special Ad Category section. Next, in the Ad creative section, select Includes media created or edited with AI."

"A label that says AI info will appear near the Paid for by disclaimer.
In the Ad Library, the Ad details section will include a disclosure stating "Media in this ad created or edited with AI.""

"Meta enables automated detection to identify when ad media for social issues, elections, or politics has been created or edited using third-party AI tools. This detection uses industry-standard technology to identify AI-generated content, and no advertiser action is required."

"Self-disclosure: You select Includes media created or edited with AI in Ads Manager. The AI info label appears on the ad near the Paid for by disclaimer.
Automated detection: Meta detects the use of third-party AI tools automatically. The AI Info label appears in the three-dot menu under About this Ad."

"You cannot request removal of automated detected labels."
"Meta's first-party generative AI creative tools remain unavailable for ads about social issues, elections, or politics."
"Automated detection does not change ad eligibility."

8. ORGANIC: Meta Help Center "How to identify AI content on Meta products"
URL requested: https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/how-ai-generated-content-is-identified-and-labeled-on-meta/
Redirects to: https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/1783222608822690/
Page date: "Updated: 10 weeks ago" (relative; about 2026-08-09 counting back from 2026-10-18)
Read: 2026-10-18

Verbatim:
"Content with signals we can identify indicating that it was created with AI will be labeled AI content. Content with signals indicating that it was modified using AI will not have the AI content label directly on the post, story, reel or thread. You can learn more about how the post, story, reel or thread was modified by AI by clicking the Options."

"If a photo editing tool uses AI to change the size or color of an image, then the image may have a signal that shows it was edited using AI. These signals are read by Meta's systems to determine if a label is applied to the content."

"You can see if a photo or video has been created or edited using AI tools with the Meta AI Detection tool." (link: https://meta.ai/identification)

"People using Meta products may also be able, and in some cases required, to label their content with AI content when they share AI-generated content or content that was modified using AI."

"Any content that is created or edited using Meta's AI tools and shared to Facebook, Instagram, or Threads as a post, story, reel, or thread may be automatically labeled as AI content or in some cases, feature a visible watermark."

"Some Instagram and Facebook accounts mostly create and post content that is AI-generated or modified. To help you understand what you're seeing, the creator can add an "AI creator" label to their profile."

"On Facebook, Instagram and Threads, Meta may require an AI label when content has photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created, modified or altered, including with AI. This requirement does not apply to images. However, images may still receive a label if the systems detect that they were AI-generated or if they were modified using AI."

"Note: There may be penalties for content shared without a label when it is required."

"Examples of digitally created content that requires a label on Facebook, Instagram and Threads: A video that appears realistic of a group of people walking around an outdoor market; An audio file of two people talking; A song created using AI-generated vocals; A reel narrated with a realistic AI-generated voiceover"

"Examples of digitally created content that does not require a label: Video of an outdoor landscape, created in a style resembling a cartoon; Image of a person riding a bull (Meta does not require a label for images)"

"Note: Not all AI content contains the information needed to identify it."

CHANGE vs 2026-10-08: the old slug now redirects to a page titled "How to identify AI content on Meta products", and that page names the organic label "AI content". Older Meta pages (Transparency Center Feb 2025, midterms post Feb 2026) still call it "AI info". For organic posts, cite "AI content" from this help page and note the older name; for ads the label is still "AI info".
