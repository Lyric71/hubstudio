1. Operation AI Comply
URL: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/09/ftc-announces-crackdown-deceptive-ai-claims-schemes
Title: "FTC Announces Crackdown on Deceptive AI Claims and Schemes"
Document date: September 25, 2024
Verbatim: "With Operation AI Comply, agency announces five law enforcement actions against operations that use AI hype"
Verbatim (Chair Khan): "Using AI tools to trick, mislead, or defraud people is illegal" ... "there is no AI exemption from the laws on the books"
Five cases, by type:
 - an "AI lawyer" service: claimed to be "the world's first robot lawyer" (AI capability / substitution-for-professional claim)
 - an AI writing tool with a "Testimonial & Review" generator: "Paid subscribers could generate an unlimited number of detailed consumer reviews" (means and instrumentalities for fake reviews); vote 3-2, Commissioners Holyoak and Ferguson dissenting
 - three business-opportunity schemes promising passive income from "AI-powered" online stores (earnings claims)
The release contains no requirement to disclose that content is AI-generated.
Rytr consent notice in FR: 89 FR 80565 (FR Doc. 2024-22767), October 3, 2024.
Rytr final order: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/12/ftc-approves-final-order-against-rytr-seller-ai-testimonial-review-service-providing-subscribers ; case timeline item dated December 18, 2024 (https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/rytr-llc-matter-timeline-item-2024-12-18).

2. The AI review-tool order was reopened and set aside
URL: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/12/ftc-reopens-sets-aside-rytr-final-order-response-trump-administrations-ai-action-plan
Title: "FTC Reopens and Sets Aside Rytr Final Order in Response to the Trump Administration's AI Action Plan"
Document date: December 22, 2025. Vote 2-0.
Verbatim: "the facts alleged in the complaint fail to support allegations that Rytr violated Section 5 of the FTC Act and that, because the order unduly burdens innovation in the nascent AI industry, it is in the public interest to set it aside."
Verbatim: "The Commission vote to issue the order reopening and setting aside the final decision and order was 2-0."
Case timeline: https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/rytr-llc-matter-timeline-item-2025-12-22
Meaning for the article: the FTC withdrew its theory that selling a general AI review-writing tool is itself a Section 5 violation. Businesses that write, buy or disseminate fake reviews remain covered by Part 465 (see ftc-consumer-reviews-rule-465.txt).

PATTERN (supported by the items above): 2025-2026 federal AI enforcement targets false or unsupported claims ABOUT AI (capability, accuracy, earnings) and fake reviews; none of these actions is about failing to label AI-generated ad content.
