TOPIC: Utah Artificial Intelligence Policy Act (SB 149, 2024) and the generative AI disclosure duties now in Utah Code Title 13, Chapter 77 (SB 226, 2025; SB 332, 2025; SB 38, 2026)
Read date (all sources): 2026-10-18

SOURCE 1 (official Utah Code, le.utah.gov)
13-77-103 "Required disclosures." (Effective 5/7/2025). Version file: https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title13/Chapter77/C13-77-S103_2025050720250507.html
Verbatim:
"(1)(a) A supplier that uses generative artificial intelligence to interact with an individual in connection with a consumer transaction shall disclose to the individual that the individual is interacting with generative artificial intelligence and not a human, if the individual asks or otherwise prompts the supplier about whether artificial intelligence is being used.
(b) The individual's prompt or question under Subsection (1)(a) must be a clear and unambiguous request to determine whether the interaction is with a human or with artificial intelligence.
(2) An individual providing services in a regulated occupation shall:
(a) prominently disclose when an individual receiving services is interacting with generative artificial intelligence in the provision of regulated services if the use of generative artificial intelligence constitutes a high-risk artificial intelligence interaction; and
(b) comply with all requirements of the regulated occupation when providing services through generative artificial intelligence.
(3) A disclosure required under Subsection (2) shall be provided:
(a) verbally at the start of a verbal interaction; and
(b) in writing before the start of a written interaction."
History note: "Enacted by Chapter 465, 2025 General Session" (SB 226).

13-77-101 "Definitions." (Effective 5/6/2026), "Amended by Chapter 95, 2026 General Session" (SB 38, 2026, Consumer Protection Modifications). Version file: https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title13/Chapter77/C13-77-S101_2026050620260506.html
Verbatim: ""Generative artificial intelligence" means an artificial intelligence technology system that: (a) is trained on data; (b) is designed to simulate human conversation with a consumer through one or more of the following: (i) text; (ii) audio; or (iii) visual communication; and (c) generates non-scripted outputs similar to outputs created by a human, with limited or no human oversight."

13-77-104 "Safe harbor." (Effective 5/7/2025). Verbatim: "A person is not subject to an enforcement action for violating Section 13-77-103 if the person's generative artificial intelligence clearly and conspicuously discloses: (a) at the outset of any interaction ... and (b) throughout the interaction that it: (i) is generative artificial intelligence; (ii) is not human; or (iii) is an artificial intelligence assistant."

13-77-105 "Enforcement." (Effective 5/7/2025). Verbatim: "the division director may impose an administrative fine of up to $2,500 for each violation of this chapter" and "A person who violates an administrative or court order issued for a violation of this chapter is subject to a civil penalty of up to $5,000 for each violation."

SOURCE 2 (official enrolled bill, sunset)
URL: https://le.utah.gov/Session/2025/bills/enrolled/SB0332.pdf
S.B. 332, Artificial Intelligence Revisions, 2025 General Session. Verbatim: "This bill extends the repeal date of the Artificial Intelligence Policy Act." and, amending 63I-2-213: "(5) Title 13, Chapter 72, Artificial Intelligence Policy Act, is repealed [May] July 1, [2025] 2027." Effective date clause: "This bill takes effect: (1) except as provided in Subsection (2), May 7, 2025".
=> The sunset of 1 July 2027 applies to Title 13, Chapter 72 (the Artificial Intelligence Policy Act: Office of AI Policy, learning lab). SB 332 does not list Chapter 77 (the disclosure duties).
The current text of 63I-2-213 could not be read on the read date (le.utah.gov returned 403/404 to our requests); whether any 2026 bill added a repeal date for Chapter 77 is NOT verified. Do not state that Chapter 77 has "no sunset" as fact; state that the 2027 repeal date in SB 332 is written for Chapter 72.

ONE LINE FOR THE ARTICLE: Utah requires a business using generative AI to talk to consumers to say so when a consumer clearly asks, and requires licensed professions to disclose up front in "high-risk" interactions (Utah Code 13-77-103, in force 7 May 2025). It is about conversational AI, not about AI images or video in ads.
