1. YOUTUBE ORGANIC: YouTube Help "Disclosing use of GenAI content"
URL: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en
Page date: undated, read 2026-10-18
Read: 2026-10-18 (expandable example lists read from the page source)

CHANGE vs 2026-10-08: the article is now titled "Disclosing use of GenAI content" (previously "Disclosing use of altered or synthetic content"), and the Studio setting is called "AI use" in the Attributes section.

Verbatim:
"We require creators to disclose AI-generated or altered content that seems realistic. Creators can make this disclosure during the upload process."

"Creators must disclose GenAI content that:
Makes a real person appear to say or do something they didn't do.
Alters footage of a real event or place.
Generates a realistic scene that didn't actually occur.
Creates music that's the main focus of the video.
This could include content that is fully or partially altered or created using AI tools."

"Realistic AI content and meaningful changes require disclosure, while non-realistic or minor edits don't."

Examples that must be disclosed (excerpt): "AI generated music"; "AI generated extra footage of a real place, like a video of a surfer in Maui for a promotional travel video"; "Making it appear as if someone gave advice that they did not actually give"

Examples that need no disclosure (excerpt): "Applying beauty filters"; "Color adjustment or lighting filters"; "Production assistance, like using generative AI tools to create or improve a video outline, script, thumbnail, title, or infographic"; "Caption creation"; "Video sharpening, upscaling or repair and voice or audio repair"; "Cloning one's own voice to create voice overs or dubs"; "AI generating or extending a backdrop to simulate a moving car"

How: "Go to YouTube Studio. Follow the steps to upload content. In the Attributes section, under "AI use," select Yes if your content meets the disclosure requirements or No if not."

Where the label appears:
"If creators select "Yes" to indicate that their content is realistic and generated by AI or meaningfully altered with AI, we'll add a label to their video."
"For AI-generated music or content that is photorealistic, a label in the video player may also appear."
"Labels may appear in the expanded description for non-music AI-generated audio or AI content that is non-photorealistic or animated."
"Note: Disclosing AI content alone won't limit a video's audience or impact its eligibility to earn money."

Automatic labels:
"YouTube may automatically apply an AI label on the video player or in the expanded description for:
Content made using YouTube's GenAI tools
Content that contains C2PA metadata
Content that our internal systems detect is Al generated or altered"
"You will receive a notification in YouTube Studio if an AI label has been automatically applied on or below the video player."
"However, content made with YouTube's AI tools, content containing C2PA metadata, or content labeled after manual review can not be adjusted."

Not disclosing:
"Creators who consistently choose not to disclose this information may be subject to manual application of a label, or penalties from YouTube, including removal of content or suspension from the YouTube Partner Program."

The page has no section on ads.

2. GOOGLE ADS (all advertisers): Google Ads Help "Use AI content label settings and disclosures"
URL: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17140115?hl=en
Page date: undated, read 2026-10-18
Read: 2026-10-18

Verbatim:
"Laws in some regions may require you to label ads that use certain assets created or edited with AI."

"The Ads AI label setting allows you to disclose assets as AI generated or edited. Ads that contain assets that you designate as AI-generated or edited will then include a disclosure in the "How this ad was made" section in the My Ad Center panel. This will be accessible globally and will provide details on AI usage in ads, including whether the assets used in the ads were AI created or edited. Users can click on the three-dot menu on ads across Search, YouTube and Discover."

"In some geographies, AI labels will also show on the ad itself. For campaigns that target the following locations, ads with assets that you designate as AI created or edited will also include visible overlays on the ads:
European Union
India
New York"

"Google may label your assets in some cases, such as in regions where Google itself is legally required to do so, or when we get signals to do so from other platforms."
"When you use Google's fully-automated features to create and serve creatives, assets may be labeled as AI created or edited on your behalf to assist with your compliance."
"In any of these cases, labels cannot be overwritten."

"Note: Use of the AI label setting in Google's advertising products doesn't guarantee your compliance with specific regulations. Seek legal guidance and take measures as needed to ensure your compliance."

"If you upload a video to YouTube from within Google Ads, you will be asked whether AI was used to generate or edit the video to comply with YouTube's AI disclosure policies for organic videos."

"When you use AI to edit or create assets for your ads, you can also apply your own AI labels to your assets using your own design tools. These labels won't be in violation of Google policies prohibiting text overlays and watermarks."

"Google applies machine-readable metadata to assets generated by Google AI tools. This includes embedding non-visible SynthID digital watermarks and C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity) open-standard markup into all images and videos generated within Google Ads tools."

5. GOOGLE ADS, ELECTION ADS: Advertising Policies Help "Political content", section "Disclosure requirements for synthetic or digitally altered content"
URL: https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6014595?hl=en
Page date: undated, read 2026-10-18
Read: 2026-10-18
Scope: "United States (US) Election Ads" and "State and local Election Ads in the United States" are listed among the regions where election ads require Google verification.

Verbatim:
"Google believes that users should have information to make informed decisions when viewing election ads that contain synthetic or digitally altered content, specifically images, video, or audio that inauthentically depict real or realistic-looking people or events."

"Ads that contain content that is synthetic or digitally altered in such a way that is inconsequential to the claims made in the ad aren't considered in scope of this policy. This includes editing techniques such as image resizing, cropping, color or brightening corrections, defect correction (for example, "red eye" removal), or background edits that do not create realistic depictions of actual events."

"An ad with synthetic content that makes it appear as if a person is saying or doing something they didn't say or do"
"An ad with synthetic content that alters footage of a real event or generates a realistic portrayal of an event to depict scenes that did not actually take place"

"Advertisers must disclose all election ads that contain synthetic or digitally altered content by selecting the checkbox in the "Altered or synthetic content" section in their campaign settings."

"For the following formats, Google will then generate an in-ad disclosure based on that checkbox, and advertisers are not required to include their own in-ad disclosure. Feeds on Mobile Phones; Shorts on Mobile Phones; In-stream (Computers, Mobile Phones, Mobile Web, and TV Screens)"

"For all other formats, the advertiser is responsible for selecting the checkbox and providing a prominent disclosure. The disclosure must be clear and conspicuous, and must be placed in a location where it is likely to be noticed by users."

Example wording: "Altered or synthetic content." / "This audio was computer generated." / "This image does not depict real events." / "This video content was synthetically generated."

"Violations of this policy won't lead to immediate account suspension without prior warning. A warning will be issued at least 7 days prior to any suspension of your account."
