TOPIC: Is there a federal (FTC) rule that requires labeling AI-generated ad content? Impersonation Rule (16 CFR Part 461) and the open dockets on AI impersonation
READ DATE (all items): 2026-10-18

ANSWER: No FTC rule, guide or policy statement found that requires an advertiser to label content as AI-generated. Checked: Part 255 (Guides), Part 465, Part 461, the Sept 2024 Operation AI Comply release, the FTC artificial intelligence index page, the Sept 10, 2026 BCP guidance-program post, and the FTC's FR documents mentioning "artificial intelligence" from June 2025 to date (federalregister.gov API: only 4 documents, none a labeling rule). The obligations are indirect: no deception (Section 5), no fake or non-existent reviewers/testimonialists (Part 465), no presenting non-consumers as "actual consumers" without disclosure (255.2(c)), no impersonation of government or businesses (Part 461).
FTC AI index page (https://www.ftc.gov/industry/technology/artificial-intelligence), WebFetch result: no statement on that page requires AI-generated ad content to be labeled.

1. Impersonation Rule, final
FR: 89 FR 15017 (FR Doc. 2024-04335), March 1, 2024; effective April 1, 2024 (federalregister.gov API effective_on 2024-04-01; Unified Agenda "Final Rule Effective Date 04/01/2024")
Codified: https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-10-15/title-16.xml?part=461 , "Source: 89 FR 15030, Mar. 1, 2024"
Verbatim 461.3: "It is a violation of this part, and an unfair or deceptive act or practice to: (a) materially and falsely pose as, directly or by implication, a business or officer thereof, in or affecting commerce ...; or (b) materially misrepresent, directly or by implication, affiliation with, including endorsement or sponsorship by, a business or officer thereof ..."
Covers government and businesses only; individuals are NOT covered by the final rule.
SNPRM footnote 98 (89 FR 15072 document), verbatim: "... make clear that the use of voice cloning for purposes of impersonation is covered where its use satisfies the Rule's prohibitions."

2. Proposed extension to impersonation of individuals (SNPRM)
Press release: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/02/ftc-proposes-new-protections-combat-ai-impersonation-individuals , February 15, 2024
Verbatim (WebFetch extraction; the original punctuation dashes are rendered here as " - "): "Emerging technology - including AI-generated deepfakes - threatens to turbocharge this scourge"
FR: 89 FR 15072 (FR Doc. 2024-03793), March 1, 2024. "Comments must be received on or before April 30, 2024."
Proposed definition, verbatim: "``Individual'' ... to mean ``a person, entity, or party, whether real or fictitious, other than those that constitute a business or government under this Part.''"
Informal hearing notice: 89 FR 104905 (FR Doc. 2024-30718), December 26, 2024; hearing January 17, 2025.

STATUS AT 2026-10-18: still open, no final action.
a) Unified Agenda, RIN 3084-AB71 (https://www.reginfo.gov/public/do/eAgendaViewRule?pubId=202510&RIN=3084-AB71; reginfo.gov, OMB/OIRA, read 2026-10-18), verbatim: "The Commission held an Informal Hearing on January 17, 2025. The Commission continues to explore and evaluate potential options for future action relating to this rule." Stage: "Proposed Rule Stage". Timetable line: "Recommendation to Commission 07/00/2026".
b) FTC ANPRM of October 1, 2026 (below), verbatim: "With respect to the impersonation of individuals provision, the Commission held an informal hearing in January 2025 and is still evaluating its options."
c) Means-and-instrumentalities (the part that would have reached AI tool providers): 89 FR 104905, quoted in the 2026 ANPRM, verbatim: the Commission "has decided not to proceed with the SNPRM's proposed means and instrumentalities provision at this time."

3. New open docket: ANPRM on platforms (October 2026)
URL: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/01/2026-20143/rule-on-impersonation-of-government-and-businesses
Raw text read: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/text/2026/10/01/2026-20143.txt
Citation: 91 FR 62347-62357 (FR Doc. 2026-20143), October 1, 2026, RIN 3084-AB90
Verbatim: "ACTION: Advance notice of proposed rulemaking (``ANPRM''); request for public comment."
Verbatim: "The Federal Trade Commission (``FTC'' or ``Commission'') proposes to commence a rulemaking proceeding to prevent certain unfair or deceptive acts or practices by search engine, social media, and other digital marketplace platforms that further government and business impersonation scams to defraud consumers."
Verbatim: "DATES: Comments must be received on or before November 30, 2026."
Verbatim: "any means-and-instrumentalities provision that the Commission considers in this proceeding will focus on ad-optimization practices by digital marketplace platforms"
Scope: platforms' ad systems and scam ads, not advertiser labeling of AI content.
