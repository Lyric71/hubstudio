1. SB 942 (2024), Stats. 2024, Ch. 291. Approved and filed 2024-09-19 (bill record: "CHAPTERED 2024-09-19 APPROVED 2024-09-19 FILED 2024-09-19").
=====================================================================
Original definition (verbatim): ""Covered provider" means a person that creates, codes, or otherwise produces a generative artificial intelligence system that has over 1,000,000 monthly visitors or users and is publicly accessible within the geographic boundaries of the state."
Original manifest-disclosure duty, 22757.3(a) (verbatim): "A covered provider shall offer the user the option to include a manifest disclosure in image, video, or audio content, or content that is any combination thereof, created or altered by the covered provider's GenAI system that meets all of the following criteria: (1) The disclosure identifies content as AI-generated content. (2) The disclosure is clear, conspicuous, appropriate for the medium of the content, and understandable to a reasonable person. (3) The disclosure is permanent or extraordinarily difficult to remove, to the extent it is technically feasible."
Original latent-disclosure duty, 22757.3(b): covered provider "shall include a latent disclosure in AI-generated image, video, or audio content ... created by the covered provider's GenAI system" conveying "(A) The name of the covered provider. (B) The name and version number of the GenAI system that created or altered the content. (C) The time and date of the content's creation or alteration. (D) A unique identifier."
Original penalty, 22757.4(a)(1): "A covered provider that violates this chapter shall be liable for a civil penalty in the amount of five thousand dollars ($5,000) per violation to be collected in a civil action filed by the Attorney General, a city attorney, or a county counsel." 22757.4(b): "Each day that a covered provider is in violation of this chapter shall be deemed a discrete violation."
Original 22757.6 (verbatim): "This chapter shall become operative on January 1, 2026."

2. AB 853 (2025), Stats. 2025, Ch. 674. Approved and filed 2025-10-13 (bill record: "CHAPTERED 2025-10-13 APPROVED 2025-10-13 FILED 2025-10-13").
=====================================================================
Legislative Counsel's Digest (verbatim): "Existing law makes the California AI Transparency Act operative on January 1, 2026. This bill would delay the operation of the California AI Transparency Act until August 2, 2026."
New 22757.6 (verbatim, SEC. 6): "This chapter shall become operative on August 2, 2026."
Current-code history note for 22757.6 (LAW_SECTION_TBL, verbatim): "Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 674, Sec. 6. (AB 853) Effective January 1, 2026." Current text (verbatim): "This chapter shall become operative on August 2, 2026." (Not changed by SB 1000.)

Large online platforms, new 22757.3.1 (verbatim excerpts): "(a) A large online platform shall do all of the following: (1) Detect whether any provenance data that is compliant with widely adopted specifications adopted by an established standards-setting body is embedded into or attached to content distributed on the large online platform. (2) (A) Provide a user interface to disclose the availability of system provenance data that reliably indicates that the content was generated or substantially altered by a GenAI system or captured by a capture device." ... "(c) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2027."
Definition (verbatim): ""Large online platform" means a public-facing social media platform, file-sharing platform, mass messaging platform, or stand-alone search engine that distributes content to users who did not create or collaborate in creating the content that exceeded 2,000,000 unique monthly users during the preceding 12 months."
GenAI hosting platforms, new 22757.3.2 (verbatim): "(a) A GenAI system hosting platform shall not knowingly make available a GenAI system that does not place disclosures pursuant to Section 22757.3. (b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2027."
Capture device manufacturers, new 22757.3.3 (verbatim excerpts): "(a) A capture device manufacturer shall, with respect to any capture device the capture device manufacturer first produced for sale in the state on or after January 1, 2028, do both of the following: (1) Provide a user with the option to include a latent disclosure in content captured by the capture device ... (2) Embed latent disclosures in content captured by the device by default." ... "(c) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2028."
Current-code history notes (verbatim): 22757.3.1 and 22757.3.2: "Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 674 ... (AB 853) Effective January 1, 2026. Operative January 1, 2027, by its own provisions." 22757.3.3: "Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 674, Sec. 4. (AB 853) Effective January 1, 2026. Operative January 1, 2028, by its own provisions."
Penalty as amended by AB 853, 22757.4 (verbatim): "(a) (1) A violator of this chapter shall be liable for a civil penalty in the amount of five thousand dollars ($5,000) per violation to be collected in a civil action filed by the Attorney General, a city attorney, or a county counsel. ... (b) Each day that a covered provider, large online platform, or capture device manufacturer is in violation of this chapter shall be deemed a discrete violation."

3. SB 1000 (2026), Stats. 2026, Ch. 861. URGENCY statute. Approved and filed 2026-09-30 (bill record: "CHAPTERED 2026-09-30 APPROVED 2026-09-30 FILED 2026-09-30"). In effect immediately.
=====================================================================
Title (verbatim): "An act to amend Sections 22757.1, 22757.2, 22757.3, 22757.4, and 22757.5 of, and to add and repeal Section 22757.4.1 of, the Business and Professions Code, relating to artificial intelligence, and declaring the urgency thereof, to take effect immediately."
Current-code history notes (verbatim, e.g. 22757.1): "Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 861, Sec. 2. (SB 1000) Effective September 30, 2026."
Legislative Counsel's Digest (verbatim): "This bill would recast those provisions to, among other changes, delete the user threshold from the definition of "covered provider," replace the term "AI detection tool" with "disclosure verification tool," delete the above-described requirement of a covered provider to offer the user the option to include a manifest disclosure in content, and additionally require a covered provider to include in the above-described latent disclosure whether the GenAI system created or altered the content."
New definition (verbatim): ""Covered provider" means a person that creates, codes, or otherwise produces a generative artificial intelligence system that is publicly accessible within the geographic boundaries of the state."
New "minor modification" definition (verbatim): ""Minor modification" means any of the following alterations: (1) A change to brightness, contrast, or color. (2) Sharpening. (3) Saturating. (4) File resizing. (5) Scaling. (6) Cropping. (7) File format conversions. (8) Denoising and removal of background noise in audio."
New 22757.3(a) (verbatim): "To the extent it is technically feasible, a covered provider shall include a latent disclosure that meets all of the following criteria in any image, video, or audio content, or content that is any combination thereof, created or altered, except by minor modification, by the covered provider's GenAI system: (1) The disclosure conveys all of the following information, either directly or through a link to a permanent internet website: (A) The name of the covered provider. (B) The name and version information of the GenAI system that created or altered the content. (C) The time and date of the content's creation or alteration. (D) A unique identifier. (E) Whether the GenAI system created or altered the content. (F) On and after January 1, 2029, whether the GenAI system is designed to primarily function as assistive technology. (2) The disclosure is permanent or extraordinarily difficult to remove or tamper with."
New 22757.4(c) (verbatim): "A civil action brought under this section before the effective date of the act adding this subdivision shall not be maintained if the alleged conduct does not violate this chapter on and after the effective date of the act adding this subdivision."
New 22757.4.1 (verbatim): "(a) (1) A violator of subdivision (d) of Section 22757.3 shall be liable for a civil penalty in the amount of fifty thousand dollars ($50,000) per violation ... (c) This section shall remain in effect until January 1, 2029, and as of that date is repealed." (22757.3(d): "A covered provider shall not falsely represent that a GenAI system is designed to primarily function as assistive technology.")
New 22757.5 (verbatim): "(a) This chapter does not apply to any product, service, internet website, or application that provides exclusively non-user-generated videogames. (b) Before January 1, 2029, this chapter does not apply to a GenAI system that is designed to primarily function as assistive technology."
SEC. 8 (verbatim): "This act is an urgency statute necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health, or safety within the meaning of Article IV of the California Constitution and shall go into immediate effect."

4. AB 2713 (2026), Stats. 2026, Ch. 856. NOT pending: chaptered. Approved and filed 2026-09-30 (bill record: "CHAPTERED 2026-09-30 APPROVED 2026-09-30 FILED 2026-09-30"). Not an urgency bill.
=====================================================================
Title (verbatim): "An act to amend Section 22757.3.1 of the Business and Professions Code, relating to artificial intelligence."
What it changes: rewrites the large-online-platform duties in 22757.3.1. New 22757.3.1(c) (verbatim): "A large online platform shall not, to the extent technically feasible, knowingly strip any system provenance data or digital signature from content uploaded to, distributed on, or downloaded from the large online platform." New (d) (verbatim): "This section does not require a large online platform to take any action with respect to provenance data, system provenance data, or digital signatures that are not compliant or interoperable with widely adopted specifications issued by an established standards-setting body." (e) (verbatim): "This section shall become operative on January 1, 2027."
Effective date: no urgency clause, so it takes effect 1 January 2027 under Cal. Const. art. IV, sec. 8(c)(1) (see state-ca-sb1050-synthetic-performers.txt for the verbatim constitutional text). That coincides with the section's own operative date of 1 January 2027.
NOTE: the coordinator's map called AB 2713 "pending". The official record says Chaptered, Ch. 856.

STATUS ON 2026-10-18 (our synthesis from the texts above)
=====================================================================
- The Act has been operative since 2 August 2026 (AB 853).
- Since 30 September 2026 (SB 1000, urgency): no 1,000,000-user threshold; no manifest (visible) disclosure option; latent disclosure required in AI-created or altered image, video, audio except "minor modification"; the free tool is now a "disclosure verification tool".
- 1 January 2027: large online platform duties (22757.3.1 as rewritten by AB 2713) and GenAI hosting platform duty (22757.3.2).
- 1 January 2028: capture device manufacturer duties (22757.3.3).
- 1 January 2029: assistive-technology exemption ends; latent disclosure must say whether the system is assistive technology; 22757.4.1 repealed.
- Penalty: $5,000 per violation, each day a discrete violation, civil action by the Attorney General, a city attorney or a county counsel.
- WHO IT BINDS: AI system makers (covered providers), large platforms, hosting platforms, capture device makers. It does NOT put a disclosure duty on an advertiser or brand that publishes AI content.
