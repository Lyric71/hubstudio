TOPIC: How many states regulate deepfakes in political messaging (tracker check)
Read date: 2026-10-18

SOURCE: National Conference of State Legislatures (NCSL), "Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Elections and Campaigns"
URL: https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns/artificial-intelligence-ai-in-elections-and-campaigns
Page date (verbatim): "Updated October 13, 2026"
Publisher: NCSL, a bipartisan organization serving state legislatures (neutral, but not a primary legal source).

Verbatim count: "Thirty-one states have enacted laws regulating the use of deepfakes in political messaging. States have generally taken two approaches: prohibitions and disclosures. Three states - California, Minnesota and Texas - prohibit the publication of political deepfakes a certain number of days prior to an election. . California only prohibits certain content, but allows other content so long as a disclosure is present. Maryland also has a prohibition but prohibits deceptive deepfakes related to the election year-round. The other 27 states require disclosures on the media"
Verbatim scope rule (the only method statement on the page): "Many states have also enacted broader impersonation of deepfake laws, although they do not specifically mention candidates or elections. For this reason, they are not included for the purposes of this page, although they may apply in certain election contexts."

METHOD ASSESSMENT
- Inclusion rule stated (election- or candidate-specific statutes only); no stated cut-off date beyond "Updated October 13, 2026"; it counts enacted laws, including laws a court has enjoined (e.g., California's).
- The page contains dating errors against the primary record: it says Kohls v. Bonta found the law unconstitutional "in September 2025" (the order is dated 29 August 2025, Document 101) and that "The legislature amended the law in September 2025" (AB 502 was chaptered on 19 September 2026, Stats. 2026, Ch. 333).
- Same page, verbatim: "Babylon Bee, LLC v. Lopez: The U.S. District Court of Hawaii found the state's law unconstitutional in January 2026." NOT verified at a court source in this run.
