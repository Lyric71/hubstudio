L50: ## SECTION 396-B
L51: 
L52: ### Advertisements
L53: #### General Business (GBS) CHAPTER 20, ARTICLE 26
L54: § 396-b. Advertisements. 1. For the purposes of this section, the
L55: following terms shall have the following meanings:
L56: (a) "Artificial intelligence", "artificial intelligence technology",
L57: or "AI" means a machine-based system that can, for a given set of
L58: human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommendations, or
L59: decisions influencing real or virtual environments, and that uses
L60: machine- and human-based inputs to perceive real and virtual
L61: environments, abstract such perceptions into models through analysis in
L62: an automated manner, and use model inference to formulate options for
L63: information or action. This definition includes but is not limited to
L64: systems that use machine learning, large language model, natural
L65: language processing, and computer vision technologies, including
L66: generative artificial intelligence.
L67: (b) "Generative artificial intelligence" means a class of artificial
L68: intelligence models that are self-supervised and emulate the structure
L69: and characteristics of input data to generate derived synthetic content,
L70: including, but not limited to, images, videos, audio, text, and other
L71: digital content.
L72: (c) "Synthetic performer" means a digitally created asset created,
L73: reproduced, or modified by computer, using generative artificial
L74: intelligence or a software algorithm, that is intended to create the
L75: impression that the asset is engaging in an audiovisual and/or visual
L76: performance of a human performer who is not recognizable as any
L77: identifiable natural performer.
L78: 2. Any person, firm, corporation or association, or agent or employee
L79: thereof, hereinafter called person, who, being engaged in the business
L80: of dealing in any property, makes, publishes, disseminates, circulates
L81: or places before the public or causes, directly or indirectly, to be
L82: made, published, disseminated, circulated or placed before the public,
L83: in this state, any advertisement respecting any such property, in any
L84: newspaper, magazine, or other publication, or over any radio station or
L85: television station, unless it is stated in any such advertisement that
L86: the advertiser is a dealer in such property or from the context of any
L87: such advertisement, it plainly appears that such person is a dealer in
L88: such property so offered for sale in any such advertisement; or when
L89: placing or causing any such advertisement to appear in any newspaper,
L90: magazine or other publication or radio or television station as
L91: described in this section, if requested by the publisher of any such
L92: newspaper, magazine or other publication or owner or operator of such
L93: radio or television station or any agent or representative thereof to
L94: file with such owner or operator, publisher, agent or representative
L95: thereof such person's true name, or where such person is transacting
L96: business under a name other than the true name pursuant to law, then the
L97: name under which such business is transacted, and each business address
L98: wherein any business is transacted by such person, in the class of
L99: property advertised or to be advertised for sale in such advertisement,
L100: shall make any false statement in relation to any of such items; or if
L101: requested by the publisher of any such newspaper, magazine or other
L102: publication or owner or operator of such radio or television station or
L103: any agent or representative thereof to file with such owner, operator,
L104: publisher, agent or representative thereof a statement showing whether
L105: such person is causing such advertisement to appear or is offering to
L106: make such sale or disposition or transaction, as herein set forth, as
L107: principal or agent, and if as agent, to set forth such information as is
L108: specified in this section, in relation to such person's principal as
L109: well as in relation to such person, shall make any false statement in
L110: relation to any of such items; is guilty of a misdemeanor.
L111: 3. Any person engaged in the business of dealing in any property or
L112: service who for any commercial purpose produces or creates an
L113: advertisement respecting any such property or service, in any medium or
L114: media in which such advertisement appears, shall conspicuously disclose
L115: in such advertisement that a synthetic performer is in such
L116: advertisement, where such person has actual knowledge. A violation of
L117: this subdivision shall result in a civil penalty of one thousand dollars
L118: for a first violation, and five thousand dollars for any subsequent
L119: violation.
L120: 4. This section shall not apply to advertisements and promotional
L121: materials for expressive works, including but not limited to, motion
L122: pictures, television programs, streaming content, documentaries, video
L123: games, or other similar audiovisual works, provided that the use of a
L124: synthetic performer in the advertisement or promotional material is
L125: consistent with its use in the expressive work.
L126: 5. Nothing in this section shall limit, reduce, or enlarge any rights
L127: any person may have under section fifty, fifty-f, or fifty-one of the
L128: civil rights law or under any other law.
L129: 6. Nothing in this section shall be construed to limit, or to enlarge,
L130: the protections that 47 U.S.C. section 230 confers on an interactive
L131: computer service for content provided by another information content
L132: provider, as such terms are defined in 47 U.S.C. section 230.
L133: 7. This section shall not apply to advertisements and promotional
L134: materials in any of the following circumstances:
L135: (a) Audio advertisements; or
L136: (b) Where the use of artificial intelligence solely involves the
L137: language translation of a human performer.
L138: 8. Nothing in this section shall apply to any medium used for
L139: advertising, including, but not limited to, newspapers, magazines, and
L140: television networks and stations, streaming services, cable television
L141: systems, billboards, and transit advertisements, by whom any
L142: advertisement or solicitation in violation of this section is published
L143: or disseminated.
