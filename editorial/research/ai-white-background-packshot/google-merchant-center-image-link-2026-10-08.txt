Image link [image_link]
Use the image link
[image_link]
attribute to add the primary image for your product. Your main image is the first thing potential customers see across Google channels. A high-quality image grabs shoppers' attention, builds trust, and helps your products stand out.
Important: We recently announced new image size requirements of at least 500 x 500 pixels for all products beginning January 31, 2027
. However, to ensure best performance in all listing formats, we recommend that you provide images 1500x1500 pixels or above.

Format
Follow these guidelines to format and submit image URLs correctly so Google can process, crawl, and display your products.
Size requirements
At least 500 x 500 pixels
When possible, we recommend that you provide images around 1500x1500 pixels or above to ensure best performance in all listing formats.
No image larger than 64 megapixels
No image file larger than 16MB
URL guidelines
Technical specifications
Type
: URL. Must start with
http://
or
https://
, contain
ASCII characters only
, and be
RFC 3986 compliant
.
Limits
: 1 – 2,000 characters.
Repeated field
: No. Submit only one URL in the image link
[image_link]
attribute. To provide additional images, use the additional image link
[additional_image_link]
attribute.
Schema.org property
:
Product.image
(Type: URL).

Important file format rules
Extension Matching
: The file extension must correspond to its actual format. Using incorrect file extensions (for example, using
.jpg
for a
.png
file) leads to 'Unsupported image type' errors.
Third-Party Syncing
: If images are synced from third-party platforms, verify they generate correct image URLs and supported file types.
URL encoding and crawl access
URL Encoding
: Replace symbols and spaces in your URL with standard URL-encoded entities. For example, replace an ampersand (
&
) with
%26
, and a comma (
,
) with
%2C
.
Crawlability
: Ensure your
robots.txt
file allows access to both
Googlebot
and
Googlebot-image
so Google can fetch your images.
Example setup:
User-agent: Googlebot
followed by
Allow: /
and
User-agent: Googlebot-image
followed by
Allow: /
.
Note that it may take 24-48 hours after updating
robots.txt
for changes to take effect.
Best practices and optimization
Use stable URLs
: Avoid dynamic parameters (for example, timestamps or session IDs) that change on every feed submission. Static URLs prevent unnecessary recrawling and server load.
Enable automatic image improvements
: Turn on automatic improvements in Merchant Center to allow Google to automatically fix minor image policy issues (like promotional overlays) and avoid disapprovals.
Image requirements
To prevent your product from being disapproved, ensure your image avoids the violations listed below. If your image doesn't meet these requirements, it won't appear on Google surfaces, and you'll see a notification in the Diagnostics section of your Merchant Center account.
Avoid image disapprovals
Don’t use an image that contains promotional elements or overlays
Your product image must offer an unobstructed, clean view of the item for sale. Adding post-production text, marketing callouts, or graphics distracts shoppers from your product and will cause an image disapproval. Keep your main image focused solely on the product itself.
What to avoid:
Calls to action
: Text prompting action, such as "Buy", "Shop now", or "Order today".
Price and shipping details
: Mentions of price, discounts, or offers like "Free shipping".
Service-related information
: Extended service or warranty claims, such as "Includes 2-year warranty".
Promotional adjectives
: Subjective marketing words, such as "Best", "Cheap", or "Top quality".
Condition or compatibility text
: Added labels describing state or fit, such as "New", "2-piece set", or "Adaptable".
Barcodes and tracking codes
: UPC barcodes, QR codes, or serial tags overlaid on the photo.
Overlays and watermarks
: Stock photo watermarks, copyright symbols, or graphic stamps.
Added brand, manufacturer, or retailer names and logos
: Superimposed logos or store names added in post-production (allowed only if they are an inherent, physical part of the product itself).
Don’t use a placeholder, generic image, or graphic that doesn't show your product
Your primary image must clearly display the exact item being sold. Placeholders and generic visuals prevent shoppers from seeing what they are buying, which leads to image disapprovals.
What to avoid:
Temporary or text-based placeholders:
Don't submit graphics with text such as "Coming soon", "No image available", or "Photo pending".
Store logos and icons:
Don't use your company logo, brand watermark, or stock icons as a substitute for a product photo.
Placeholders:
Don't use shared stand-in images or reuse one general photo across multiple distinct products where unique, individual images are required.
Generic graphics and illustrations:
Don't submit line art, mockups, or illustrations that don't depict the physical product.
Don't use a single-color image that is only a square of color.
Don't use an image with a border
. To ensure that your image works with a variety of design elements, don’t include borders around your image.
Don't include any product that's not sold together with the main product
.

AI image metadata
All images created using generative AI must contain meta data indicating that the image was AI-generated (for example, the IPTC
DigitalSourceTypeTrainedAlgorithmicMedia
metadata tag). Don't remove embedded metadata tags such as the IPTC DigitalSourceType property from images created using generative AI tools, for example
Product Studio
. The following IPTC NewsCodes specify the type of digital source that was used to create the image, and should be preserved:
TrainedAlgorithmicMedia
: The image was created using a model derived from sampled content.
CompositeSynthetic
: The image is a composite that includes synthetic elements.
AlgorithmicMedia
: The image was created purely by an algorithm not based on any sampled training data (for example, an image created by software using a mathematical formula).
Note
: If you're planning to upload an image of a product in its original packaging, make sure that the image clearly displays multidimensional product packaging and not just the front of the packaging.
Best practices
These are the best practices that can help you go beyond the basic requirements to optimize your product data performance.
Use an image that shows a clear view of the main product being sold
.
And meet all size requirements
At least 500x500 pixels
When possible, we recommend that you provide images around 1500x1500 pixels or above to ensure best performance in all listing formats.
No image larger than 64 megapixels
No image file larger than 16MB
Any additional views of the product can be submitted with the
additional image link
[additional_image_link]
attribute.
Submit a unique image that represents the distinguishing details of each variant
When selling products that come in multiple options, submit a distinct, dedicated image for every individual variant. Shoppers expect the image they see in ads and free listings to accurately reflect the exact version, color, and finish they are selecting.
Key guidelines for variant imagery:
Submit a unique image for each variant:
Don’t reuse one general photo across different product variations. Provide a separate, dedicated image for every SKU that highlights its specific features.
Show the correct variant:
Match the image directly to the product option being sold. Ensure the photo accurately reflects the exact color, pattern, finish, and material.
Show only one variant per image:
For products with multiple colors or styles, display only the single variant being sold in that listing. Avoid group photos that showcase multiple colorways together.
Accurately represent customized products:
If you are selling a customized or personalized item, use an image that shows the customization. Do not use an uncustomized product photo for a customized listing, and vice versa. Learn more about
best practices for customized products
.
Frame your product in the image space
so that it takes up no less than 75%, but not more than 90%, of the full image.
Any additional views of the product showing highlighted areas can be submitted with the
additional image link
[additional_image_link]
attribute.
Note
: If you're planning to upload an image of a product in its original packaging, make sure that the image clearly displays multidimensional product packaging and not just the front of the packaging.
Helpful tips
Enhance your images with Product Studio: You can increase resolution, remove backgrounds, and generate custom scenes using
Product Studio
, available in Merchant Center and the Google & YouTube app on Shopify.
Keep in mind that images may be automatically cropped: Google may test subtle cropping on your image to better frame the item and highlight key product details for shoppers.
