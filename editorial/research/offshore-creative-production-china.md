## The bar

- **Length.** Not measured in this pass. Brief 19 measured the overlapping
  offshore-benchmark domains at roughly 900 to 2,000 words with one table at

- **Depth.** No page on any of the five queries cites a primary Chinese
  instrument. Clearing the field needs dated instruments named with article
  numbers for customs, data export, copyright and payment, plus the honest
  absences stated on the page.
- **Tables.** The brief requires the engagement-model table, a pilot plan and a

  the operational page does not exist.

## R1. What has to be true before anything is looked up

Listed before any search was run. Each item is either sourced in the claims
table or declared unsourceable in "Do not publish".

1. China has a named customs instrument that lets product samples and
   production equipment enter temporarily and leave again, with a stated
   period, a stated extension rule and a stated guarantee requirement.
2. China accepts ATA carnets for the goods a production actually moves:
   commercial samples and professional equipment.
3. Samples of no commercial value have a named duty treatment, by article.
4. Someone publishes a typical customs clearance time for inbound samples.
   (Expected to fail. It did.)
5. The cross-border data instrument has thresholds a single shoot sits far
   below, and an exemption for files that come into China from abroad and go
   back out.
6. The personal information statute says which categories are sensitive, so a
   buyer can tell whether a talent file changes tier.
7. Chinese law requires consent for use of a likeness and separate consent for
   face or voice editing (reuse, briefs 13 and 26).
8. Generated assets carry a named labeling duty with an effective date (reuse,
   briefs 13, 23, 24, 26).
9. Chinese copyright law has a default owner for commissioned work when the
   contract is silent, and it is not automatically the buyer.
10. A foreign-related contract may choose its governing law, and a foreign
    arbitral award is enforceable in China under a named treaty with a date.
11. There is a named rule on the payment paperwork for money leaving China for
    services, with a threshold.
12. Working-hours overlap from China to the buyer's main markets is checkable
    arithmetic (London, New York reused from brief 19; continental Europe, US
    West Coast, Sydney added).
13. The national holiday calendar that stops production is published with
    dates by a named body.
14. The circulating 40 percent savings figure has a traceable instrument, a
    sample and a method. (Expected to fail. It did.)
15. Somebody publishes a benchmark for pilot length or pilot gates in creative
    production. (Expected to fail. It did.)
16. The engagement-model split (dedicated team, project, platform) is described
    somewhere with evidence rather than seller copy. (Structural only. No
    figure needed or available.)
17. hubStudio's own studio locations, file retention and proposal turnaround
    are published on the site, so they can run under the first-party exception.

### Samples and equipment

> Product samples can enter China on temporary terms and leave again. The
> customs measures list samples among thirteen categories of temporary goods.
> They must be re-exported within six months of entry, in their original
> condition apart from normal wear, and customs may extend that period no more
> than three times, by no more than six months each time.
> Source: China's customs measures on temporary entry and exit goods, Order No.
> 233, issued 7 December 2017 and effective 1 February 2018, Articles 3, 6 and
> 10, read in the State Council Gazette text, September 2026.

> Goods entering on temporary terms without an ATA carnet need a guarantee
> lodged with the local customs office. Under a carnet, China's trade promotion
> council stands as the national guarantor.
> Source: China's customs measures on temporary entry and exit goods, Order No.
> 233, Article 9, read in the State Council Gazette text, September 2026.
