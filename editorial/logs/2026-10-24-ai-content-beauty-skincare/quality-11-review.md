# Quality iteration 11: hostile-reader findings and fixes

1. The FAQ H2 is a label rather than a question. Turn it into a buyer question.
2. The market H2 is abstract. Name claims rules and the production decision.
3. “Campaign casting” can imply a real casting process. Call it campaign imagery.
4. A buyer unfamiliar with Amazon may not know A+. Explain it as enhanced product-page content at first mention.
5. The reader cannot immediately see when to use a generation brief and when to supply observation records. State that split before the asset table.
6. The long US classification explanation reaches its action too late. Put the illustrated claim and review decision in a shorter sentence.
7. The reviewer-packet section does not say how to handle a batch with different claims. Require each variation to identify its claim and evidence reference.
8. The body never says whether approval of one market is approval everywhere. Make the existing translation warning explicitly cover reuse in other markets.
9. There is no succinct commissioning boundary for a model image beside an efficacy claim. Keep the complete placement with the reviewer rather than sending an isolated file.
10. The workflow gives no direct way to prevent a producer from confusing a visual approval with a claims approval. Label the type of approval in the review comment.

These are clarity and workflow recommendations grounded in C1-C15. No new legal test, product feature, price or empirical claim is introduced.
