# Quality iteration 2: ten weaknesses

1. “Casting” in the opening can be mistaken for proof that a depicted model used the product. Explain that the model image is campaign imagery.
2. The asset table's before-and-after row does not explicitly say that comparable records still need review of the whole ad.
3. The China exemption paragraph could better distinguish exemption from summary publication from an exemption from support.
4. The synthetic-model opening does not immediately distinguish human-model rights from invented-person disclosure. Limit its scope explicitly.
5. “Covered ad creators” in New York is vague. Describe the commercial creator and actual-knowledge condition in plain language.
6. The California paragraph omits the non-identifiable-person boundary, although it is in the cleared research.
7. The evidence-packet paragraph lacks a clear response when the proposed claim has no support. Tell the producer to remove or revise the claim.
8. Case-study prose risks inviting readers to treat the projects as validation of cosmetic efficacy. Introduce the section as production examples.
9. The workflow section does not explicitly tell buyers to choose the production route around who can do the work and approve it.
10. The final brief request could better expose workload without a rate: list product variants and required placements before asking for a production scope.
