## Brief 01 is blocked, deliberately

The Tmall specs cannot be verified from open sources. The Taobao Rule Center
(淘宝规则中心) blocks external access, and the Chinese spec sites that publish
the numbers conflict on the file size cap (500KB against 3MB) and on the
product fill ratio (60 percent against 70 to 80 percent), with none
distinguishing Tmall Global from domestic or accounting for category
variation. Part 7 of the search spec puts both figures on the do-not-publish
list.

The row stays blocked until the Qianniu (千牛) seller-backend capture session
happens. Filling the slot from secondary sources would convert the one asset a
rival cannot copy into an ordinary blog, which the runbook names as the single
most damaging thing this system can do.

## What the research changed

The gate earned its place three times. It did not just supply citations, it
corrected the briefs.

**Brief 17.** Two angles in the brief had no source at all: that centralization
changes who a producer reports to, and that centralized units price on studio
utilization. Neither could be sourced from the buy side or the supply side.
Both appear in the draft only as questions the buyer asks a partner, never as
statements of fact, and the draft says so on the page. Also cut: an 85 percent
agency-review figure that turns out to describe media agency contracts rather
than production, which search summaries silently convert into a production
claim.

**Brief 23.** The brief treated the export requirement as attaching to the
label generally. It does not. Article 4 attaches it to the explicit label, and
a lot of secondary commentary blurs the two. The research also surfaced
Article 9, which no ranking page uses and which turns out to be the legal shape
of a normal production handover: an unlabeled master is contemplated,
conditional on the user agreement and a six-month log duty. That became the
spine of the handover section. The regulator's own November 2025 enforcement
round supplied the "where the label gets lost" evidence, sourced rather than
inferred.

**Brief 27.** Three corrections. The AI image production page publishes no
ratio, contrary to the brief. The site never scopes 20/80 to color variants.
And the two published ratios count different things, so presenting them as one
metric at two scopes would have produced a false method statement. The 20/80
is therefore absent from the draft.

## Flags for review

1. **Brief 27 runs long.** 2,091 body words against a 1,600 slot ceiling. Two
   trimming rounds removed the rhetoric; what remains is nine primary
   citations. The asset brief recommends revising the brief's word target to
   2,000 rather than deleting evidence no competing page carries. Decide on
   review.
2. **Brief 27 needs a method statement.** The 70 percent generated catalog
   figure is published on the site with no method on file. The draft labels it
   as a published observation rather than a measured average. To upgrade it:
   what counts as one asset, how many engagements, over what period, median or
   mean.
3. **Brief 17, judgment call.** The count of four reorganizations is retained
   rather than the safer variant research offered. A count across an unnamed
   set identifies nobody, but a reader following the trade press could
   enumerate them. The safer variant is recorded in the research file if
   review disagrees.
4. **Brief 27, judgment call.** The New York statute's civil penalty figures
   were cleared but cut from the draft. Not a pricing claim, so the no-rate
   rule does not reach them, but a dollar figure on a hubStudio page invites
   the wrong reading. Reinstate if review disagrees.
5. **The briefs carried British spellings** inherited from the search spec
   (centralised, utilisation). Fixed at source in `briefs-data.mjs` and all 30
   briefs regenerated, because those meta descriptions ship as site copy and
   the house standard is American English.
6. **The spec's image path convention does not match this repo.** The spec
   says `public/images/insights/<slug>.webp`. The repo uses
   `public/Images/insight-<slug>.webp`, which `src/data/insights.ts` and the
   pre-commit optimizer both key on. Repo wins, recorded in
   `editorial/CLAUDE.md`.
7. **`public/content/hubstudio-search-spec.md` is untracked and was left that
   way.** Anything under `public/` is served, so committing it would publish
   the competitive analysis and the 12-week calendar at
   `hubstudio.ai/content/hubstudio-search-spec.md`. Suggested fix: move it to
   `editorial/SEARCH-SPEC.md`, tracked but not served.

## Images

Every prompt was checked for a named person before generation. None names one,
per the permanent rule that the model rejects such prompts and the job fails.

- 17: Shanghai studio, contact sheets laid across a table, one window light.
  The face is turned away and out of focus, which sidesteps the
  poreless-symmetry tell. 76KB.
- 23: Changsha post suite, a drive passed hand to hand, faces cropped out
  entirely. Hands are the classic failure mode and these hold up. 67KB.
- 27: Shanghai product set, one glazed bottle, one softbox, one shadow. The
  glaze highlight is on-theme, since the piece argues that material response is
  exactly where generation breaks. 47KB.

Each was opened and checked against the AI-tells list in
`hubstudio-image-style-guide.md` before saving. No text, no logos, no
watermarks, one light source and one shadow in each.
