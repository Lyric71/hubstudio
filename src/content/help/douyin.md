Douyin (抖音) in hubStudio is where creators prepare a brand's Douyin posts. The app writes each post in Simplified Chinese, in the format you pick, with its English reading beside it, then hands it over to the Douyin creator center, where you publish it yourself.

## Where you find it

Open **Douyin** in the menu, under **Publish**. The page holds every Douyin post of the brand, from first draft to published.

## Why you publish it yourself

Douyin offers no way for an app like this one to publish for an ordinary brand account. So hubStudio doesn't connect to a Douyin account. It writes and checks the post, then the last step opens Douyin's own page with everything ready to paste. The post leaves from the brand's own account, which Douyin asks to be registered with a Chinese phone number.

## Pick the format

A Douyin post starts at the brief, and the first choice is the format: what you're publishing, the way Douyin names it. Each format is a card saying what it is, whether it's posted from a computer or only from the phone app, and what its composer takes.

| Format | What it is | Posted from |
|---|---|---|
| **Video** (视频) | The upright short video, with a title, a description and two covers. | Computer or phone |
| **Picture post** (图文) | Pictures people swipe through with music. | Computer or phone |
| **Long article** (文章) | A long read of up to 8,000 characters. | Computer or phone |

Under the cards, the picked format asks its own questions, and only those. **What it takes, and what the draft writes** lists the limits of every field.

The questions depend on the format:

- **Video**: **Big words on the cover**, a **Location to tag**, and whether the video is made with AI.
- **Picture post**: **Suggest the music**, the mood to search for in Douyin's music library; a location; and whether the pictures are made with AI.
- **Long article**: the **Length**.
- Every format: **What the reader should do**.

## Write the brief

1. Pick the format and answer its questions.
2. Write the brief in English or in Chinese: the angle, who it speaks to, what the post has to achieve. Attach material if you have some, as on any network.
3. Press **Draft with AI**, or **Write it myself** to write it by hand.

The draft comes back with every field the format's composer asks for, in Chinese, and lands in the copy step. Depending on the answers, it also gets the **Cover line (封面文字)**, the **Music to look for**, or the **Summary (摘要)** of a long article.

Every draft is checked against the format's limits when it comes back, and one that breaks a rule gets a single repair pass from the AI. A field still too long is cut to fit at the end of a sentence.

## Written in Chinese, read in English

Every post here is written in Simplified Chinese. There's no language to pick: write the brief in English or in Chinese, and the post comes out in Simplified Chinese either way.

You don't need to read Chinese to run it. Every field the AI writes comes with its English reading:

- **What it says, in English**, under the editor, holds the English of the title and the copy. When you change the Chinese, the box says the reading is of an earlier wording. Press **Read it again in English** to translate what's in the editor now.
- Each of the format's other fields shows its English in italics under its box.
- **Write it in English, publish it in Chinese**, in the copy step, takes a post you wrote in English and localizes it into Chinese under Douyin's rules. The Chinese becomes the post's next version, with its English reading.

If you read Chinese, tick **I read Chinese** once. The English readings then stay folded for you in this browser.

Under the reading, the app flags what Douyin would refuse or penalize, such as a link, a phone number or a WeChat ID in the copy, and words the Advertising Law (广告法) forbids in a brand's claims, such as 第一, 最佳 or 顶级.

## Pictures and video

After the copy, the post's pictures or video come in the next step, in the shape the format takes: render them with AI at the price shown, pick them from the Assets Library or upload your own. A render makes up to 8 pictures; add more from the library or your computer. A picture post reads best at 3:4 (1080 x 1440). A video takes two covers in Douyin: an upright 3:4 and one across at 4:3.

The [image editor](/help/assets-library#the-image-editor) and the [video editor](/help/assets-library#the-video-editor) both have a Douyin panel with its shapes: the picture post at 3:4, a full-screen picture with the zones Douyin covers, both video covers, and the video upright or across.

## Publish on Douyin

The publishing step has one way out: **Publish interactively**. Press it and:

1. The pictures or the video download to your computer.
2. The Douyin creator center opens in a new tab.
3. The title goes on your clipboard.

The steps under the button follow the format's own composer, in the order it asks for each field. Every field waits behind its own Copy button: **Copy the title**, **Copy the text**, and one for each of the format's other fields.

Once the post is live, paste its address under **Where it stands**, lower in the publishing step, and set the post to published. The app is never told about it otherwise.

### Limits

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Video | 3 seconds to 15 minutes, 4 GB, MP4, WEBM or MOV, 9:16 recommended, 720p at least |
| Video title | 30 characters |
| Description | 1,000 characters, topics and @ included; 5 topics at most |
| Picture post | Up to 30 pictures, JPG or PNG, 20 MB each; title 20 characters |
| Long article | 300 to 8,000 characters |

Douyin restricts posts that send people elsewhere: no link, phone number, WeChat ID or QR code. The draft never writes one, and the checks flag one you type. Since September 2025, content made with AI must be declared: tick Douyin's AI declaration when you post it.

## What it costs

Publishing on Douyin costs nothing: the post is yours to send. Drafting with AI, **Translate into Chinese**, **Read it again in English** and rendering pictures or a video are charged to your balance, and each price shows before you press. **Write it myself** is free. See [balance and payments](/help/balance-and-payments).

## Who can do this

Creators and admins write the posts. Viewers can read them, with their English readings. Client logins see a post only when it was made for their client. See [your team](/help/your-team).

## Troubleshooting

**The composer opened on the wrong account.** Douyin opens on the account your browser is signed in to. Sign out there, sign in with the brand's account, and press **Publish interactively** again.

**The English reading says it's of an earlier wording.** The Chinese changed since it was read. Press **Read it again in English** before you post.

**The video is longer than Douyin accepts.** The app keeps 15 minutes, the limit every account has. Some accounts may post longer videos; cut it in the video editor if Douyin refuses it.
