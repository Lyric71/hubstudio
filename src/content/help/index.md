hubStudio makes images and videos with the AI engines of several makers, turns them into posts for LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and X, and delivers them to the clients your team works for, for you alone or for your whole team, paid from one prepaid balance. These guides follow the menu, from your first render to your team, your clients and your invoices.

## Start here

1. [Getting started](/help/getting-started): what hubStudio is, creating your account (country, Just me, Create a team or Join a team, how you heard about us), the menu, the bar at the top of the page, asking Support, and your first render.
2. [Discovery and tips](/help/discovery-and-tips): the Discovery page your first sign-in opens on, its modules and first steps, the quick tour of the screen, and the tips that come after you sign in.
3. [Scratchpad](/help/scratchpad): your own notes on every page, in sheets with bold, lists, checklists and links, dictation, files and recordings, inserting a note into a post, keeping a file in the Assets Library, and what it costs.

## Make images and videos

4. [Explore the engines](/help/explore): the gallery, what each type of engine does, filters, sorting, the New tag and the prices on the cards.
5. [Create an image](/help/create-an-image): text to image, editing and upscaling, choosing an engine, the options, the price line, Improve with AI, several images per run, and Made for.
6. [Create a video](/help/create-a-video): text to video, image to video, references, duration, resolution or mode, sound, the price per second, rendering time, and Made for.
7. [History](/help/history): finding, opening, downloading, editing, reusing a prompt, saying which client a piece was made for, deleting, and what storing files costs.
8. [Assets Library and the tools](/help/assets-library): every file of the team in folders, finding, tagging and moving them, the Image editor (framing for X, Instagram or LinkedIn, crop, adjust, looks, text, arrows and shapes, a logo, saving a copy or a new version), the Video editor (framing for Instagram, TikTok or Facebook with what each network covers and its checks, trims, speed, texts, captions word by word, music, a cover) and the Image anonymizer.
9. [Shorts autopilot](/help/shorts-autopilot): a long video cut into vertical shorts by itself, for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels: the choices, the run, the shorts it saves and what it costs.
10. [Campaigns](/help/campaigns): the files of one campaign gathered under a name and a brief, added from the Assets Library one by one or several at once, and a post written from the whole campaign.
11. [Skills](/help/skills): the instructions that shape Improve with AI and the drafts of your posts, the Catalog, your own skills and team skills.
12. [Choosing a model](/help/choosing-a-model): the Quick, Balanced and Best choices of every writing step, what a run costs, All models, and the Model benchmarks page that compares the text models on public benchmarks.

## Publish, get approval and keep watch

13. [LinkedIn](/help/linkedin): briefing and drafting a post, a picture or a carousel, then scheduling or publishing it as a feed post or an article.
14. [Instagram](/help/instagram): the picture, the carousel or the Reel first, then its caption, then a feed post, a Story or a Reel.
15. [Facebook](/help/facebook): a page post with pictures or a video, and its first comment.
16. [TikTok](/help/tiktok): the video first, then its caption, the in-depth Brief, and TikTok's own posting choices.
17. [YouTube](/help/youtube): the video first, then its title and description, the upload in YouTube Studio, and the channel kit: name, handle, description, links, banner and profile picture.
18. [X](/help/x): a post or a thread with up to four pictures, then updating or deleting it on X.
19. [Validation](/help/validation): sending an image, a video, a post or a file for approval, deciding, new versions, comments, and your clients as validators.
20. [Agents](/help/agents): the Social publishing watcher, agents you create from scratch over your posts, campaigns and library, the agent form and its Scope tab, schedules, Run now, findings, the results email, and what a run costs.

## Your team and your clients

21. [Your team](/help/your-team): working alone or in a team, the Admin, Creator, Viewer and Client roles, invitations, requests to join, daily limits, pausing a login, clients and their logins, invoice details, the interface languages and renaming the team.
22. [Client space](/help/client-space): for the people of your clients: what they see, downloading, and approving the team's work.

## Your money and your account

23. [Balance and payments](/help/balance-and-payments): a prepaid balance in US dollars, the team balance then your own, topping up by card, Alipay or WeChat Pay, promotional codes, automatic top-up, invoices and the usage log.
24. [Account and sign-in](/help/account-and-sign-in): the interface in English, French or Chinese, your name, picture, email and password, the sign-in code by email, your social accounts on My Connections, the connection check, light or dark, date and time, My models, voice input, password reset and a login left unused.
25. [Partners](/help/partners): the partner area, for commercial partners only.

## When something goes wrong

26. [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting): the messages hubStudio shows, what they mean and what to do, and where to ask when you are stuck.
