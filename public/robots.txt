# hubStudio robots.txt

# Split by bot PURPOSE, not by company. The distinction that matters:
#
#   Training crawlers      collect text to train a model. Blocking them costs
#                          nothing in AI answers, because they do not answer
#                          anything. Blocked below.
#   Retrieval crawlers     fetch a page at answer time so an assistant can
#                          read and cite it. Blocking them deletes this site
#                          from AI answers entirely. Allowed below, explicitly.
#
# Being fetchable by the retrieval crawlers is the single highest-scored
# citation factor in the field, so the allowances below are deliberate
# declarations, not an accident of a permissive default.
#
# Reviewed 2026-09-09. Verify with: node scripts/verify-crawler-access.mjs
